  • Recommended Motion:

    The Administration and Finance Committee recommends as follows:


    1.    That the Regional Municipality of Waterloo approve the revised Municipal Election Compliance Audit Committee Terms of Reference, as set out in Appendix A attached to report PDL-LEG-26-011, dated June 2, 2026.


    2.    That the Regional Municipality of Waterloo take the following action with respect to the 2026 first quarter update as set out in report COR-CFN-26-009 dated June 2, 2026:


    a)    Receive the 2026 First Quarter Update that shows implementation and financial progress towards delivery on the Region’s Annual Business Plan, in support of achieving the vision set out by Council in the Growing with Care 2023-2027 Strategic Plan; and 


    b)    Amend the 2026 Capital Budget as set out in Appendix B.


    3.    Whereas Under the ESA, 2000, an employee is currently entitled to unpaid Reservist Leave for Canadian Forces training or deployment, provided they have at least two (2) months of continuous employment and
    Whereas if the employee is taking Reservist Leave because they are deployed to a Canadian Forces operation inside Canada related to handling an emergency or its aftermath, there is no minimum employment requirement and 
    Whereas recent amendments via the Working for Workers Act, 2023, have expanded this leave to also include recovery from deployment-related injuries or illnesses and
    Whereas the current ESA leave type creates a "break in service" in OMERS, which can potentially reduce an employee’s credited service and future retirement benefits and
    Whereas, currently, the Region has not elected to share the cost of contributions, meaning that the employee is responsible for both their portion and the employer's portion, known as "double contributions" and 
    Whereas OMERS regulations permit the purchase of service for certain leaves of absence, including those mandated by statute, to restore full credited service. For Reservist Leave, the employee may elect to buy back the period by contributing their share of premiums, with the employer matching its portion. This process requires explicit employer approval and, for municipal entities, formal authorization via a bylaw to ensure compliance with the Municipal Act, 2001, and to allocate public funds appropriately and 
    Whereas the proposed bylaw will honour the employee's service to both the municipality and the nation, while upholding OMERS eligibility rules and 
    Whereas the there are minimal financial implications associated with approving the OMERS Reservist Leave By-law and where an employee elects to take a Reservist Leave for Canadian Forces training or deployment, the Region as the employer will make an equal OMERS contribution in accordance with subsection 12(3) of the Primary Plan, funded from within the existing divisional operating budget, 
    Now therefore be it resolved that the Region of Waterloo adopt the OMERS Region of Waterloo Reservist Bylaw to authorize the Region to match OMERS contributions for employees purchasing OMERS broken service resulting from an approved Reservist Leave, in accordance with the Employment Standards Act (ESA), 2000.


    4.    That the Regional Municipality of Waterloo approve a By-law to delegate authority to the outgoing council to pass by-law(s) to authorize the potential adoption of the prudent investor standard, as set out in report COR-TRY-26-016 dated June 2, 2026.


    5.    That the Regional Municipality of Waterloo take the following action with respect to the 2027 Plan and Budget as set out in report COR-CFN-008 dated June 2, 2026:


    a)    That staff be provided direction to present 2027 Plan and Budget timetable schedule to the incoming Regional Chair as set out in Appendix A.
