4.1
2023 Budget: Supporting the Plan

 
1.2023 Budget - Supporting the Plan.pdf

Craig Dyer, Commissioner, Corporate Services and Chief Financial Officer, provided the Committee with a presentation regarding the 2023 Budget: Supporting the Plan.

 A copy of the presentation is attached to the original minutes.

The presentation provided an overview of the Region’s current 2023 operating and capital budget, including the Waterloo Regional Police Services budget, user rates, fees and charges, and the preliminary total weighted property tax increase at a proposed 8.6%. Additionally, the presentation focused on providing an overview of service levels and budgets for Water and Wastewater, Public Transit, Housing and Homelessness, and Paramedic Services.

C. Dyer provided the purpose of the 2023 Plan and Budget, which was to establish service levels objectives and outcomes; determine the annual business plan and the financial resources for service delivery; property tax and the user rate revenue requirements; and to set the user rates, fees and charges.

The Committee asked that staff bring back for information a full description of the Region of Waterloo’s reserve fund policy and historical use and purpose of the tax rate stabilization fund.

Responding to questions from the Committee, staff will bring back information on the KPMG Review and if service levels are adjusted, determine what programs would be impacted.

Jennifer Rose, Commissioner, Engineering and Environmental Services gave an overview of the Water and Wastewater services, which addressed objectives to become resilient to climate change, protection of water resources and promotion and protection of land, while meeting the strategic plan’s focus area of Environment and Climate Action. J. Rose highlighted the importance of providing safe drinking water, and noted the capital investments and asset management plans for 2023-2032. C. Dyer gave an overview of the proposed Water and Wastewater user rate changes.

Mathieu Goetzke, Commissioner, Transportation Services provided an overview of the Transit Services, which addressed objectives of enhancing the transit system that increases ridership, accessibility and public appeal, also improving road and rail transportation to the Waterloo Region and road safety, while meeting the strategic plan’s focus area of Sustainable Transportation. Neil Malcolm Director, Transit Services highlighted the proposed 2023 Transit Fares. M. Goetzke also noted proposed changes for Transit Service Hours.

The Committee asked in regards to transit service route to the Canadian Hindu Temple and asked staff to come back to the budget process.

The Committee asked that free fare be provided to students to attend museums across the regions for the months of September, October, November and February, and come back to the budget process.

The Committee recessed at 3:42 p.m. and resumed at 3:53 p.m.

Peter Sweeney, Commissioner, Community Services  gave a summary of the Housing and Homelessness objectives for making affordable housing more available to individuals and families, promote and enhance equity in policies, planning and service and enhance community safety and wellbeing. Cheryl Braan, Director, Corporate Finance provided an overview of the Housing and Homelessness 2023 operating budget impacts, the 2023-2032 Housing Capital Program, and the Waterloo Region Housing Master Plan projects for 2023-2032. Ellen McGaghey, Director, Fleet and Management provided an overview of the Housing and Homelessness portfolio management.

In response to questions from the Committee, C. Dyer replied that staff will come back to the Committee’s requests for building affordable housing as part of the material for the Capital Review Program.

Kevin Petendra, Deputy Chief, Paramedic Services gave an overview of Paramedic Services Master Plan. He highlighted the current challenges with respect to high call volumes, need for additional paramedics, replacement of emergency vehicles and paramedic stations, and the rise of fuel costs. C. Braan noted the net property tax increase for Paramedic Services is $4.3 million and the ten year Capital Plan for 2023-2032. 

The Committee asked questions regarding a pedestrian access point to accelerate this project within the current budget, staff to report back on financial implications of projects such as roads and pedestrian walkways, to the budget process in February.
