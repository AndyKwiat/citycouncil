TSD-TRS-26-007, Housekeeping Updates to GRT Affordable Transit Program Fare Price

 
1.TSD-TRS-26-007, Housekeeping Updates to GRT Affordable Transit Program Fare Price .pdf
  • Recommended Motion:

    That the Regional Municipality of Waterloo:

    1. Waive the notice requirements under the Region of Waterloo’s Notice Policy in order to allow for an amendment to the Fees and Charges By-Law without giving twenty-one (21) days notice to the public; and
    2. Amend the 2026 Fees and Charges By-law to reflect housekeeping updates to Grand River Transit’s Affordable Transit Program (ATP) fare product prices as outlined in report TSD-TRS-26-007, dated June 2, 2026.

    The 2026 fare prices for the following ATP fare products will be:

    • ATP - Stored Value: $1.35
    • ATP - Monthly Pass: $47.00
    • ATP - MobilityPLUS tickets (strip of 5): $6.75
