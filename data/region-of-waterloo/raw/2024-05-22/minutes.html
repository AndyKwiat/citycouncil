8.
Delegations

 

8.1
Kelly Smith, FRIENDS of Waterloo Region Museums

 

K. Smith appeared before Council on behalf of the FRIENDS of Waterloo Region Museums. She provided an overview of the fundraising work that the organization has undertaken, noting that they have donated almost $500,000 since 1998 to enhance programing at Regional museums. She noted that in 2024 they expanded their mandate to include fundraising support for Schneider Haus and McDougall Cottage, which unites all four Regional sites into their activities. She announced that they are providing 3 donations totaling over $100,000 over the next 3 years.

8.2
Wren Wombwell, Organizer, Fightback KW with Andrew Reeves, Jaime Stief and Acer Bonaparte

 

A. Reeves spoke in support of the petition from Fightback KW. He noted that in the last 12 years there has been a significant decline in renting opportunities and that, given the magnitude of the housing crisis, the demands in the petition are reasonable. A. Reeves outlined the demands in the petition including freezing the Waterloo Region Police Services budget for 5 years, halting construction at 100 Victoria Street until there are housing solutions for all current and future residents, and a meaningful review and overhaul of the shelter system. 

8.3
Krista Straus, Wilmot

 

K. Straus appeared in opposition to the land assembly in Wilmot Township, noting that it is causing uncertainty in the farming community, which would not be done to any other industry. She stressed that agriculture is a vital industry in Wilmot Township and many other businesses depend on this sector. K. Straus highlighted the lack of public consultation on the project and stated that the residents support farmers and want the Region to look for sites closer to the Cities. 

D. McCabe joined the meeting at 7:24 PM. ()
J. Liggett joined the meeting at 7:24 PM. ()
C. Huinink joined the meeting at 7:24 PM. ()

8.4
Stewart Snyder, Wilmot

 

S. Snyder appeared before Council in opposition to the land assembly in Wilmot Township, noting that land owners and residents have assembled in opposition to the land assembly. He expressed concerns with the lack of communication from the Region, the unknown final use of the land, and how water quality and transportation will be affected.

In response to a question from Council, S. Snyder stated that Vive Development knew enough about the project to assume that they were aware of the Region’s plans in advance.

Notice of Motion

 

17.1
Councillor C. James Notice of Motion

 

C. James introduced the motion noting that the current name of the Planning and Works Committee is not reflective of the work that comes before the Committee and the revised name will better help the community understand what the committee does.

In response to a question from Council, William Short, Regional Clerk, stated that in order to put the name change into effect, an amendment to the Procedure By-law is necessary, so the earliest the change can be made is for the August Committee meeting.

  • Moved byC. James
    Seconded byC. Huinink

    WHEREAS the title of Planning and Works no longer represents the full scale of the committee's responsibilities and can be confusing to residents;
    AND WHEREAS the Region’s strategic priority “Climate Aligned Growth”, our climate goals, our commitment to our natural heritage system, including rivers and streams, water and ground water resources, transit, waste and other environmental issues could be better represented by "Sustainability”
    AND WHEREAS the title should be modernized from the non-descriptive term "Works" to the more descriptive term "Infrastructure " including roads, transportation, waste, water and wastewater facilities;
    AND WHEREAS the Region’s strategic priority “Housing for All” directs our commitment to land readiness and the more accurate term “development” to support both housing and employment solutions for the Region’s growing population;
    THEREFORE be it resolved that the title of the standing committee Planning and Works be updated to the Sustainability, Infrastructure and Development Standing Committee.

    In Favor (15)J. Erb, J. Nowak, K. Redman, M. Harris, S. Shantz, S. Foxton, D. McCabe, J. Liggett, D. Craig, P. Wolf, C. James, R. Deutschmann, N. Salonen, C. Huinink, and K. Williams
    Carried (15 to 0)
