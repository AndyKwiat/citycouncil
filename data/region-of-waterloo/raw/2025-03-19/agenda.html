Community and Health Services Committee Summary - March 4, 2025

 
1.CHS Summary - March 4, 2025.pdf
  • Recommended Motion:

    The Community and Health Services Committee recommends as follows:

    1. That the Regional Municipality of Waterloo approve the revised Building Better Futures Framework set out in Appendix A, as the Region of Waterloo’s direction for the continued creation and protection of affordable and non-market housing over the next five years (2025 to 2030), contingent upon federal and provincial funding, as outlined in report CSD-HOU-25-003, dated March 4, 2025.

    2. That the Regional Municipality of Waterloo take the following actions with regard to the Canada Mortgage and Housing Corporation Affordable Housing Funding (CMHC AHF) for 589 Langs Drive, Cambridge as outlined in report CSD-HOU-25-002 dated March 4, 2025:
      1. Authorize the Regional Municipality of Waterloo to enter into a loan agreement with Canada Mortgage and Housing Corporation for the construction of the Waterloo Region Housing (WRH) affordable housing building 589 Langs Dr., Cambridge;
      2. Authorize the Commissioner of Corporate Services/Chief Financial Officer to execute the loan agreement with CMHC and all ancillary documentation in a form satisfactory to both the Chief Financial Officer and the Regional Solicitor; and
      3. Authorize the Director, Housing Services, to execute and deliver all other administrative documentation as required by CMHC after substantial completion to fulfill requirements.

10.2.4
Closed Committee Summary

 
1.Closed Committee Summary - March 4, 2025.pdf
  • Recommended Motion:

    That the following recommendation from the Closed Committee be approved:

    1. That the Regional Municipality of Waterloo approve the following:

    I.  That The Regional Municipality of Waterloo By-law No. 22-018, as amended, being a by-law to authorize and govern the delegation of Signing Authority on behalf of The Regional Municipality of Waterloo, be further amended as follows:

    Section 2 of Schedule “A” be removed, and renumber the sections that follow accordingly.

    II.  That The Regional Municipality of Waterloo By-law 20-027, as amended, being a by-law to Provide for the Appointment of a Chief Administrative Officer, be further amended as follows: 

    Add as a new Section 6(a) the following provision:
    6(a) Authority to recommend to Council any changes to the departmental structure of the Corporation for approval.

    Add as a new Section 6(b) the following provision:

    6(b)Authority to execute employment agreement for any department head, except where the department head also holds the position of clerk or treasurer of the municipality, and related authority to dismiss, discipline, and any other like action provided that:

    i.    Regional Council has been informed; and as applicable.
    ii.   that the Regional Chair and Standing Committee Chairs have participated in the hiring process for any department head.

    And that the subsections 6(b) and (c) be renumbered accordingly.

    III.  That The Regional Municipality of Waterloo Human Resources policies be amended to reflect the foregoing amendments.

    2. That the Regional Municipality of Waterloo take the following action to support a proposed affordable housing development that was submitted in response to Request for Proposal P2024-28, as described in Report PDL-LEG-25-009 dated March 4, 2025:

    1. Authorize the Commissioner of Planning, Development and Legislative Services to issue a conditional letter of commitment for a maximum of six months to enter into long term lease with the Central Ontario Co-Operative Housing Federation and/or an affiliated non-profit organization and any other documentation required by Canada Housing and Mortgage Corporation to facilitate funding applications, all being satisfactory to the Regional Solicitor.
