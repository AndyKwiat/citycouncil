2	8. Public Meetings to Consider Zoning Amendments
2	8.1 Mayor Watters indicated the purpose of this public meeting is to present
2	and receive public input regarding a proposed zoning by-law
2	amendment on the subject land known as 8196 Wellington Road 19 in
2	the former Township of West Garafraxa.
2	He noted the purpose of the proposed zoning by-law amendment is to
2	change the zoning from “A” Agricultural and “EP” Environmental
2	Protection to “A” Agricultural Exception and “EP” Environmental
2	Protection on the retained parcel to restrict residential uses.
2	Furthermore, the amendment will seek to permit a maximum floor area
2	of 6.6% for accessory buildings on the severed parcel, and to recognize
2	the height of the existing accessory building (barn) measuring 10.5
3	metres.
3	The effect of the proposed zoning by-law amendment is to implement
3	zoning regulations necessary to facilitate a farm dwelling surplus
3	severance and to recognize existing buildings on the severed parcel.
3	The zoning amendment is a condition of approval of the County of
3	Wellington Land Division Committee Application B86-23
3	Mayor Watters stated if a person or public body does not make oral
3	submissions at the public meeting, or make written submissions to the
3	Township of Centre Wellington before the by-law is passed, the person
3	or public body is not entitled to appeal the decision of the Township of
3	Centre Wellington to the Ontario Land Tribunal (OLT).
3	He indicated if a person or public body does not make oral submission
3	at the public meeting, or make written submissions to the Township of
3	Centre Wellington before the by-law is passed, the person or public
3	body may not be added as the party to the hearing of an appeal before
3	the OLT unless, in the opinion of the Tribunal, there are reasonable
3	grounds to do so.
3	Mayor Watters advised if anyone wishes to be notified of the adoption of
3	the proposed zoning by-law amendment, a written request must be sent
3	to the Municipal Clerk, Township of Centre Wellington, 1 MacDonald
3	Square, Elora, ON, N0B 1S0.
3	The Municipal Clerk advised notice was mailed to prescribed persons,
3	agencies and public bodies on or before February 22, 2024. A copy of
3	the notice was also advertised in the Wellington Advertiser on February
3	22, 2024. She confirmed the requirement for giving notice was
3	completed on February 22, 2024.
3	Mayor Watters called on Jeff Buisman from Van Harten Surveying to
3	introduce the application. J. Buisman provided an overview of the farm
3	surplus dwelling proposal.
3	Mayor Watters indicated there are no registered delegations and asked
3	if there was anyone present wishing to speak to the application.
3	There being no delegations, Mayor Watters offered Council an
3	opportunity to ask any questions of the applicant or staff.
3	In response to a question from Councillor MacDonald, J. Buisman
3	indicated the existing accessory farm structures are empty and primarily
3	used for storage.
4	Mayor Watters thanked everyone for attending and advised Council will
4	be considering the by-law at a future Council meeting.

7	12. Consideration of Reports
7	12.1 Decision Report - South Fergus Secondary Plan (OPA 14) and Master
7	Environmental Servicing Plan (MESP)
7	Report from B. Salmon, Managing Director of Planning and
7	Development
7	B. Salmon, Managing Director of Planning and Development introduced
7	Dave Aston and Emily Elliott from MHBC Planning Consultants on
7	behalf of the land owners.
7	Dave Aston, Planner, MHBC Planning Consultants outlined the purpose
7	of the Secondary Plan and the Master Environmental Servicing Plan
7	(MESP) and provided an overview of the public consultation process
7	and the feedback received, the draft Secondary Plan, including
7	proposed land uses and amendments, the recommendations and
7	implementation from the MESP and the next steps in the process.
7	D. Aston responded to questions regarding changes made from the
7	comments/feedback received, the suggested locations of the community
7	and neighbourhood parks, the use of design guidelines, designs
7	encouraging active transportation, and design steps taken to interface
7	the urban area with the agricultural area.
7	Members of Council expressed the importance of community
7	connectivity, a safe highway 6 crossing, the importance of a welcoming
7	and eye-pleasing gateway to the community, and the importance of park
7	locations in close proximity to high density housing.
7	Mayor Watters called for a 5 minute recess, the meeting resumed at
7	7:37 pm.
7	B. Salmon provided an overview of the report, indicating the effect of the
7	amendment is to implement detailed land use plans and policies to
7	guide orderly development of the remaining designated greenfield land
7	in South Fergus. He noted the South Fergus Secondary Plan provides a
7	conceptual framework for the area and addresses the mix, arrangement
7	and density of land uses, collector road network, size and location of
7	parks and school sites, the location of major services, and the natural
7	heritage network.
8	B. Salmon and D. Aston continued to respond to questions regarding
8	the incorporation of the public comments received in the Secondary
8	Plan, regulations regarding park land dedication, and timing of the
8	community planning permitting system.
8	Moved by Councillor Jennifer Adams, Seconded by Councillor Lisa
8	MacDonald.
8	THAT Council defer consideration of the South Fergus Secondary Plan
8	(OPA 14) and Master Environmental Servicing Plan (MESP) to a
8	Committee of the Whole meeting to be scheduled in April.
8	CARRIED
