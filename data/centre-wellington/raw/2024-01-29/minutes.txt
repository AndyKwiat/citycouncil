6	11.3 Declaration of Surplus Lands
6	Report from P. Newson, Managing Director of Community Services
6	P. Newson, Managing Director of Community Services provided an
6	overview of the report, indicating the Wellington Catholic District School
6	Board (WCDSB) is wishing to expand St. Joseph Catholic School and
6	perhaps include a future high school. The Township can assist the
6	WCDSB by selling Strathallan Park to the Board for the purpose of
6	expanding the school.
6	Members of Council expressed concerns with the loss of green space in
6	the area, the impact on property values, and traffic implications.
6	In response to a question, the Municipal Solicitor noted the sale is
6	subject to Ministry approval and a public planning process where more
6	detail will be provided on the development of the site.
6	Councillor Wilton requested a recorded vote.
6	Moved by Councillor Lisa MacDonald, Seconded by Councillor Jennifer
6	Adams.
6	THAT the Council of the Township of Centre Wellington declare surplus
6	to the Township’s requirements Strathallan Park and two pedestrian
6	access walkways located at 220 Strathallan Street, Fergus, and more
6	particularly described as PINs 71497-0047 (LT), 71497-0050 (LT), and
6	71497-0058 (LT), for the purposes of a fee simple conveyance to the
6	Wellington Catholic District School Board, subject to the reservation of
6	any required utility easements, as determined by the Managing Director
6	of Infrastructure Services;
6	AND THAT upon finalization of an agreement with the Wellington
6	Catholic District School Board, the Chief Administrative Officer is
6	authorized to execute on behalf of the Township such agreement(s) or
6	other documents necessary to complete the transfer, on such terms as
6	may be acceptable to the CAO and Township Legal Counsel.

9	Proposed Motion from Councillor MacDonald re Legislative
9	Amendments to Improve Municipal Codes of Conduct and Enforcement
9	for Centre Wellington Township
9	Councillor MacDonald read the proposed motion and provided a brief
9	rationale.
9	Moved by Councillor Lisa MacDonald, Seconded by Councillor
9	Bronwynne Wilton.
9	WHEREAS, all Ontarians deserve and expect a safe and respectful
9	workplace;
9	AND WHEREAS, municipal governments, as the democratic institutions
9	most directly engaged with Ontarians need respectful discourse;
9	AND WHEREAS, several incidents in recent years of disrespectful
9	behaviour and workplace harassment have occurred amongst members
9	of municipal councils;
10	AND WHEREAS, these incidents seriously and negatively affect the
10	people involved and lower public perceptions of local governments;
10	AND WHEREAS, Municipal Codes of Conduct are helpful tools to set
10	expectations of council member behaviour;
10	AND WHEREAS, municipal governments do not have the necessary
10	tools to adequately enforce compliance with municipal Codes of
10	Conduct;
10	AND WHEREAS 201 municipalities support the call for legislation to
10	ensure appropriate accountability measures for sitting councillors,
10	including OBCM, AMO and ROMA;
10	AND WHEREAS a fundamental, underlying principle of broadening
10	diversity, equity and inclusion in politics rests on the assumption that the
10	workplace is safe;
10	AND WHEREAS we believe that municipal elected officials should be
10	held to account in this way, we also believe that federal and provincial
10	elected officials should take similar actions to hold themselves to
10	account;
10	THEREFORE BE IT RESOLVED THAT:
10	The Council of the Township of Centre Wellington supports the call of
10	the Association of Municipalities of Ontario for the Government of
10	Ontario to introduce legislation to strengthen Municipal Codes of
10	Conduct and compliance with them in consultation with municipal
10	governments;
10	AND THAT legislation encompass the Association of Municipalities of
10	Ontario’s recommendations for:
10	a. Updating Municipal Codes of Conduct to account for workplace safety
10	and harassment,
10	b. Creating a flexible administrative penalty regime, adapted to the local
10	economic,
10	c. Increasing training of municipal Integrity Commissioners to enhance
10	consistency of investigations and recommendations across the province,
10	d. Allowing municipalities to apply to a member of the judiciary to
10	remove a sitting member if recommended through the report of a
10	Municipal Integrity Commissioner,
10	e. Prohibit a member so removed from sitting for election in the term of
11	removal and the subsequent term of office.
11	AND THAT this motion be circulated to the Right Honourable Justin
11	Trudeau, Prime Minister of Canada, the Honourable Doug Ford, Premier
11	of Ontario, the Honourable David Lametti the Minister of Justice, the
11	Honourable Paul Calandra, Minister of Municipal Affairs and Housing,
11	Kate Manson-Smith, Deputy Minister of Municipal Affairs and Housing,
11	Scott Pierce, Federation of Canadian Municipalities Acting President,
11	and Colin Best, President of the Association of Municipalities of Ontario.
11	CARRIED
