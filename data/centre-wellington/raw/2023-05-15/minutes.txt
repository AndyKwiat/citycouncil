3	Garafraxa
3	Report from R. Bossence, Chief Building Official
3	B. Salmon, Managing Director of Planning & Development provided an
4	overview of the demolition control by-law and process.
4	R. Bossence provided an overview of the report, indicating the existing
4	house no longer meets the needs of the owners and it is their intention
4	to remove it and rebuild a new home.
4	In response to a question regarding the reason why the house is not

4	Kimberley Jefferson.

4	issuance of a demolition permit to demolish the single detached dwelling
4	located at 6834 Sixth Line, former Township of West Garafraxa.
