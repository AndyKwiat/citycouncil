3	8.2 County Council Report - Councillor Steve O'Neill - County Ward 4
3	provided an update on the changes to recycling currently underway,
3	including a minimum user fee for waste facilities and a transition to
3	producer responsibility for the blue box program. He indicated beginning
3	January 1, 2025, a $10 minimum user fee will apply to all waste
3	materials brought to Wellington County waste facilities and further
3	explained, this fee is intended to reduce landfill use and encourage
3	residents to use curbside collection.
3	Councillor O’Neill highlighted the new automated speed enforcement
3	camera expected to be deployed in key areas starting in early 2025. He
3	indicated signage will be installed 90 days prior to the activation of the
3	cameras to ensure residents, commuters and visitors have sufficient
3	notice before the program begins.
3	Councillor O’Neill responded to questions regarding leaf drop off at the
3	transfer stations and continuation of the yellow bag garbage system.

7	11.4 Outdoor Water Use By-law Information Report
7	Report from D. Masiero, Manager of Water Services and Environmental
7	Sustainability
7	Dino Masiero, Manager of Water Services and Environmental
7	Sustainability provided an overview of the report, indicating the new by-
7	law includes a tiered system based on drought severity levels with more
7	stringent restrictions during drought conditions. He explained in order to
7	establish the tiers, it is required to recognize the existing provincial
7	Ontario Low Water Response Program.

11	13. Notice of Motions
11	13.1 Deputy Mayor Lustgarten-Evoy indicated a notice of reconsideration and
11	subsequent motion has been brought forward by Councillor Wilton,
11	however, before the proposed motion can be considered, Council must
11	first agree with 2/3rds majority to waive the procedural requirement for
11	giving notice, and agree to reconsider the previous decision of Council
11	adopted at the October 15, 2024 meeting to endorse a new outdoor
11	refrigerated multi-use surface with mechanical and storage buildings for
11	Bissell Park.
11	Deputy Mayor Lustgarten-Evoy stated if agreement is obtained to waive
11	the notice provision of Procedure By-law 2021-31 for reconsideration,
11	and agreement is achieved to reconsider a previous decision, Council
11	must either amend the previous decision or rescind it and consider new
11	action.
11	The purpose of reconsidering a vote is to permit the correction of hasty,
11	ill-advised or erroneous action, or to take into account added information
11	or a changed situation that has developed since the taking of the vote.
11	Councillor Adams raised a Point of Order.
11	Deputy Mayor Lustgarten-Evoy requested Councillor Adams state the
11	substance of and the basis for the Point of Order. Councillor Adams
11	pursuant to Section 3.8 of the Procedure By-law, indicated both motions
11	are a deviation or departure from the rules in the procedure by-law, she
11	questioned the validity of both motions and suggested motion 1
11	proposes to amend the decision and motion 2 proposes to amend and
11	rescind the motion which is contrary to Section 7.2 of the procedure by-
11	law.
11	Deputy Mayor Lustgarten-Evoy called for a recess to confer with staff.
11	Deputy Mayor Lustgarten-Evoy resumed the meeting and ruled the
11	Point of Order put forth by Councillor Adams to be out of order. She
11	indicated support for dividing the motion.
11	Councillor Adams respectfully appealed the ruling of the Chair and
11	requested Members of Council decide whether to uphold or overturn the
11	decision of the Chair.
11	Council voted and upheld the ruling the Chair.
11	Moved by Councillor Bronwynne Wilton, Seconded by Councillor
11	Kimberley Jefferson.
12	THAT the Council of the Township of Centre Wellington waives the
12	requirement for giving notice of a Notice of Motion to Reconsider,
12	pursuant to section 7.2 of Procedure By-law 2021-31, in order to
12	reconsider the decision of Council passed on October 15, 2024,
12	regarding the Bissell Park Multi Use Pad, given the deadline of the
12	Community Sport and Recreation Fund grant application;
12	AND THAT Council rescinds the decision of October 15, 2024 to support
12	a new outdoor refrigerated multi-use surface with mechanical and
