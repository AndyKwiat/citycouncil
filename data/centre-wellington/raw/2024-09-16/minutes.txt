2	9. Delegations
2	9.1 Daniel Segal, Segal Construction (via zoom)
2	re: Municipal Residential Development of Attainable Housing
2	D. Segal, Segal Construction proposed a partnership to provide
2	attainable housing that is funded through a mix of municipal bonds and
2	federal/provincial funding. He noted this approach leverages the
3	municipality's low cost of capital to create attainable housing and once
3	complete, Council can choose to sell the units on the open market,
3	manage them directly, or transfer them to affordable housing providers.
3	Mayor Watters thanked D. Segal for his presentation and after a brief
3	discussion, the following was put forward:
3	Moved by Councillor Bronwynne Wilton, Seconded by Councillor Lisa
3	MacDonald.
3	THAT the Council of the Township of Centre Wellington refer the subject
3	matter presented by Daniel Segal, Segal Construction regarding
3	Municipal residential development of attainable housing to staff for
3	review and investigation and a report back to Council after this years’
3	budget season.
3	CARRIED
3	9.2 Introduction of Artist - Michael Madden
3	Barb Lee from the Elora Fergus Arts Council introduced local artist
3	Michael Madden. Michael Madden highlighted the inspiration for his
3	rural landscapes.
3	9.3 MacLean Hann, Chair Elora BIA spoke in support of the staff proposal
3	regarding item 11.6 Tourism Washroom Project Update and the
3	modification of the design to include Molok containers to address the
3	mounting garbage problem in downtown Elora. He outlined the concerns
3	with the amount of garbage generated, the insufficient weekly pick-up
3	system and the lack of storage for garbage. He responded to questions
3	regarding the amount of garbage generated and efforts taken to reduce
3	that amount.
3	9.4 Randy Vaine
3	re: 11.8 Termite Management Update
3	Randy Vaine, resident spoke regarding item 11.8 Termite Management
3	Update. He expressed concerns with the potential spread of termites.
3	He highlighted the City of Guelph’s program and the belief they have
3	eradicated termites. He suggested the Township consult with the City of
3	Guelph and actively enforce the Township’s termite by-law. He also
3	suggested the Township implement an education program.
3	9.5 Maria Creco-O’Neill, Elora Fergus Termites appeared before Council in
3	support of the termite subsidy program as a starting point. She
3	suggested the Township support termite management and eradication
3	efforts by assisting with wood and soil removal, identify designated
3	termite management zones with tracking and monitoring, the treatment
4	of public spaces, free burn permits, etc.
4	9.6 John Kears and Renata Carter appeared in opposition to the
4	recommendation in item 11.6 Tourism Washroom Project Update with
4	the proposed inclusion of the installation of Molok garbage containers
4	adjacent to the proposed washrooms and their property. He indicated
4	there was no consultation undertaken, they were unaware of the
4	proposal and respectfully requested an alternative location be
4	considered.
4	Geoffry Wild, owner of the Badley and Wild Tart, 61 Metcalfe Street
4	appeared in support of the Molok garbage system but does not support
4	the proposed location. He believes more consultation should take place
4	to seek solutions to reduce the amount of garbage and find storage
4	suitable for all businesses.

7	11.6 Tourism Washroom Project Update
7	Report from P. Newson, Managing Director of Community Services
7	Kasey Beirnes, Building and Facilities Project Manager provided an
7	overview of the proposal to include molok garbage containers adjacent
7	to the proposed washroom building on East Mill Street. He outlined the
7	current challenges and struggles with the garbage and recycling in
7	downtown Elora and noted installation of the moloks would be the first
7	step in finding a solution to the garbage issues.
7	Staff responded to questions regarding the project timeline, consultation
7	with the BIA and what other locations were considered.
8	Moved by Councillor Bronwynne Wilton, Seconded by Councillor Lisa
8	MacDonald.
8	THAT the Council of the Township of Centre Wellington defer
8	consideration of Report CS2024-27 regarding the Tourism Washroom
8	Project Update to the September 30th Council meeting to provide staff
8	an opportunity to provide additional information and respond to
8	questions raised at the September 16th meeting.
8	CARRIED

8	11.8 Termite Management Update
8	Report from A. McNabb, Managing Director of Corporate Services &
8	Treasurer
8	Adam McNabb, Managing Director of Corporate Services & Treasurer
8	provided an overview of the report, indicating staff is seeking Council
8	direction regarding the Township’s involvement in termite management
8	services. He indicated staff are recommending the development of a
8	subsidy style program to support property owners with termite mitigation
8	strategies with the aim to significantly reduce the termite population in
8	Centre Wellington.
9	Moved by Councillor Lisa MacDonald, Seconded by Councillor Jennifer
9	Adams.
9	THAT the Council of the Township of Centre Wellington direct staff to:
9	Part I - develop a subsidy style program based on the details
9	contained in report COR2024-49.
9	Part II - delegate authority to the Managing Director of Corporate
9	Services and Treasurer to sign agreements with Aetna Pest Control
9	Ltd., and Orkin Canada outlining services and preferred pricing.
9	Part III - Bring forward a formalized program and summary of the
9	vendors' communications plan(s) at a future meeting of Council.
9	CARRIED
