1	6.1 Extension of Draft Plan Approval, Draft Plan of Subdivision 23T-16003
2	(Storybrook West)
2	Report from B. Salmon, Managing Director of Planning & Development
2	B. Salmon, Managing Director of Planning & Development provided an
2	overview of the report and background information on the subdivision
2	approval. He noted the draft plan of subdivision 23T-16003 was
2	approved by the Local Planning Appeal Tribunal, now known as the
2	Ontario Land Tribunal. The draft approval was granted because of a
2	settlement between the applicant, the Township of Centre Wellington,
2	and the County of Wellington. It is the County's standard practice to
2	include a lapsing provision in the conditions of approvals, usually five
2	years from the date the draft approval is granted.
2	B. Salmon indicated the draft plan approval will lapse after October 9,
2	2024, and due to an oversight, a special meeting of council was required
2	to avoid the need for the applicant to re-apply for draft plan approval at
2	considerable time and expense.
2	B. Salmon responded to questions regarding alignment with the density
2	target and the timing of the special meeting.
2	Moved by Councillor Denis Craddock, Seconded by Councillor
2	Bronwynne Wilton.
2	THAT the Council of the Township of Centre Wellington supports a
2	three-year extension to the draft plan approval of Plan 23T-16003
2	(Storybrook West);
2	AND THAT a copy of this resolution and Staff Report PLN2024-37 be
2	forwarded to the approval authority (County of Wellington).
2	CARRIED
