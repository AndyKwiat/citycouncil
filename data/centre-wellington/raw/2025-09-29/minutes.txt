6	11. Consideration of Reports
6	11.1 Municipal Accommodation Tax Implementation Update
6	Report from D. Wilson, Chief Administrative Officer
6	Presentation from K. Linton, Linton Consulting
6	D. Wilson, Chief Administrative Officer provided background on the
6	Municipal Accommodation Tax Implementation, noting Council approved
6	a 4 step approach as follows:
6	 Step 1 – Determine the appropriate structure for the MSC
6	 Step 2 – Prepare the legislated required case study to establish
6	the MSC
6	 Step 3 – Community engagement
6	 Step 4 – Preparation of necessary corporation, by-laws, and
6	agreements
6	K. Linton, Linton Consulting provided background on the context and
6	project objectives, the collaborative approach, the current state
6	organizational model, municipal comparative practices, input from
6	Council, the design principles and the preferred model. He outlined the
6	next steps and responded to questions regarding the role of the
6	Executive Director, the reporting structure back to Council and
6	operational oversight, the goal of a well balanced, skill-based Board of
6	Directors, regular communication and the establishment of a sustainable
6	revenue stream for the MSC through MAT revenues, percentage of paid
6	parking revenues and Ontario Lottery and Gaming revenues.
6	Moved by Councillor Bronwynne Wilton, Seconded by Councillor Lisa
6	MacDonald.
6	THAT the Council of the Township of Centre Wellington approve the
7	Municipal Service Corporation Structure for Economic Development and
7	Tourism services, outlined in the Linton Consulting Services Inc. report
7	included in report CAO2025-18;
7	AND THAT Council approve proceeding to community engagement,
7	preparing the required case study to establish a municipal service
7	corporation, and to begin preparation of the necessary articles of
7	incorporation, by-laws, and agreements;
7	AND THAT Council approve the funding increase for the Municipal
7	Accommodation Tax Implementation Project outlined in report
7	CAO2025-18;
7	AND THAT Council direct staff to report back to Council with
7	engagement results, a draft case study, draft by-laws, and draft
7	agreements prior to formally establishing a municipal service
7	corporation.
7	CARRIED
7	Moved by Councillor Bronwynne Wilton, Seconded by Councillor Lisa
7	MacDonald.

7	with Section 7.7(a) of Procedure By-law 2024-65, having reached the 3

9	11.6 Housing Needs Assessment Follow Up Report
9	Report B. Salmon, Managing Director of Planning and Development
9	B. Salmon, Managing Director of Planning and Development provided
9	an overview of the report, indicating staff is seeking Council direction to
9	include housing initiatives outlined in the 2026 Budget and Business
9	Plan, in particular, a) prepare a Township Housing Action Plan, b)
9	investigate the creation of a Township Housing Advisory Committee c)
9	investigate potential applications to Wellington County for housing
9	funding, and d) consider staffing implications of a Township housing
9	mandate going forward.
9	B. Salmon responded to questions regarding the establishment of a
9	housing advisory committee, polices or incentives for developers to build
9	attainable housing.
9	Moved by Councillor Bronwynne Wilton, Seconded by Councillor
9	Jennifer Adams.
9	THAT the Council of the Township of Centre Wellington acknowledges
9	the housing supply and affordability crisis in Ontario and supports the
9	Township having a defined role in addressing this crisis going forward.
9	AND THAT the Township's 2026 Budget and Business Plan incorporate
9	the following in order to affirm the Township's future role in addressing
9	the housing supply and affordability crisis:
9	1. Prepare a Township Housing Action Plan
9	2. Investigate the creation of a Township Housing Advisory
10	Committee
10	3. Investigate potential applications to Wellington County for
10	housing funding
10	4. Consider staffing implications of a Township housing mandate
10	going forward
