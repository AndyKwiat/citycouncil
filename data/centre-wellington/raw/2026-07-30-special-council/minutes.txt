8	14. Notice of Motions
8	14.1 Councillor Wilton - Data Centres
8	Given the rapid growth of AI-driven systems, cloud computing and digital
8	infrastructure has accelerated the development of new and large-scale
8	data centres to meet industry defined needs and the fact that the
8	Province of Ontario has not yet established a provincial regulatory
8	framework for AI data centre industry there is cause to exercise caution
8	regarding the introduction of data centres within the Township of Centre
8	Wellington.
8	1) Therefore the Council of the Township of Centre Wellington directs
8	staff to undertake a review and study costing no more than $49,999
8	(of staff time) of the Township’s land use planning policies in relation
8	to artificial intelligence (AI) data centres, pursuant to section 38 of
8	the Planning Act, applicable to all zones where the use is permitted
8	in the Township of Centre Wellington, including, but not limited to, its
8	Official Plans and Zoning By-law, and any required changes to the
8	Township’s land use planning policies, mapping, and regulatory
8	framework with consideration of, but not limited to the following:
8	a) Existing Official Plan policies and Zoning By-law permissions
8	and regulations;
8	b) Energy use and the impacts to power grids,
8	c) Water use and the impacts to long term drinking water quality
8	and quantity, and
8	d) Noise and light pollution impacts,
8	2) That staff be directed to report back to Council on the items listed
8	above (a) in Q3 of 2027.
8	3) That staff be directed to prepare for Council’s consideration at the
8	Council meeting on September 28, 2026, an Interim Control By-Law
9	under Section 38 of the Planning Act, prohibiting the use of AI data
9	centres within the study area described in (a) above.
