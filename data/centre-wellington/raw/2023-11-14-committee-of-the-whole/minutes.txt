3	8.3 Draft Plan of Subdivision and Zoning Amendment Decision Report -
3	6542 and 6560 Gerrie Road, Elora (James Keating Construction) and
3	Township-initiated Official Plan Amendment
3	Report from M. Iglesias, Manager of Planning
3	M. Iglesias, Manager of Planning provided an overview of the report.
3	She indicated the site is 53 acres with a site plan that results 48
3	residents per hectare with a housing mix of single detached and multi-
3	units with a total of 248 proposed units.
3	Staff responded to questions regarding traffic calming in the proposed
3	subdivision and sustainability measures such as low impact
3	development and construction.
3	Moved by Councillor Jennifer Adams, Seconded by Councillor
3	Kimberley Jefferson.
3	THAT the Council of the Township of Centre Wellington adopts a
3	Township-initiated Official Plan Amendment to designate certain lands
3	Core Greenlands and Residential to implement County Official Plan
3	Amendment No. 69 to expand the Urban Growth Centre boundary and
3	facilitate development of certain lands;
4	THAT the Council of the Township of Centre Wellington recommends to
4	the County of Wellington that proposed draft plan of subdivision 23T-
4	18002, as modified, be granted draft approval, subject to the conditions
4	of approval set out in Schedule "A" to staff report PLN2023-46; and
4	THAT the Council of the Township of Centre Wellington approves
4	rezoning application RZ06/18 and that the appropriate zoning by-law
4	amendment be given three readings.
4	CARRIED
