2	8. Public Meetings to Consider a Zoning Amendment
2	8.1 Proposed Zoning By-law Amendment re 7010 Third Line in West
2	Garafraxa.
2	Mayor Watters stated the purpose of this public meeting is to present and
2	receive public input regarding a proposed zoning by-law amendment on
2	the subject land known as 7010 Third Line, in the former Township of
2	West Garafraxa.
3	He noted the purpose of the proposed zoning by-law amendment is to
3	change the zoning from “A” Agricultural and “EP” Environmental
3	Protection to “A” Agricultural Exception and “EP” Environment Protection
3	on the retained parcel. The effect of the proposed zoning by-law
3	amendment is to implement zoning regulations necessary to restrict
3	residential uses. The zoning amendment is a condition of approval of the
3	County of Wellington Land Division Committee Application B47-25.
3	Mayor Watters stated if a person or public body would otherwise have an
3	ability to appeal the decision of the Township of Centre Wellington to the
3	Ontario Land Tribunal (OLT). However, if the person or public body does
3	not make oral submissions at a public meeting or make written
3	submissions to the Township of Centre Wellington before the by-law is
3	passed, the person or public body is not entitled to appeal the decision.
3	Mayor Watters stated if a person or public body does not make oral
3	submission at the public meeting, or make written submissions to the
3	Township of Centre Wellington before the by-law is passed, the person
3	or public body may not be added as the party to the hearing of an appeal
3	before the OLT unless, in the opinion of the Tribunal, there are reasonable
3	grounds to do so.
3	Mayor Watters advised if anyone wished to be notified of the adoption of
3	the proposed zoning by-law amendment, you must make a written request
3	to the Clerk, Township of Centre Wellington, 1 MacDonald Square, Elora,
3	Ont., N0B 1S0.
3	The Municipal Clerk advised notice was mailed to prescribed persons,
3	agencies and public bodies on or before April 28, 2026. Also, a notice
3	sign was posted on the subject lands on April 28, 2026. He further
3	confirmed the for giving notice was completed on April 28, 2026.
3	Mayor Watters asked Deanna Maiden, Senior Development Planner, to
3	introduce the application.
3	D. Maiden, Senior Development Planner provided an overview of the
3	proposed zoning by-law amendment on the subject land known as 7010
3	Third Line, in the former Township of West Garafraxa. She noted the
3	subject property is designated as prime agricultural land in the County
3	official plan and contains lands identified as green lands and core green
3	lands, it is zoned agricultural and environmental protection, the property
3	was the subject of a severance application, to sever the existing
3	agricultural lands from an existing blacksmith and farrier service
3	operation.
4	D. Maiden indicated the application would rezone the subject lands, which
4	are currently vacant, to prohibit a residential dwelling which is a condition
4	of approval of the severance application. No buildings are proposed and
4	a report will be provided to council at a later date.
4	Mayor Watters called on Hailey Keast from Van Harten Surveying to make
4	a presentation on the application.
4	H. Keast, Van Harten Surveying, delivered a brief overview of the
4	proposed rezoning to prohibit a residential dwelling on the 7‑hectare
4	parcel.
4	At the time of the meeting, no delegations had been registered.
4	Mayor Watters asked if anyone if anyone is present wished to speak to
4	the application.
4	None.
4	At the time of the meeting, there had been no registration of a response
4	from the applicant.
4	Mayor Watters offered the applicant or staff an opportunity to respond to
4	comments/questions raised.
4	None.
4	Mayor Watters offered Council an opportunity to ask any questions of the
4	applicant or staff.
4	None.
4	Mayor Watters thanked everyone for attending and advised Council will
4	be considering the by-law at a future Council meeting and declared the
4	public meeting adjourned.

7	12. Consideration of Reports
7	12.1 Transit Feasibility Study - Final Report
7	Report from Colin Baker, Managing Director of Infrastructure Services
7	C. Baker, Managing Director of Infrastructure Services introduced the
7	Transit Feasibility Study. The study provides a practical framework for
7	considering future transit service within Centre Wellington. Its findings
7	and recommendations have been formed through extensive community
7	consultation, ensuring that the proposed direction supports local needs,
7	travel demands, and implementation strategy.
7	Dennis Kar, Dillon Consulting presented the results of the Transit
7	Feasibility Study and provided an overview of the report to explore
7	practical, financially sustainable public transit options in response to
7	population growth, rising travel demand, and changing mobility needs.
7	The study reviewed local and regional travel (including to Guelph),
7	existing services, costs, governance, and community feedback.
7	D. Kar noted the results show a growing need for transit, supported by the
7	Township’s compact urban form and strong travel links between Fergus
7	and Elora. A phased transit system is recommended, positioning transit
7	as key infrastructure that supports access to jobs, services, tourism, and
7	community participation.
7	D. Kar responded to questions and comments from Council regarding the
7	service plan, costs, external funding opportunities, collaboration with
7	Wellington County, pilot plan and potential opportunity for green vehicles.
7	C. Baker responded to questions from council regarding shared service
7	contract with Wellington County and Municipal Class Environmental
7	Assessment process and communicating service levels to the public.
7	Moved by Councillor Barbara Lustgarten-Evoy, Seconded by Councillor
7	Bronwynne Wilton.
8	THAT the Council of the Township of Centre Wellington receive for
8	information a Transit Feasibility Study as outlined Report No. IS-2026-13
8	dated May 25, 2026.
