3	9.2 Samantha Lawson, Chief Administrative Officer, Grand River
3	Conservation Authority (GRCA) provided background on the proposed
3	2026 operating budget, special projects, watershed health
3	improvements, ongoing support for municipal Source Protection Plan
3	implementation, and the regulations related to funding.
4	She provided an update on the Provincial proposal for the consolidation
4	of conservation authorities, recognizes the need for improvement and
4	the GRCA supports modernization of the system at the source
4	protection region scale.
4	S. Lawson responded to questions regarding the proposed scale of
4	consolidated conservation authorities, continued collaboration with
4	watershed partners, ongoing education and training initiatives for school
4	children, and available government funding opportunities.
4	Moved by Councillor Bronwynne Wilton, Seconded by Councillor
4	Barbara Lustgarten-Evoy.
4	WHEREAS the Government of Ontario approved Bill 68, which
4	establishes the Ontario Provincial Conservation Agency;
4	AND WHEREAS the Government of Ontario is proposing the
4	consolidation of the province's 36 conservation authorities into seven
4	regional conservation authorities, including the Grand River
4	Conservation Authority (GRCA), which will be amalgamated amongst
4	eight current authorities to become part of the proposed Lake Erie
4	Regional Conservation Authority (LERCA);
4	AND WHEREAS the 38 municipalities within the GRCA watershed
4	boundaries today and the 81 municipalities that are proposed to make
4	up the LERCA in 2027 will be levied to fund both the regional
4	conservation authority as well as the provincial conservation agency;
4	AND WHEREAS the new organizations will result in the degradation of
4	local governance, local fiscal accountability, local service delivery, local
4	environmental focus and unprecedented funding by local municipalities
4	of both a large regional authority and a provincial agency;
4	AND WHEREAS the GRCA Board of Directors has responded to Bill 68
4	and the proposal for Conservation Authorities consolidation with a
4	constructive and thoughtful alternative that retains local governance,
4	local service delivery, local environmental focus and local fiscal
4	accountability while responding constructively to the Government of
4	Ontario's concerns about accountability, planning and responsiveness;
4	NOW THEREFORE BE IT RESOLVED THAT the Minister of
4	Environment, Conservation and Parks and the Chief Conservation
4	Executive meet GRCA representatives to review the GRCA alternative
4	proposal to Conservation Authorities consolidation;
4	AND THAT the Minister and the Chief Conservation Executive seriously
5	consider the GRCA proposal for conservation authorities as it
5	enunciates a path forward that will address the Government's concerns
5	most democratically and productively;
5	AND THAT this motion be circulated to the Premier of Ontario, Minister
5	of Environment, Conservation & Parks, Chief Conservation Executive
5	and local Members of Provincial Parliament for their information and
5	action, where appropriate.
5	CARRIED

6	11.5 Bill 68, the Plan to Protect Ontario Act (Budget Measures), 2025 -
6	OMERS Governance Changes
6	WHEREAS the Ontario Municipal Employees Retirement System
7	(OMERS) Pension Fund serves over 1,000 employers and over half a
7	million employees and retirees from diverse groups including: municipal
7	governments, school boards, libraries, police and fire departments,
7	children’s aid societies, and electricity distribution companies; and
7	WHEREAS the long-standing jointly-sponsored governance model with
7	two corporate boards has provided stability, accountability, and fairness
7	for both plan members and employers for more than two decades; and
7	WHEREAS the Government of Ontario has passed legislative changes
7	to OMERS’ governance structure through Bill 68; and
7	WHEREAS these changes would replace the current OMERS Sponsors
7	Corporation with a new Sponsors Council that would lose its corporate
7	status and independent resources; and
7	WHEREAS the proposed model could allow pension decisions affecting
7	municipal employers and employees to be made without meaningful
7	municipal oversight, increasing financial risk for municipalities and local
7	taxpayers; and
7	WHEREAS municipalities are already under significant financial strain
7	and cannot absorb additional pension costs without consequences for
7	property taxes or local services;
7	THEREFORE BE IT RESOLVED THAT Township of Centre Wellington
7	does not support the legislative changes to the OMERS Act contained in
7	Bill 68 and requests that the Government of Ontario reconsider the
7	advisability of proceeding with these changes;
7	FURTHER BE IT RESOLVED THAT the Township of Centre Wellington
7	Council supports the Association of Municipalities of Ontario (AMO) in
7	calling on the Government of Ontario to maintain the current OMERS
7	governance model, with two corporate Boards, as the structure that
7	would best deliver on the long-term interests of municipalities,
7	taxpayers, and employees.
7	BE IT FURTHER RESOLVED THAT this resolution be circulated to:
7	•The Honourable Rob Flack, Minister of Housing and Municipal Affairs;
7	•The Honourable Peter Bethlenfalvy, Minister of Finance;
7	•The Honourable Michael D. Chong, Member of Parliament; and
7	•The Association of Municipalities of Ontario (AMO).
