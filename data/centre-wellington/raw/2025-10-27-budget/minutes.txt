4	Report from K. Beirnes, Manager of Buildings & Property

5	purposes and the requirements required, such as a generator.

5	THAT the Council of the Township of Centre Wellington receives report
5	CS2025-38 Belwood Hall Renovation Update for information.
5	AND THAT the Council of The Township of Centre Wellington directs
5	staff to proceed with the full scope of the Belwood Hall Renovation
5	Project.
5	AND THAT the Council of the Township of Centre Wellington pre-
5	approves a budget top up of $980,000 as part of the 2026 Capital
5	Budget, with $400,000 of this top up funded from grants, donations, and
5	community partnerships.
5	AND THAT the Council of The Township of Centre Wellington directs
5	staff to implement a Donor Capital Program to collect and recognize
5	community donations, to contribute to a portion of the Capital project
5	budget for the Belwood Hall Renovation Project.
5	AND THAT the Council of the Township of Centre Wellington directs
5	staff to initiate the procurement process for a general contractor for the
5	Belwood Hall Renovation Project in 2025.
5	THAT Council directs staff to obtain a cost estimate for electrical to
5	accommodate a future generator at the Belwood Hall to include this
5	location as a reception centre in an emergency.
