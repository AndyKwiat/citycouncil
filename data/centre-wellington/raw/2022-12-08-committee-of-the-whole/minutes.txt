2	Dan Wilson, CAO and Adam McNabb, Managing Director of Corporate Services

2	policies.

2	Officer Charles Hamilton, Wellington County Training Officer provided an
2	overview of the ongoing fire training program.

2	Luisa Artuso, Social Services Administrator, County of Wellington provided an
2	overview of the three divisions of social services; Children’s Early Years,
2	Housing Services and Ontario Works.
2	6. Solid Waste Services
2	Don Kudo, County Engineer, County of Wellington provided an overview of Solid
2	Waste Services, including major functions, current activities, emerging issues
2	and challenges.

2	Sarah Wilhelm, Manager of Policy Planning, County of Wellington provided an
2	overview of the responsibilities of the planning department, including emerging
2	issues related to Bill 23.
2	Karen Chisholme, Climate Change and Sustainability Manager, County of
2	Wellington provided an overview of ongoing initiatives targeted to reduce
2	community greenhouse gases.

2	programs and tools available, including Explore Wellington, highlighting the

2	Source Water Protection, including the origin of source water projection, the
2	Clean Water Act, and municipal well head protection areas.
