3	8.1 Karyn Boscariol, Habitat for Humanity Guelph Wellington
3	re: Hands On For Housing Youth & Housing Conference 2024
3	Karyn Boscariol, Habitat for Humanity Guelph Wellington invited
3	Members of Council to participate in the Hands On For Housing youth
3	conference. She noted, this three-day event held at Elora’s Grand River
3	Raceway is a unique experience provided at no cost to over 150 youth
3	participants from local high schools in the Upper Grand and Wellington
3	Catholic District School Boards, and select community organizations, to
3	address the critical issue of affordable housing. K. Boscariol indicated
3	through hands-on housing advocacy workshops, participants will
3	develop youth-driven recommendations for the future of housing in
3	Wellington County.
3	Karyn Boscariol responded to questions and noted several local skilled
3	trade companies will be participating.

9	partners, service groups, and potential donors to gather commitments

9	current project deficit should the project application be successful;
9	AND THAT Council directs staff to return to Council for further direction
9	and consideration of the project, should the grant application be
9	unsuccessful.

10	11.3 Termite Management Subsidy Program
10	Report from A. McNabb, Managing Director of Corporate Services &
10	Treasurer
10	Adam McNabb, Managing Director of Corporate Services & Treasurer
10	provided an overview of the report seeking Council’s endorsement of the
10	proposed Termite Management Program. He indicated the subsidy
10	program is based on Council direction using $1.39 million dollars to
10	facilitate program spending over a 5-year term. The program subsidy
10	amount is based on the number of properties identified in the 2020/2021
10	survey data and further expanded to account for potential termite
10	migration.
11	A. McNabb responded to questions regarding launch of the program, the
11	communication strategy, plan for treatment of public spaces, and plans
11	for public awareness and education.
11	Moved by Councillor Jennifer Adams, Seconded by Councillor
11	Kimberley Jefferson.
11	THAT the Council of the Township of Centre Wellington approve the
11	Termite Management Subsidy Program;
11	AND THAT the Council of the Township of Centre Wellington direct staff
11	to activate the Termite Management Program with immediate effect;
11	AND THAT the Council of the Township of Centre Wellington pass a By-
11	law to adopt the Termite Management Subsidy Program as presented in
11	report COR2024-57;
11	AND THAT the Council of the Township of Centre Wellington delegate
11	authority to a newly formed Termite Management Review Committee
11	comprised of Township staff to administer the Termite Management
11	Program and delegate authority to the Managing Director of Corporate
11	Services & Treasurer to approve Termite Management Subsidies.
11	CARRIED
