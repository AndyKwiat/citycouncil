4	7.5 Ontario Lottery & Gaming (OLG) Agreement Amendment
4	Report from D. Wilson, Chief Administrative Officer
4	D. Wilson, Chief Administrative Officer reviewed the current contribution
4	agreement for gaming revenue and indicated the amending agreement is
4	required to allow contribution to the Township for sports betting at 4% to
4	be allocated to the Township on a quarterly basis. D. Wilson further
4	outlined the OLG contribution policy on how the funds received are
4	utilized by the Township and further that the policy will be reviewed as
4	part of the 2023 budget process.
4	Moved by Councillor Jennifer Adams, Seconded by Councillor
4	Bronwynne Wilton.
4	THAT the Council of the Township of Centre Wellington authorize the
4	Mayor and Clerk to sign the Ontario Lottery & Gaming agreement
4	amendment as outlined in report CAO2022-13 dated December 12, 2022.
4	CARRIED

4	7.7 2023 Interim Tax Levy By-law
4	Report from A. McNabb, Managing Director of Corporate Services &
4	Treasurer
5	D. Wilson, Chief Administrative Officer indicated this by-law allows
5	property tax bills to be generated in January and mailed to residents. He
5	further explained the first and second installments are calculated based
5	on 50% of the previous years levy. He highlighted the recommended
5	change in installment due dates for commercial properties.
5	Moved by Councillor Barbara Lustgarten-Evoy, Seconded by Councillor
5	Denis Craddock.
5	THAT the Council of the Township of Centre Wellington pass a By-law to
5	provide for an Interim Tax Levy for 2023 and to provide for the payment
5	of taxes by installments.

6	7.10 Fergus Elora Belwood Snowmobile Club Agreement
6	Report from P. Newson, Managing Director of Community Services
6	M. Tucker, Manager of Parks and Facilities Operations outlined the report
6	and introduced the president of the snowmobile club present in the
6	audience. He gave a brief history of the snowmobile club, outlined the
6	season for snowmobiling in our geographic area as typically in January
6	and February, reviewed the clubs responsibility for grooming trails and
6	monitoring safety. He suggested staff has no concerns regarding
6	insurance and liability for the Township under the proposed agreement.
6	In response to questions from Council, staff confirmed the trails are
6	monitored by the OPP as well as the snowmobile club, and there are no
6	additional risks to the Township.
6	Moved by Councillor Lisa MacDonald, Seconded by Councillor Barbara
6	Lustgarten-Evoy.
6	THAT the Council of the Township of Centre Wellington authorize the
6	Mayor and Clerk to sign and execute an agreement between the
6	Township of Centre Wellington and the Fergus Elora Belwood
6	Snowmobile Club for the operation, inspection and maintenance of
6	Snowmobile Trails located on Township property identified in the map
6	attached to report CS2022-16.
6	CARRIED
