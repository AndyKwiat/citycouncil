2	6. Opening Remarks
2	6.1 Mayor Shawn Watters welcomed everyone to their first budget
2	discussion following the election.
2	6.2 Dan Wilson, Chief Administrative Officer gave a presentation providing
2	an executive summary of the 2023 budget and outlined the budget
2	process. He indicated there are many competing priorities for funding
2	including growth and maintaining service levels in a high inflation
2	environment.
2	He indicated staff are presenting a Township Levy increase of 3.48%
2	representing a $45 increase to taxes on the average property assessed
2	at $384,898. This results in a blended rate of 3.19% which includes
2	County, Township and the Education levy. He further reviewed the
2	sources of funding in the operating budget.
2	7. 2023 Draft Budget
2	7.1 Budget Presentation
2	Adam McNabb, Managing Director of Corporate Services & Treasurer
2	reviewed the Asset Management planning process, the link to the
2	annual budget process and the planning recommendations from the
2	Asset Management Plan, the public engagement process to the 2023
2	budget and the 2023 Fees and Charges By-law.
2	Mark Bradey, Manager of Finance & Deputy Treasurer reviewed the Tax
2	supported operating budget explaining the average residential Tax Bill
3	breakdown, how the municipal budget works, shared budget facts
3	indicating the Township operating budget is $49.3 million dollars and
3	reviewed the budget timeline. He outlined the current funding
3	challenges, highlighted areas with a dollar impact of over $10 000 and
3	how the Township is paying for growth.
3	M. Bradey further reviewed the 2023 Capital Budget sources of funding.
3	A. McNabb reviewed the debt analysis from 2022 through to 2033.
3	7.2 Staffing Strategy

3	of the strategy and the staff forecast from 2023 through 2025.

3	CAO’s office, legislated QMS positions, Climate Change Coordinator
3	and Manager of Planning.

5	8. Delegations - 5:00 PM
5	8.1 John Scott, Green Lanes Centre Wellington requested streets be
5	designed for more than just one mode of transportation and an increase
5	in funding to the traffic calming budget. He shared statistics and facts
5	about multi vehicle street design and shared stories about people who
5	are unable to drive anymore and how they get around.
5	8.2 Dr. Sarah Gower addressed Council regarding Health Care Recruitment
5	Funding. She outlined the current issue in the health care system and
5	the introduction of the use of hospitalist positions where physicians
5	assist at Groves Memorial to assess hospital patients and the addition of
5	temporary emergency staff from out of town requiring temporary housing
5	while working in Centre Wellington.
5	Councillor MacDonald requested clarification on what would be the most
5	beneficial for assistance to the hospital.
5	Councillor Lustgarten-Evoy requested clarification if they are looking for
5	additional funding to the $20 000 and asked for further explanation on
5	what funding is required for.
5	8.3 Deputy Mayor Jefferson having declared a conflict left the room. Mayor
5	Watters assumed the Chair.
5	Talia Jenkins, Chair of the Board, Fergus BIA informed Council of the
5	new executive and reviewed the membership. She reviewed the events
5	and challenges of 2022 including garbage collection and indicated they
5	requesting a 6% increase which amounts to approximately $3000 to the
5	levy.
5	8.4 Deputy Mayor Jefferson returned to the meeting and reassumed the
5	Chair.
5	Maclean Hann, Chair of the Board, Elora BIA reviewed the composition
5	of the executive board and the successes of 2022. He indicated they are
5	requesting a 7% levy increase to assist in hosting additional events.
5	8.5 Randy Vaine, resident withdrew his delegation request.
5	8.6 Stephen Kitras addressed Council asking for the creation of a public
5	advisory finance committee and reviewed the qualifications of the
5	individuals he felt should form the advisory committee.
5	He indicated Council could control costs by controlling staffing and the
5	use of the building reserve to cover part of the staffing cost as well as
6	only adding positions that are legally required. Further, he would like
6	Council to reconsider the development of the operations facility and
6	consider funding attainable housing.
6	8.7 Councillor Craddock having declared a conflict left the meeting.
6	Millicent Gordon, CW Black Community outlined the three main events
6	hosted by the CW Black Community for black history month, including
6	the global black mobile museum on February 16 at John Black Public
6	School sponsored by UGDSB, and Awakened Voices February 18 and
6	February 25. She indicated they are seeking $3000 to assist in covering
6	the costs of the event.
