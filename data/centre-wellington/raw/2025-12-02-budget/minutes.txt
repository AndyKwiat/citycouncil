3	7.2 Bissell Park Multi-Use Pad Update
3	Report from K. Beirnes, Manager of Buildings & Property
4	K. Beirnes, Manager of Buildings & Property provided an overview of the
4	project, indicating staff and the Bissell Park Working Group have
4	developed two options now under consideration. These options are as
4	follows:
4	Option 1: Replace Like-for-Like – This option includes replacing the pad,
4	boards, lighting, basketball nets, and constructing an accessible
4	pathway. It provides a modernized facility that maintains its current
4	functionality and supports year-round recreational use.
4	Option 2: Replace Like-for-Like with Refrigeration Infrastructure
4	(preferred option, provided it can be constructed within the current
4	approved budget) – This option mirrors the above but includes installing
4	refrigeration lines and a recirculation pump. Although it does not include
4	the refrigeration plant itself, it prepares the site for future installation,
4	reducing long-term disruption and positioning the facility for improved
4	winter usability when/if additional funding becomes available.
4	K. Beirnes outlined the next steps and the proposed timeline.
4	Discussion ensued and staff responded to questions regarding future
4	design options, refinement of the cost estimates with detailed design,
4	allowing for future refrigeration and anticipated changes to operating
4	procedures and volunteers.
4	Moved by Councillor Barbara Lustgarten-Evoy, Seconded by Councillor
4	Denis Craddock.
4	THAT the Council of the Township of Centre Wellington direct staff to
4	proceed with detailed design of the Bissell Park Multi-Use Pad project
4	as outlined in Report CS2025-40, dated December 2, 2025.
4	CARRIED
