10	12.12 Improvements to Highway 6 Related to Future Development Plans
10	Report from C. Baker, Managing Director of Infrastructure Services
10	C. Baker, Managing Director of Infrastructure Services provided an
10	overview of the report, indicating with significant anticipated growth and
10	employment development planned in the Fergus urban area on both
10	sides of the Highway 6 corridor, the Township would like to establish a
10	collaborative process with land developers, the County of Wellington
10	and the Province (MTO) to complete a preliminary design and staging
10	plan for Highway 6 outside of the connecting link limit of Fergus.
10	C. Baker responded to questions regarding future public meetings in
10	addition to comments submitted through the Ministry’s website,
10	challenges with the required MTO setbacks and noise mitigation
10	requirements on road design, future consideration for transit, and
10	continued discussions and expected timelines regarding a future Fergus
10	by-pass.
10	Moved by Councillor Jennifer Adams, Seconded by Councillor
10	Bronwynne Wilton.
10	THAT the Council of the Township of Centre Wellington direct staff to
10	engage the Ontario Ministry of Transportation in discussion on a
11	collaborative process on the future design of Highway 6 to service
11	population and employment growth in the Fergus urban area;
11	AND THAT Council supports the inclusion of Active Transportation
11	facilities including sidewalks and multi-use paths within the section of
11	the Highway 6 corridor located in the Fergus urban area;
11	AND THAT Council encourages the Ministry of Transportation to
11	consider a roundabout design for the Highway 6 and Second Line and
11	Wellington Road 22 / Eighth Line intersection improvements as part of
11	the MTO's Class Environmental Assessment and review of alternatives.
11	CARRIED

11	Report from K. O’Kane, Manager of Legislative Services & Municipal
11	Clerk
11	K. O’Kane, Manager of Legislative Services & Municipal Clerk provided
11	an overview of the report and staff responded to questions regarding
11	costs related to retaining a consultant and bundling the review of short
