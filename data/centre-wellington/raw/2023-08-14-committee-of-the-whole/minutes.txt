2	7. Delegations
2	7.1 John Scott, Greenlanes indicated his preference for the multi-use pad
2	proposed for Bissell Park to be open, welcoming, inclusive and without
2	boards. He expressed concerns with a design that only promotes hockey
2	and encouraged an open skating rink similar to Nathan Phillips Square in
2	Toronto or the skating rink/splash pad in front of City Hall in Guelph. He
2	wished to see a continuation of all the existing uses in the space and
2	responded to questions.
2	8. Consideration of Reports
2	8.1 Bissell Park Multi-use Pad Project Update
2	Report from P. Newson, Managing Director of Community Services
2	P. Newson, Managing Director of Community Services provided an
2	overview of the report, including details of the current condition of the
2	existing facility, project background and current status, a summary of the
2	request, and the proposed project time frame.
2	Tom Keating, Elora Rockers / Keating Construction introduced David
2	Alves as a dedicated member of the community with 41 years of volunteer
2	service with the ice surface at Bissel Park. He explained the reasons for
2	the delay, the challenges with the project, the financial commitment to
2	date and the desire to see the Township take the lead on over seeing the
2	completion of the project. He indicated an ongoing commitment of support
2	for the project by both the Elora Rockers and the Elora Lions Club and
2	noted thei support includes future fund-raising efforts.
2	Staff and T. Keating responded to questions regarding the role of the
2	Elora Rockers and Lions Club in conjunction with the Healthy
2	Communities Advisory Committee, the ongoing community support and
2	fund-raising efforts, the safety of the existing pad, and the necessary
2	geotechnical work to be undertaken.
2	Moved by Councillor Kimberley Jefferson, Seconded by Councillor Lisa
2	MacDonald.
2	THAT the Council of the Township of Centre Wellington terminate the
2	Bissell Park Multi-Use Pad Project: Jack R MacDonald Grant Agreement,
3	dated September 1, 2020 between the Township of Centre Wellington,
3	James Keating Construction Ltd., and the Elora Lions Club;
3	AND THAT Council approves the allocation of $413,255.52 in existing
3	funds to the Bissell Park Multi-Use Pad Project as outlined in report
3	CS2023-21 dated August 14, 2023;
3	AND THAT Council directs staff to issue a Request for Proposal to hire a
3	consultant for conceptual and detailed design, construction ready
3	documents, and a Class A construction budget, to an upset limit of
3	$100,000;
3	AND THAT Council establish a Bissell Park Multi-Use Pad Project
3	Working Group (under the Healthy Communities Advisory Committee)
3	which will include members of the advisory committee as well as
3	representatives from the Elora Rockers and the Elora Lions Club.
3	CARRIED
