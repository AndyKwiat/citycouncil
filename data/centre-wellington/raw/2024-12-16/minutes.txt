7	11.3 Short Term Rental Licensing By-law Update
7	Report from K. O’Kane, Manager of Legislative Services & Municipal
7	Clerk
7	Kerri O’Kane, Manager of Legislative Services & Municipal Clerk
7	provided an overview of the report and responded to questions.
7	Members of Council provided comments on a requirement for all Short
7	Term Rental’s (STRs) provide a parking plan and no grandfathering of
7	existing STRs within the commercial core, consideration to limit the
7	number of bedrooms permitted to prevent party houses and limiting
7	STRs to principle residences or hosted rather than un-hosted and use of
7	the urban boundary area as a template rather than the heritage overlay.
7	Moved by Councillor Barbara Lustgarten-Evoy, Seconded by Councillor
7	Kimberley Jefferson.
7	THAT the Council of the Township of Centre Wellington receive Report
7	COR2024-77 dated December 16, 2024 for information;
7	AND FURTHER THAT Council directs staff to bring forward a draft
7	licensing by-law to regulate short term rental accommodations and a
7	draft implementation plan for review and comment by the Economic
7	Prosperity & Growth Advisory Committee prior to Council's
7	consideration.
7	CARRIED
