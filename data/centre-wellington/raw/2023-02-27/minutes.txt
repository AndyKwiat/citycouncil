4	8.1 Health Care Professional Recruitment
4	Report from D. Wilson, Chief Administrative Officer
4	D. Wilson, Chief Administrative Officer introduced Angela Stanley,
4	President and CAO of the Wellington Health Care Alliance.
4	Angela Stanley discussed the current challenges with health care
4	recruitment and changes to the delivery of the care model. She further
4	provided suggestions on how to improve recruitment by offering
4	accommodations to physicians temporary servicing the community and
5	when visiting to consider this location as well as providing offices and/or
5	office space for clinicians.
5	Dan Wilson, Chief Administrative Officer updated the Council on the
5	history of assisting with health care recruitment which traditionally has
5	been $20 000 a year and providing subsidized office space. In response
5	to questions from Council, he indicated staff is requesting Council’s
5	approval to establish a working group to bring recommendations back to
5	Council to assist with the issues in health care.
5	Moved by Councillor Barbara Lustgarten-Evoy, Seconded by Councillor
5	Lisa MacDonald.
5	THAT the Council of the Township of Centre Wellington identifies health
5	care professional recruitment as a priority;
5	AND THAT the Council of the Township of Centre Wellington direct staff
5	to work with relevant stakeholders to propose health care professional
5	recruitment approaches that could involve the Township.
5	CARRIED
5	8.2 Zoning Amendment RZ016/22 - 7197 Wellington Road 21, Pilkington
5	Report from M. Iglesias, Senior Planner
5	M. Iglesias, Senior Planner outlined the report, indicating the rezoning
5	application is for 7197 Wellington Road 21 in the former Township of
5	Pilkington. The application seeks to rezone the 26 ha retained farm parcel
5	to add an exception to the Agricultural zoning to prohibit residential uses
5	as required by Provincial and County policies for farm dwelling surplus
5	severances. She further indicated no objections were received from the
5	public or agencies on this application.
5	Moved by Councillor Bronwynne Wilton, Seconded by Councillor Lisa
5	MacDonald.
5	THAT the Council of the Township of Centre Wellington approves
5	rezoning application RZ16/22 for the property at 7197 Wellington Road
5	21, Pilkington, and that the appropriate zoning by-law amendment be
5	given three readings.
5	CARRIED
5	8.3 Zoning Amendment RZ018/22 - 7158 Third Line, West Garafraxa
5	Report from M. Iglesias, Senior Planner
5	M. Iglesias, Senior Planner outlined the report, indicating the subject
5	property is located at 7158 Third Line, in the former Township of West
5	Garafraxa which currently supports an active farming operation and
6	residential dwelling. The zoning amendment would add an exception to
6	the Agricultural zoning to prohibit residential uses as required by
6	Provincial and County policies for farm dwelling surplus severances. The
6	severed parcel with the dwelling also requires an exception to recognize
6	a reduced rear yard setback for the existing barn. She further indicated
6	no objections were received from the public or agencies.
6	Moved by Councillor Bronwynne Wilton, Seconded by Councillor
6	Jennifer Adams.
6	THAT the Council of the Township of Centre Wellington approves
6	rezoning application RZ18/22 for the property at 7158 Third Line, West
6	Garafraxa, and that the corresponding zoning by-law amendment be
6	given three readings.
6	CARRIED
