5	11.2 Use of Indigenous-Themed Names, Logos and Images within or on
5	Township Facilities and Properties Policy
5	Report from K.Bettiol, Manager of Community Development
5	K. Bettiol, Manager of Community Development provided an overview of
5	the report
5	outlining the background and various phases undertaken to the
5	reconciliation-focused policy pathway to respectful indigenous
5	representation. She
5	outlined the key points from the draft policy as follows:
5	• no new indigenous-themed imagery may be introduced by non-
5	indigenous groups;
5	• existing imagery (banners) may be reinstalled, but only with
5	appropriate
5	• historical and cultural context and permanent contextual display;
5	• emphasis on education, transparency and reconciliation through public
5	communication and training;.
5	• establish a framework for respectful and inclusive representation going
5	forward.
5	K. Bettiol highlighted the plans for the contextual display, including the
5	location and the
5	specific wording of the display and introduced the art work of Kory
5	Parkin. She
5	explained the next steps that include: 1) re-installation of the banners
5	with the
5	permanent contextual display, 2) Establish the education components of
5	the
5	reconciliation-focused policy pathway to respectful indigenous
5	representation; 3) build
5	out the webpage linked to the display’s QR code.
5	Artist Kory Parkin provided an overview and background on the
5	permanent contextual
5	Display and shared the inspiration for his artwork.
6	Moved by Councillor Bronwynne Wilton, Seconded by Councillor
6	Kimberley Jefferson.
6	THAT the Council of the Township of Centre Wellington approve the
6	Mayor and
6	Clerk to execute a bylaw to adopt the Use of Indigenous-Themed
6	Names, Logos
6	and Images within or on Township Facilities and Properties Policy,
6	included as
6	Appendix A to report CS2026-06;
6	AND THAT Council approves of and directs Township staff to proceed
6	with the
6	Jefferson Elora Community Centre Permanent Contextual Display as
6	presented in
6	Council Report CS2026-06.

7	11.4 Stormwater Management Facility By-law
7	Report from G. Atkinson, Manager of Wastewater Services
7	Colin Baker, Managing Director of Infrastructure Services provided an
7	overview of the report, indicating the purpose of the by-law is to outline
7	what is and is not allowed to occur in the storm water management
7	facilities. He noted enforcement of the by-law will assist in protecting
7	public safety as stormwater management ponds and channels may look
7	harmless, but often have deep water, sudden drop-offs, slippery banks
7	or fast-moving flow.
7	C. Baker responded to questions regarding invasive species in storm
7	water management facilities and the plan to undertake an education and
7	awareness campaign.
7	Moved by Councillor Bronwynne Wilton, Seconded by Councillor Lisa
7	MacDonald.
7	THAT the Council of the Township of Centre Wellington authorize the
7	Mayor and Municipal Clerk to execute a By-law to regulate and prohibit
7	activity in and around stormwater management facilities within the
7	Township of Centre Wellington.
7	CARRIED
7	11.5 Downtown Elora Traffic Study
7	Report from E. Alessio, Engineering Technologist - Transportation
7	Colin Baker, Managing Director of Infrastructure Services introduced
8	Trevor Brown, the new Manager of Engineering and Public Works.
8	Ryan Maiden, Supervisor of Capital Projects and Transportation
8	provided an overview of the report, indicating staff is recommending a
8	study be completed to assess current traffic conditions in the downtown
8	core and provide strategies to address congestion.
8	In response to questions, R. Maiden explained the project, led by the
8	Township, will be completed in partnership with the County of
8	Wellington. The project budget is $100,000 with Wellington County
8	contributing $50,000 of the funding and the Township contributing
8	$50,000. Staff responded to additional questions regarding the fall 2025
8	study undertaken by the County and the recommendations to be
8	implemented, the timing and scope of the project.
8	Moved by Councillor Lisa MacDonald, Seconded by Councillor Denis
8	Craddock.
8	THAT the Council of the Township of Centre Wellington directs staff to
8	issue a Request For Proposal for a Downtown Elora Traffic Study;
8	AND THAT Council approves the Township’s share of funding as
8	outlined in Report No. IS2026-05.
8	CARRIED
