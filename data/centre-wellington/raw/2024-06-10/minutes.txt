5	Decision Report OP02/23 and RZ05/23: 19-19A East Mill St.
5	Report from M. Iglesias, Manager of Planning Services
5	Mariana Iglesias, Manager of Planning Services outlined the report
5	indicating the property is a 0.3 hectare site, on the east side of Geddes
5	St, south of East Mill Street in the Elora Salem Urban Centre. It currently
5	contains a vacant residential dwelling along East Mill Street and a shed
5	proposed to be demolished, as well as a former industrial use heritage
5	building, which housed the Elora Concrete Burial Vault factory. The
5	property is in an area designated “Residential” in the Official Plan and
5	adjacent to the “Central Business District Commercial” designation to
5	the west and north, with Residential to the east.
5	She indicated the owner is seeking an Official Plan and zoning
5	amendment to change it from Residential - R1A to Central Business
5	District and C1 – commercial exception zoning. The purpose of the
5	applications are to facilitate an infill development of 18 residential units
5	and ground floor commercial space in a proposed 4 storey mixed use
5	building, with a commercial building at the rear of the property closest to
5	the river that will house a future restaurant. Staff anticipate a future
5	application for a plan of condominium on the property.
5	Moved by Councillor Kimberley Jefferson, Seconded by Councillor
5	Barbara Lustgarten-Evoy.
5	THAT the Council of the Township of Centre Wellington approves
5	applications OP02/23 and RZ05/23;
5	AND THAT proposed Official Plan Amendment No. 15 be adopted and
5	submitted to the County of Wellington for approval and the required by-
5	law given three readings and passed;
5	AND THAT the proposed Zoning By-law Amendment be given three
6	readings and passed.
6	CARRIED
6	Councillor Wilton rejoined the meeting.

7	8.4 2025 Budget Directions
7	Report from A. McNabb, Managing Director of Corporate Services &
7	Treasurer
7	A. McNabb, Managing Director of Corporate Services & Treasurer
7	gave a presentation outlining the report indicating staff is seeking
7	direction for the preparation of the 2025 budget.
7	Staff responded to questions regarding the budget meeting schedule,
7	the timing of the termite management program, what the increase would
7	like to the average resident, and use of funds in the sale of lots from the
7	new business park.
7	Council requested fees and charges be looked at with a view of the
7	impact to residents and user groups.
7	Moved by Councillor Denis Craddock, Seconded by Councillor Barbara
7	Lustgarten-Evoy.
7	THAT the Council of the Township of Centre Wellington receive report
7	COR2024-27 dated June 10, 2024 as information.
7	AND THAT the following direction be provided to staff in the preparation
7	of the 2025 Draft Budget:
7	1: Maintain a 2025 tax rate increase equal to or less than 4.5%
7	excluding asset and termite management allocations,
7	2: That staff instate an increase equivalent to a 2% increase in taxation
7	during the 2025 Draft Budget process to generate the required funds to
7	mitigate the funding gap within specific asset types identified in the
8	Township's 2022 Asset Management Plan.
8	3: That staff instate an increase of $88,500 to the total levy requirement
8	for the 2025 Draft Budget process to generate the funds required to
8	ensure sufficient Termite Management program funding.
8	4: Assume 2025 assessment growth equal to 2.5%, to be used to fund
8	budget increases considered to be growth in nature, with assessment
8	growth figures to be updated in November, when received.
8	5: Increase fees and charges for 2025 by 4.5%, other than where
8	pressure on rates indicates an alternate rate change.
8	6: Maintain a 3-year funding average of approximately $1,900,000 in
8	growth related capital projects to be funded by development charges
8	within the Draft Ten Year Capital Forecast.
8	7: Allocate the following funding to the Draft Capital Budget:
8	· $7,870,000 in combined General Capital Reserve, Canada
8	Community-Building Fund (CCBF), OLG Funds, and Ontario Community
8	Infrastructure Fund (OCIF).
8	· An increase in funding to the tax supported Equipment, Vehicle,
8	and Facility Replacement Reserves to support budgetary needs.
8	8: That Council approve the proposed 2025 Budget Schedule.
8	Moved by Councillor Adams
8	Seconded by Councillor Wilton
8	THAT the motion be amended to include “staff recommendations to
8	achieve 3% or less to the end of item “1: Maintain a 2025 tax rate
8	increase equal to or less than 4.5% excluding asset and termite
8	management allocations”
8	Councillor Adams and Councillor Wilton agreed to withdraw the motion
8	and note in the minutes that staff will provide recommendations to
8	Council on how the projected tax increase may be reduced.
8	Moved by Councillor Craddock
8	Seconded by Councillor Evoy
8	THAT the Council of the Township of Centre Wellington receive report
8	COR2024-27 dated June 10, 2024 as information.
9	AND THAT the following direction be provided to staff in the preparation
9	of the 2025 Draft Budget:
9	1: Maintain a 2025 tax rate increase equal to or less than 4.5%
9	excluding asset and termite management allocations,
9	2: That staff instate an increase equivalent to a 2% increase in taxation
