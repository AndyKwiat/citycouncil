3	9. Presentations and Announcements
3	9.1 County Council Report
3	Mary Lloyd, County Councillor Ward 5 provided an overview of the
3	ongoing County road, bridge and culvert projects, upgrades at the Elora
3	transfer station, assessed policing cost of $430.29 per residence, social
3	housing activities, the addition of an e-bike at Wellington Terrace,
3	museum activities, Ridewell usage and upcoming Remembrance Day
3	ceremonies.
3	9.2 Nadia Marquez Pabst, Vice President, Policy & Regulatory Affairs, Aypa
3	Power provided an update on Aypa Power’s Elora Battery Energy
3	Storage System (BESS) Project they are exploring as a part of The
3	Independent Electricity System Operator's Long-term 1 Request for
3	Proposals to expand Ontario’s grid capacity over the course of the
3	decade. She explained Aypa Power would like Council to consider
3	issuing a Municipal Support Resolution (MSR) for Aypa Power to include
3	as a part of its bid submission which represents a sizable portion of
3	points. The MSR is to acknowledge support from Centre Wellington to
3	host the project in principle and does not supersede zoning by-laws or
3	environmental assessments. N. Marquez Pabst noted it is important to
3	note this request is time sensitive as the bid submission deadline is
3	December 12, 2023.
3	Nadia Marquez Pabst and Ondrej Benjik responded to questions
3	regarding the potential strain on volunteer fire departments and
3	emergency protocols, compliance with the Canada Environmental Act,
3	engagement with the indigenous community, use of water reservoirs,
3	potential job creation, partnership with the community foundation, visual
3	screening, battery disposal and recycling, land requirements, taxation
3	and rehabilitation.
3	After a brief discussion, the following motion was put forward:
3	Moved by Councillor Lisa MacDonald, Seconded by Councillor
3	Bronwynne Wilton.
3	THAT consideration of municipal support for Aypa Power’s Elora Battery
3	Energy Storage System (BESS) Project be referred to staff for a report.
3	CARRIED

4	10. Report from the Committee of the Whole – October 16, 2023
4	10.1
4	Moved by Councillor Jennifer Adams, Seconded by Councillor
4	Bronwynne Wilton.
4	THAT the Council of the Township of Centre Wellington approve the
4	report from the Committee of the Whole dated October 16, 2023 as
4	follows.
4	1. THAT the Council of the Township of Centre Wellington receive report
4	IS2023-27 regarding the implementation of the Memorandum of
4	Understanding required as part of the Conservation Authority Act,
4	between the Township and with the Grand River Conservation Authority;
4	AND THAT Council approves entering into said Memorandum of
4	Understanding and delegates authority to sign the Memorandum of
4	Understanding to the Mayor and Clerk.
4	2. THAT the Council of the Township of Centre Wellington direct staff to
4	include the 2024 Preliminary Draft Capital Budget and the Preliminary
4	Draft Bridge and Major Culverts 10 Year Plan, as outlined in report
4	COR2023-87 dated October 16, 2023, in the 2024 Draft Budget.
4	3. THAT the Council of the Township of Centre Wellington direct staff to
4	use the existing OLG Funding Allocation Policy, as outlined in By-law
4	2023-02 for 2024 Budget purposes, with the policy to be reviewed as
4	part of the 2025 Budget process.
4	4. THAT the Council of the Township of Centre Wellington direct staff to
4	include the 2024 staff initiatives outlined in report CAO2023-11 in the
4	Draft 2024 Business Plan.
4	AND THAT any changes made to the 2024 staff initiatives be tracked
4	and presented to Council during 2024 Budget deliberations.
4	5. THAT the Council of the Township of Centre Wellington receives for
4	information Report No. IS2023-25 St. David Street North Reconstruction
4	Project Update.
4	AND THAT Council endorses the implementation of a new controlled
4	pedestrian crossing of St. David Street North at St. George Street, which
4	will be constructed with the St. David Street Reconstruction Project.
5	6. THAT the Council of the Township of Centre Wellington authorizes
5	Township Staff to undertake a pilot program to implement 40 kilometer
5	per hour posted speed limits in an urban neighborhood in Centre
5	Wellington, and report back to Township Council with findings and
5	recommendations.
5	AND THAT Council authorizes Township Staff to begin a community
5	consultation program on 40 kilometer per hour posted speed limit
5	neighborhoods.
5	7. THAT the Council of the Township of Centre Wellington authorize the
5	Mayor and Municipal Clerk to execute a by-law to delegate certain
5	powers and duties.
5	8. THAT the Council of the Township of Centre Wellington repeal By-law
5	2007- 021, being a By-law to Designate the Township of Centre
5	Wellington as an Area Subject to Demolition Control in Accordance with
5	Section 33 of the Planning Act R.S.O. 1990.
5	CARRIED
