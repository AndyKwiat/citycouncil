2	8. 6:00 PM - Public Meeting to Consider a Proposed Zoning Amendment
2	8.1 Mayor Watters indicated the purpose of the public meeting is to present
2	the proposed by-law amendment and to receive public input prior to
2	making a decision. The Township’s File Number for this application is
2	RZ07/23.
2	Mayor Watters stated the purpose of the proposed zoning by-law
2	amendment is to change the zoning from “A” Agricultural and “EP”
2	Environmental Protection to “A” Agricultural Exception and “EP”
2	Environmental Protection on the retained parcel to restrict residential
2	uses, and to permit a reduced lot frontage on the severed parcel. The
2	effect of the proposed zoning by-law amendment is to implement zoning
2	regulations necessary to facilitate a farm dwelling surplus severance.
2	The zoning amendment is a condition of approval of the County of
2	Wellington Land Division Committee Application B19-23.
2	If a person or public body does not make oral submissions at the public
2	meeting, or make written submissions to the Township of Centre
2	Wellington before the by-law is passed, the person or public body is not
2	entitled to appeal the decision of the Township of Centre Wellington to
2	the Ontario Land Tribunal (OLT).
2	If a person or public body does not make an oral submission at the
2	public meeting, or make written submissions to the Township of Centre
3	Wellington before the by-law is passed, the person or public body may
3	not be added as the party to the hearing of an appeal before the OLT
3	unless, in the opinion of the Tribunal, there are reasonable grounds to
3	do so.
3	If you wish to be notified of the adoption of the proposed zoning by-law
3	amendment, you must make a written request to the Clerk, Township of
3	Centre Wellington, 1 Macdonald Square, Elora, Ont., N0B 1S0.
3	K. O'Kane, Municipal Clerk, advised the notice was mailed to prescribed
3	persons, agencies, and public bodies on or before August 31, 2023.
3	Also, a copy of the notice was advertised in the Wellington Advertiser on
3	August 31, 2023.
3	Presentation: Hailey Keast, Planning Technician, Van Harten Surveying
3	Delegations:
3	Richard Wright, the applicant, indicated very few changes would be
3	made to the property through the proposed amendment. His family has
3	farmed the land for 25 years and will continue to farm the land.
3	No comments from Council.

4	Report from M. Iglesias, Manager of Planning Services

4	of Intent to Designate the property municipally known as 255 Lamond

5	outlined in Section 29 of the Ontario Heritage Act; and
5	THAT, if no objections are received within the prescribed time period,

5	enactment as per Ontario Heritage Act requirements;
5	AND THAT staff be authorized to proceed with establishing a Pierpoint
