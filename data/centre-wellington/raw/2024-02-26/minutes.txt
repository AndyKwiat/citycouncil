4	2024 Downtown Parking Strategy
4	Report from D. Wilson, Chief Administrative Officer
4	Dan Wilson, Chief Administrative Officer introduced the Municipal
4	Parking Strategy and the consultant Gordon Hui from RJ Burnside &
4	Associates Limited.
4	Gordon Hui outlined the phases of the parking study including a review
4	of the study process and the parking needs and opportunities, as well as
4	presented the draft recommendations and financial considerations.
4	The recommendations, as a result of the study, include increasing the
4	parking supply with joint use agreements with private spaces, utilizing
4	the parking lot at the Jefferson Elora Community Centre, planning for
4	the construction of a parking garage/structure and consideration of paid
5	parking implementation options. In addition, active transportation can
5	lessen the parking burden through bike shares and a shuttle bus
5	program.
5	Mr. Hui further outlined the financial considerations including the
5	estimated expense for a parking structure, joint use agreements, shuttle
5	bus, enforcement, and paid parking implementation. He further outlined
5	the anticipated revenue generation from paid parking and enforcement.
5	D. Wilson outlined the staff recommendation including the requests for
5	2024. He indicated anything beyond 2024 will come forward in a future
5	Council report.
5	Councillor Lustgarten-Evoy requested meetings be set with the BIA’s to
5	review the options.
5	Staff responded to questions regarding the enforcement of 3 hour
5	parking, shuttle stops and parking for the shuttle stops, accessible
5	parking for churches and medical offices, how growth was included in
5	the determination of the parking needs, and the cost to expand the
5	program outside of the downtown areas.
5	Further discussion occurred around the topics of parking enforcement,
5	paid parking and issuing parking passes.
5	Moved by Councillor Bronwynne Wilton, Seconded by Councillor Lisa
5	MacDonald.
5	THAT the Council of the Township of Centre Wellington receive the
5	Municipal Downtown Parking Strategy Report for information;
5	AND THAT Council direct staff to execute the proposed parking
5	implementation plan for 2024;
5	AND THAT Council direct staff to investigate a parking enforcement
5	administration system for 2024 outside of the County process and report
5	back with proposed solutions;
5	AND THAT Council give delegated authority to the Managing Director of
5	Corporate Services & Treasurer to sign contracts and agreements
5	related to Municipal Downtown Parking Strategy implementation in 2024
5	as outlined in this report;
5	AND THAT Council direct staff to prepare a report in conjunction with
5	the 2025 Budget process that will bring forward the Municipal Downtown
6	Parking Strategy for Council adoption and outline parking
6	implementation recommendations for beyond 2024.
6	CARRIED

6	8.2 Municipal Class Environmental Assessment Study: Preliminary
6	Preferred Alternative for Bridges 1-P, 28-P, 30-P, 32-P, and 33-P
6	Report from A. Dickieson, Engineering Services Coordinator
6	Adam Dickieson, Engineering Services Coordinator gave a presentation
6	indicating the Municipal Class Environmental Assessment Study
6	recommends the preferred option for each bridge within the study. He
6	reviewed the process including public consultation, feedback received,
6	alternative solutions considered, evaluation criteria, and the preferred
6	alternative to reopen 28-P, 32-P and 33-P and removal of bridge 1-P
6	and 30-P. He further reviewed the bridge replacement timeline which
6	includes construction in 2027.
6	Moved by Councillor Barbara Lustgarten-Evoy, Seconded by Councillor
6	Kimberley Jefferson.
6	THAT the Council of the Township of Centre Wellington endorse
6	Preferred Alternative No. 5 – Replacement of Bridges 28-P, 32-P and
6	33-P and the removal without replacement of Bridges 1-P and 30-P as
6	outlined in Report No. IS2024-04, dated February 26, 2024;
6	AND THAT Council directs staff to publish the Notice of Study
6	Completion for Municipal Class Environmental Assessment Study for
6	Bridges 1-P, 28-P, 30-P, 32-P and 33-P in accordance with the
6	requirements of the Environmental Assessment Act.
6	CARRIED
