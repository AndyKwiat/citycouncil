4	11.2 Additional Residential Unit (ARU) Zoning Regulation Update
4	Report from B. Salmon, Managing Director of Planning & Development
4	B. Salmon, Managing Director of Planning & Development provided an
4	overview of the report, indicating the addition of one or more dwelling
4	unit to existing residences is a form of gentle residential intensification
4	that has been permitted by municipal planning regulations for several
4	years. He outlined the changes to the final draft noting staff are of the
4	opinion the proposed amendment is in accordance with the provisions of
4	the Planning Act and its regulations.
4	Moved by Councillor Bronwynne Wilton, Seconded by Councillor
4	Kimberley Jefferson.
4	THAT the Council of the Township of Centre Wellington adopt a zoning
4	by-law amendment to regulate Additional Residential Units in Centre
4	Wellington;
4	AND THAT the Council of the Township of Centre Wellington confirms
4	that further public notice is not required;
4	AND THAT the corresponding by-law be given three readings and
4	passed.
4	CARRIED

5	11.4 OLG Funding Allocation Policy
5	Report from A. McNabb, Managing Director of Corporate Services &
5	Treasurer
5	Adam McNabb, Managing Director of Corporate Services & Treasurer
5	provided an overview of the report and outlined the proposed
5	amendment to the Township’s allocation policy as follows:
5	92% to Township Capital
5	4% to Community Impact Grants
5	2% to Heritage and
5	2% to Economic Development.
5	Councillor Wilton suggested allocating the 2% heritage transfer to the
5	housing reserve as seed money to facilitate projects related to stainable
5	housing.
5	D. Wilson, CAO responded to questions regarding future funding of
5	housing initiatives and the establishment of a housing advisory
5	committee.
5	Moved by Councillor Bronwynne Wilton, Seconded by Councillor
5	Jennifer Adams.
5	THAT the Council of the Township of Centre Wellington directs staff to
5	redirect the 2% heritage allocation to the housing reserve for 2026.
5	CARRIED
5	Moved by Councillor Lisa MacDonald, Seconded by Councillor Denis
5	Craddock.
5	THAT the Council of the Township of Centre Wellington authorize the
5	Mayor and Clerk to execute a by-law to amend the OLG Funding
5	Allocation Policy as amended;
5	AND THAT the Council of the Township of Centre Wellington directs
5	staff to use the Council amended policy in the 2026 Budget, with the
5	policy to be reviewed as part of the 2027 Budget.
5	CARRIED, as amended
