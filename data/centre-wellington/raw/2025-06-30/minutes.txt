3	6.2 Juliane von Westerholt, MHBC Planning re Item 8.4 Water and
3	Wastewater Servicing Plan – Notice of Study Completion appeared on
3	behalf of Elora Greens Inc., owners of 127 First Line in the newly
3	expanded areas of OPA 126. She indicated her clients are seeking the
3	following confirmation:
3	A) That the timing of infrastructure improvements is development driven.
3	B) That the Township is willing to consider alternatives to water
3	servicing should they be necessary and would do so once an application
3	is submitted and before them for review.
3	C) That Water supply upgrades (i.e. the water storage and watermain to
3	interconnect the Elora and Fergus water distribution systems) are not
3	required to permit the development of the ER1 lands in Elora. In other
3	words, these works will not impede a property owner within the ER1
3	area from making a development application and having it considered
3	by the municipality for approval; and
3	D) That the Township consider and accept alternative wastewater
3	servicing strategies (or modified servicing strategies) to permit the
3	development of 127 First Line in advance of any employment land
3	development.

5	8.2 Use of Indigenous-Themed Names, Logos and Imagery Within or on
5	Township Facilities and Properties Update
5	Report from K. Bettiol, Manager of Community Development
5	Presentation by K. Bettiol, Manager of Community Development and
5	Kevin Sandy, CEO of Haudenosaunee Lacrosse
5	K. Bettiol, Manager of Community Development provided an overview of
5	the background and developments, indicating the purpose of the report
5	is to: a) present a draft policy for the use of Indigenous-themed names,
5	logos, and imagery within or on Township facilities and properties, b)
5	outline the reconciliation-focused policy pathway to respectful
5	Indigenous representation, c) hear from Indigenous Leader, Kevin
5	Sandy regarding the process and impacts on youth; and d) review
5	engagement and consultation plan and request approval for the
5	allocation of $20,000 from the DEI Capital Project.
5	Kevin Sandy, Cayuga Nation Wolf Clan, Six Nations shared insight on
6	the journey of education and awareness.
6	K. Bettiol responded to questions regarding collaboration efforts with the
6	local school boards, the plan to reinstate the banners in their original
6	locations at the Jefferson Elora Community Centre, along with a
6	permanent contextual display that shares the significance of the banners
6	and the community’s thoughtful journey toward respectful representation
6	in local lacrosse, and expansion of the financial allocation.
6	Moved by Councillor Wilton
6	Seconded by Councillor
6	That the funding allocated be raised from $20,000 to $40,000.
6	Councillor Wilton withdrew her amendment.
6	Moved by Councillor Bronwynne Wilton, Seconded by Councillor Denis
6	Craddock.
6	THAT the Council of the Township of Centre Wellington receive the
6	Draft Use of Indigenous-Themed Logos and Imagery Within or on
6	Township Facilities and Properties Policy for information, and directs
6	staff to return to Council with a final draft Policy for a future Council
6	meeting;
6	AND THAT Council directs staff to execute a communication plan to
6	share the draft policy for feedback;
6	AND THAT Council directs staff to collaborate with the Diversity, Equity
6	and Inclusion (DEI) Working Group, Indigenous Partners and the local
6	lacrosse organizations to finalize the permanent contextual display for
6	the Jefferson Elora Community Centre, and return to Council at the
6	same time as the final policy for consideration of approval;
6	AND THAT Council directs staff to execute the Reconciliation-Focused
6	Policy Pathway to Respectful Indigenous Representation, outlined in
6	Report CS2025-15;
6	AND THAT Council allocate $20,000 from the DEI Capital Project to
6	execute the Reconciliation-Focused Policy Pathway to Respectful
6	Indigenous Representation.
6	CARRIED

7	8.4 Water and Wastewater Servicing Master Plan - Notice of Study
7	Completion
7	Report from R. Maiden, Water and Wastewater Capital Project Manager
7	R. Maiden, Water and Wastewater Capital Project Manager provided an
7	overview of the report, indicating the Township has prepared its first
7	Water and Wastewater Servicing Master Plan (WWSMP). The WWSMP
7	identifies the preferred water and wastewater servicing strategy to
7	service population and employment growth to the 2051 planning
7	horizon.
7	Staff responded to questions regarding the request from the delegation
7	and the staging plan for development.
7	Moved by Councillor Lisa MacDonald, Seconded by Councillor
7	Bronwynne Wilton.
7	THAT the Council of the Township of Centre Wellington endorses the
7	Water and Wastewater Servicing Master Plan and directs staff to publish
7	a Notice of Study Completion for the Study, as outlined in Report
7	IS2025-21;
7	AND THAT Council directs staff to bring forward programs, studies and
7	projects included in the Water and Wastewater Servicing Master Plan
7	for consideration in capital and operating budget deliberations.
8	CARRIED
