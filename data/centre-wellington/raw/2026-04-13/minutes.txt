4	8.1 Growing Canada’s Community Canopies – Urban Forestry Plans and
4	Studies Grant Submission
4	L. Rogerson, Manager of Parks provided an overview of the report,
4	indicating staff are seeking Council support to apply for the Federation of
4	Canadian Municipalities' (FCM) Green Municipal Fund under the Growing
4	Canada’s Community Canopies (GCCC) – Urban Forestry Plans and
4	Studies stream. L. Rogerson noted the proposed project will include an
4	urban forest assessment and analysis that will strengthen the Township’s
4	tree inventory, canopy analysis, and integration of urban forestry data into
4	GIS and natural asset management practices. The project would run from
4	June 1, 2026, to August 30, 2027. Staff are requesting $89,160 in grant
4	funding, and the Township will contribute $22,300 of in-kind support for
4	staff time from the Urban Forestry operating budget, for a total project
4	cost of $111,460.
4	Staff responded to questions regarding the extent of the in-kind services,
4	the community engagement strategy, and utilization of the canopy data
4	collected.
4	Moved by Councillor Bronwynne Wilton, Seconded by Councillor Barbara
4	Lustgarten-Evoy.
5	THAT the Council of the Township of Centre Wellington authorize staff to
5	submit an application to the Federation of Canadian Municipalities for
5	funding under the Growing Canada’s Community Canopies initiative in
5	support of the Urban Forest Resilience and Natural Asset Management
5	Initiative;
5	AND THAT Council endorse an in-kind contribution of $22,300 for staff
5	time spent on the project from the Urban Forestry operating budget;
5	AND THAT Council approves granting delegated authority to the
5	Managing Director of Corporate Services & Treasurer to sign a letter
5	confirming the Township’s in-kind support for the project, which will be
5	included with the grant funding application.

5	8.2 Water Capacity Buy-Back Program
5	Report from I. Duncan, Water Conservation Coordinator
5	Ian Duncan, Water Conservation Coordinator outlined the details of the
5	proposed Capacity Buy-Back Program, noting the program seeks to
5	provide large water users in the Township, who have participated in the
5	Township Water Audit Program, an incentive to implement water
5	efficiency recommendations stemming from their Water Audit Program
5	Report. He indicated a one-time incentive of up to fifty percent (50%) of
5	retrofit costs to a maximum of $10,000 calculated based on a rebate of
5	$1000 per cubic meter of water saved per average day will be offered to
5	participating Industrial, Commercial, Institutional and Community
5	applicants.
5	Staff responded to questions regarding educational opportunities and
5	success stories.
5	Moved by Councillor Bronwynne Wilton, Seconded by Councillor Denis
5	Craddock.
5	THAT the Council of the Township of Centre Wellington endorse the
5	recommended Water Capacity Buy-Back Program and associated Terms
5	& Conditions.
5	CARRIED
5	8.3 Donation, Sponsorship and Naming Rights Policy
5	Report from K. Bettiol, Manager of Community Development
6	Kristen Bettiol, Manager of Community Development indicated Township
6	staff have amended the existing Sponsorship and Naming Rights Policy
6	to include Donations.
6	The amended Policy, now titled the Donation, Sponsorship and Naming
6	Rights Policy includes the following changes: a) formally adds Donations
6	into the same governance framework; b) creates clearer distinctions
6	between Donations vs. Sponsorship/Naming Rights; c) introduces
6	donation-specific requirements and controls; d) defines Restricted
6	Donations, Confidential Donations, Significant Value thresholds, and
6	when a Donation Agreement is required; e) strengthens transparency,
6	fairness, and reputational safeguards across all contribution types; f)
6	adds/clarifies privacy and MFIPPA handling of donor information; g)
6	provides delegated authority to the CAO and Managing Director of
6	Community Services to execute Donation Agreements, consistent with
6	what has already been delegated for Naming Rights and Sponsorship
6	Agreements; and h) strengthens governance of Restricted Donations.
6	Staff responded to questions regarding maintaining a balance between
6	sense of place and brand our natural and green space, continuing with
6	current practices regarding park and amenity naming, and provided
6	clarification regarding the restricted donations.
6	Moved by Councillor Lisa MacDonald, Seconded by Councillor Kimberley
6	Jefferson.
6	THAT the Council of the Township of Centre Wellington receives Report
6	CS2026-13: Donation, Sponsorship and Naming Rights Policy for
6	information;
6	AND THAT the Council of the Township of Centre Wellington authorize
6	the Mayor and Clerk to execute a by-law to adopt the amended Donation,
6	Sponsorship and Naming Rights Policy, included as an attachment to
6	Report CS2026-13, and repeal By-law 2023-04;
6	AND THAT Council delegate authority for the execution of Agreements,
6	as outlined in the amended Donation, Sponsorship and Naming Rights
6	Policy, to the Managing Director of Community Services and Chief
6	Administrative Officer;
6	AND THAT Staff be directed to return to Council at a future meeting with
6	amendments to the Delegation of Authority Bylaw 2023-78 to include
6	Donation, Sponsorship and Naming Rights Agreements.
6	CARRIED
7	8.4 Non-Exclusive Radio Transmitter License Agreement
7	Report from C. Baker, Managing Director of Infrastructure Services
7	Dino Masiero, Manager of Water Services and Environmental
7	Sustainability indicated The Grand 101 has requested permission to
7	place a relay antenna on the Gartshore Water Tower, which is owned by
7	the Township of Centre Wellington (Township). The purpose of the relay
7	antenna is to provide wireless communications between The Grand
7	101's radio station at 540 Garafraxa Street in Fergus and their FM
7	transmitter located on a Rogers Communications tower on Wellington
7	Road 18 in Salem.
7	He indicated in order to allow the relay antenna in a clear and controlled
7	way, the Township has prepared a non-exclusive radio transmitter
7	license agreement setting out the terms and conditions under which The
7	Grand 101 can install, operate and maintain its radio equipment on
7	Township property.
7	Moved by Councillor Denis Craddock, Seconded by Councillor
7	Bronwynne Wilton.
7	THAT the Council of the Township of Centre Wellington authorize the
7	Mayor and Clerk to execute a Non-Exclusive Radio License Agreement
7	between the Township of Centre Wellington and The Grand 101 Radio
7	for the installation, operation and maintenance of a relay antenna on the
7	Gartshore Water Tower as outlined in Report No. IS2026-11 dated April
7	13, 2026.

7	8.5 Service Financing Commitment Agreement with Westbow Fergus GP Ltd
7	and Salem Development Company
7	Report from C. Baker, Managing Director of Infrastructure Services
7	D. Wilson, Chief Administrative Officer provided an overview of the report
7	indicting Centre Wellington has been working with private landowners to
7	advance the planning and servicing of the South Fergus lands to support
7	future residential and commercial development. He noted these lands
7	require significant upgrades and extensions to municipal infrastructure,
7	including roads, water, wastewater, stormwater systems, and related
7	transportation studies, before development can proceed.
7	D. Wilson indicated to enable this work to move forward in a coordinated
7	and timely manner, the Township and the landowners have negotiated a
8	Service Financing Commitment Agreement (SFCA). The agreement sets
8	out the terms under which the Township will undertake the detailed
8	engineering design for required external works, as well as the cost-
8	sharing between the developer and the Township.
8	Staff responded to questions regarding the urbanization of Guelph Street,
8	the timing for changes to the adjacent streets and compliance with dark
8	sky policies.
8	Moved by Councillor Jennifer Adams, Seconded by Councillor Lisa
8	MacDonald.
8	THAT the Council of the Township of Centre Wellington approve the
8	funding strategy for the Service Financing Commitment Agreement with
8	Westbow Fergus GP Ltd and Salem Development Company for the
8	detailed engineering design for the extension of municipal services to the
8	South Fergus Lands as outlined in Report No. IS2026-12 dated April 13,
8	2026.
8	CARRIED
