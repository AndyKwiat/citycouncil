2	7. Consideration of Reports
2	7.1 New Township Operations Centre Update - Grant Funding Opportunities
2	and Class C Construction Cost Estimate
2	Report from B. Buehler, Operations Centre Project Manager
2	Brandon Buehler, Operations Centre Project Manager provided an
2	overview of the report, indicating the purpose of the report is to:
2	 provide a further update on grant funding opportunities for the
2	new Township Operations Centre;
3	 Seek Council approval to submit an application for grant funding
3	from the FCM – Municipal Fleet Electrification Grant Funds for
3	the project; and,
3	 present the “Class C” construction cost estimate for the project
3	He explained the Project Team has investigated grant funding
3	opportunities from the Federation of Canadian Municipalities (FCM), the
3	new Sustainable Municipal and Community Buildings Fund and the
3	Municipal Fleet Electrification Grant Fund. He further explained the
3	eligibility requirements and indicated this project would not meet the
3	criteria for the Sustainable Municipal and Community Buildings Fund.
3	B. Buehler provided additional details on the Municipal Fleet
3	Electrification Grant Fund and indicated the project team will continue to
3	explore this avenue as well as other funding and third-party partnerships
3	that support complementary aspects of the project.
3	Staff responded to questions regarding collaboration with the Climate
3	Change Coordinator, investigation of hybrid heating/cooling
3	opportunities, options for solar initiatives and solar storage and
3	submission timelines.
3	Moved by Councillor Bronwynne Wilton, Seconded by Councillor
3	Barbara Lustgarten-Evoy.
3	THAT the Council of the Township of Centre Wellington endorses and
3	directs staff to submit an application for grant funding from the
3	Federation of Canadian Municipalities (FCM) – Municipal Fleet
3	Electrification Fund;
3	AND THAT the Council of the Township of Centre Wellington receives
3	for information the updated “Class C” construction cost estimate for
3	construction of the new Township Operations Centre.
3	CARRIED
