7	AND THAT Council directs staff to reinstall the banners at the Jefferson

7	8.10 Belwood Child Care and Recreation Feasibility Report
7	Report from D. Wilson, Chief Administrative Officer
7	D. Wilson, Chief Administrative Officer provided an overview of the
7	report, including the child care shortfalls, discussions with possible
7	community partners, child care in Belwood, and the recommended
7	approach and next steps.
7	Councillor Wilton expressed an interest in encouraging County Council
8	to establish more child care facilities.
8	Mayor Watters called for a recess to confer with staff, the meeting
8	resumed at 3:41 pm.
8	Mayor Watters indicated Councillor Wilton’s suggestion has been
8	included in the staff recommendation for Council’s consideration.
8	Moved by Councillor Bronwynne Wilton, Seconded by Councillor Lisa
8	MacDonald.
8	THAT the Council of the Township of Centre Wellington receives report
8	CAO2024-11 for information;
8	AND THAT the Council of the Township of Centre Wellington supports a
8	collaborative approach to child care planning, funding, administration,
8	and operations lead by Wellington County with the Township playing a
8	supportive role as outlined in this report;
8	AND THAT this report and approved recommendations be forwarded to
8	Wellington County Council and relevant staff for their information to
8	stress the importance of the County’s ongoing leadership and
8	involvement in providing child care services in Centre Wellington, as
8	well as the Township’s offer of support;
8	AND THAT Council defer decisions on future outdoor recreation needs
8	in Belwood until the completion of the ongoing Parks and Recreation
8	Plan, which will provide a comprehensive assessment of the needs in
8	Centre Wellington.
8	AND THAT Council directs staff to send a letter to the County of
8	Wellington encouraging County Council to consider investigating
8	building child care facilities.
8	CARRIED, as amended
