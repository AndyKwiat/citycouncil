2	7. Matters Arising from Closed Session
2	7.1 Mayor Watters indicated a closed session was held and wished to make
2	the following announcement by reading the following press release:
2	Township Acquires 7334 Middlebrook Road for Future Water
2	Supply
2	CENTRE WELLINGTON – Council directed staff to execute an
2	Agreement of Purchase and Sale between the Township and Triton
2	Water Canada Holdings, Inc. to purchase the lands known municipally
2	as 7334 Middlebrook Road as a future water supply source.
2	"Securing this land for a future water supply is a critical step toward
2	ensuring the long-term sustainability and resilience of our community,”
2	said Mayor Watters. “I’m proud of the proactive approach taken by both
2	the Council and staff to make this purchase a reality. Their foresight and
2	commitment to planning are essential for protecting the future of our
2	residents."
2	The Fergus and Elora Water Supply System currently includes six wells
2	in Fergus and three wells in Elora. According to the Township's 2019
2	Water Supply Master Plan (WSMP), additional water supply capacity is
2	required to support future growth in the Township. From a water supply
2	standpoint, the Middlebrook Road site would become the third new
2	water supply location (following new water supply wells in Areas three
2	and seven) to meet the Township's growth needs.
2	The Middlebrook well was constructed in 1984. It operates under
2	artesian conditions, with a static water level of 14 metres above ground
3	surface at the time of construction. The on-site buildings have been
3	demolished at the 5.5-acre site, except for the concrete foundations and
3	a small wellhouse that contains the Middlebrook well.
