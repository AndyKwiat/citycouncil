4	8.2 Heritage Reserve Fund Request - Heritage Intern Contract Position
4	Report from M. Iglesias, Manager of Planning Services
4	M. Iglesias, Manager of Planning Services, provided an overview of the
4	report, indicating due to recent changes to the Ontario Heritage Act,
5	through Bill 23, municipalities will be losing the ability to protect
5	properties from demolition or inappropriate alteration which is currently
5	afforded to “listed” properties on the Municipal Registry. She indicated
5	moving forward, municipalities will have two years after adding a
5	property to the Registry to designate or remove the property from the
5	list. The Township currently has 1,052 properties on the Municipal
5	Registry, 121 designated and over 900 listed.
5	Staff is requesting the hiring of a Heritage Intern to assist within
5	increasing the number of designations this year. The intern will cost
5	approximately $15,000 for a four month term and costs associated with
5	administration (newspaper notices and legal fees) will be approximately
5	$18,000-$20,000.
5	M. Iglesias responded to questions pertaining to the source of funding,
5	and the preservation of the information currently on the list.
5	Moved by Councillor Bronwynne Wilton, Seconded by Councillor
5	Barbara Lustgarten-Evoy.
5	THAT the Council of the Township of Centre Wellington receives staff
5	report PLN2024-16 for information;
5	AND THAT the Council of the Township of Centre Wellington authorizes
5	the use of the Heritage Reserve Fund for a Heritage Intern contract
5	position and associated administrative costs to assist with a specific
5	heritage project, as outlined in staff report PLN2024-16.
5	CARRIED

6	8.5 Termite Management Services Update
6	Report from A. McNabb, Managing Director of Corporate Services &
6	Treasurer
6	A. McNabb, Managing Director of Corporate Services & Treasurer
6	provided an overview of the report, indicating staff is recommending
6	engaging in discussions with a known supplier licensed to utilize the
6	Sentricon product in Canada. He noted the purpose of supplier
6	engagement would be to determine vendor’s willingness to participate in
6	a termite management program with the Township, potential scope of
6	service provision, preferred pricing options, or other opportunities to
6	effectively address termites in Centre Wellington.
6	Adam McNabb responded to questions regarding the rationale for
6	another survey, and the estimated timeline for the project.
6	Moved by Councillor Denis Craddock, Seconded by Councillor
6	Bronwynne Wilton.
6	THAT the Council of the Township of Centre Wellington receive report
6	COR2024-23 for information;
6	AND THAT Council direct staff to enter discussions with Aetna Pest
6	Control to determine potential scope of service provision, preferred
6	pricing options, or other opportunities to effectively address termites in
6	Centre Wellington;
6	AND FURTHER THAT Council provide staff the option of conducting a
6	survey of property owners identified in the 2020 study to determine
6	ongoing termite management program viability based on discussion with
7	the pest control vendor.
7	CARRIED
