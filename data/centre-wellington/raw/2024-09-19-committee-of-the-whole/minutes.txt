2	Downtown Parking Strategy Update
2	Report from D. Wilson, Chief Administrative Officer
2	Dan Wilson, Chief Administrative Officer provided an overview of the
2	report, including background on the development of the strategy. He
2	indicated staff is seeking approval for partial implementation of the
2	parking strategy as outlined in the report for 2025, subject to budget
2	approval. He outlined the current parking enforcement strategy, the
2	downtown shuttle service and future consideration for paid parking pilots
2	to fund future parking supply initiatives.
2	D. Wilson responded to questions regarding expansion of the shuttle
2	bus service and associated sponsorship, public engagement initiatives,
2	and a suggested pause on the improvements to the Bissell Park parking
2	lot.
2	Moved by Councillor Jennifer Adams, Seconded by Councillor Denis
2	Craddock.
2	THAT the Council of the Township of Centre Wellington approve the
2	Draft Municipal Downtown Parking Strategy Final Report.
2	AND THAT the Council of the Township of Centre Wellington approve
2	the 2025 Parking Implementation Plan, subject to 2025 Budget
2	approval.
2	AND THAT the Council of the Township of Centre Wellington delegate
2	authority to the Managing Director of Corporate Services & Treasurer to
2	sign contracts/agreements in relation to the 2025 Parking
2	Implementation Plan.
2	CARRIED

3	2025 Preliminary Draft Capital Budget Report
3	Report from A. McNabb, Managing Director of Corporate Services &
3	Treasurer
3	Presentation
3	Adam McNabb, Managing Director of Corporate Services & Treasurer
3	introduced the 2025 preliminary draft capital budget. He outlined the
3	topics planned to discuss at the November 5th budget meeting and
3	indicated the 2025 draft budget book is anticipated to be made available
3	by November 22, 2024. A. McNabb noted the Operations Centre and
3	Bissell Park projects are not included at this time.
3	Mark Bradey, Manager of Finance & Deputy Treasurer provided an
3	overview of the 2025 draft capital budget, focussing on various projects
3	such as the Belwood Hall, Farley Neighbourhood Park, Colborne Street
3	Reconstruction, Fourth Line Bridge 3-WG, 495 Union Street West
3	Renovation, and the Water Meter Modernization/Replacement.
3	Staff responded to questions regarding the various studies including the
3	flow of information between the advisory committees and Council,
3	inclusion of shade features in neighbourhood parks, urbanization of
3	Colborne Street John Street to Wilson Crescent, funding of the Fourth
3	Line Bridge, and the future uses proposed for 495 Union Street.
3	Staff provided details regarding the Fergus WWTP Administration
3	Building Expansion and the water metre modernization replacement
3	project.
3	Mayor Watters called for a 10 minute recess, with the meeting resuming
3	at 11:10 am.
3	A. McNabb, Managing Director of Corporate Services and Treasurer
3	reviewed the various items forming the 2025 capital budget.
3	Staff responded to questions regarding the implementation of the
3	municipal accommodation tax, the scope of the public engagement
3	strategy, the development charges study, the extent of the tourism
3	building renovations, work planned in Belsyde Cemetery, downtown
3	Fergus streetlight replacements, the scope of the Jefferson Elora
3	Community Centre parking lot project, signage at the Pierpoint Nature
3	Reserve, status of the Victoria Park Elora stair replacement project,
3	status of the Confederation Park upgrades, timing for the Churchill
3	Crescent playground, and policies pertaining to donations or
3	private/public partnerships.
4	Moved by Councillor Jennifer Adams, Seconded by Councillor
4	Bronwynne Wilton.
4	THAT the Council of the Township of Centre Wellington direct staff to
4	include the 2025 Preliminary Draft Capital Budget as outlined in report
4	COR2024-51 dated September 19, 2024, in the 2025 Draft Budget.
4	CARRIED
