3	9.2 Sarah Bowers-Peter Crime Stoppers Guelph Wellington
3	Sarah Bowers-Peter indicated Crime Stoppers is celebrating their 35th
3	anniversary. She noted Crime Stoppers is an anonymous, confidential
3	method of reporting crime, there are cash rewards if arrests are made and
3	they rely on fundraising and donations from individuals, businesses and
3	organizations to pay rewards and operate the program.
3	She highlighted statistics such as total number of tips received 23,108,
3	resulting arrests 1,596, cases cleared 2,307, charges laid 4,506 and the
3	value of the property recovered $10,398,366.
3	9.3 County Council Report - Mary Lloyd, County Councillor Ward 5
3	County Councillor Mary Lloyd highlighted the recent construction at
3	Carroll Creek, ongoing activities at the various County Libraries, the
3	successful Centre Wellington shuttle, the upcoming antique car show at
3	the Wellington Museum, the new works garage in Arthur, and the new
3	library in Erin. She noted the recent opening of Grace Gardens,
3	transitional emergency housing, for the most vulnerable and responded
3	to questions regarding the delivery of these services.

4	11.2 Procurement Policy
4	Report from A. McNabb, Managing Director of Corporate Services &
4	Treasurer
4	Adam McNabb, Managing Director of Corporate Services & Treasurer
4	provided an overview of the report and responded to questions regarding
4	efforts to streamline the procurement process and the inclusion of
4	processes to encourage flexibility.
4	Councillor Adams indicated support for improving efficiency and removing
4	barriers, but expressed a desire to maintain the signing authority of the
4	Mayor and Clerk for contracts over $2 million and wished to put forward
4	an amendment to that effect.
4	Moved by Councillor Jennifer Adams, Seconded by Councillor Lisa
4	MacDonald.
4	THAT the Council of the Township of Centre Wellington endorse the
4	Procurement Policy attached to Report COR2023-72 as amended: by
4	striking out the upper signing authority limit of the CAO and Managing
4	Director of Corporate Services and Treasurer to $2 million and inserting
4	an additional row below which would maintain the Mayor and Clerk with
4	the authority to execute contracts with a value of $2 million and over in
4	Section 5.3, Table 6 of the Policy;
4	AND THAT the Procurement Policy, as amended be enacted by by-law
4	with immediate effect.
4	CARRIED
