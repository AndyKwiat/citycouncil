16	14. Notice of Motions
16	15. Motions (where Notice had been given)
16	15.1 Notice of Motion - Councillor Wilton - Data Centre 08-24-26
16	Moved by Councillor Bronwynne Wilton, Seconded by Councillor
16	Kimberley Jefferson.
16	Given the rapid growth of AI-driven systems, cloud computing and digital
16	infrastructure has accelerated the development of new and large-scale
16	data centres to meet industry defined needs and the fact that the Province
16	of Ontario has not yet established a provincial regulatory framework for
16	AI data centre industry there is cause to exercise caution regarding the
16	introduction of data centres within the Township of Centre Wellington.
16	1. THEREFORE the Council of the Township of Centre Wellington
16	directs staff to undertake a review and study costing no more than
16	$49,999 of the Township’s land use planning policies in relation to
16	artificial intelligence (AI) data centres, pursuant to section 38 of the
16	Planning Act, applicable to all zones where the use is permitted in
16	the Township of Centre Wellington, including, but not limited to, its
16	Official Plans and Zoning By-law, and any required changes to the
16	Township’s land use planning policies, mapping, and regulatory
16	framework with consideration of, but not limited to the following:
16	a. Existing Official Plan policies and Zoning By-law
16	permissions and regulations;
16	b. Energy use and the impacts to power grids,
16	c. Water use and the impacts to long term drinking water
16	quality and quantity, and
16	d. Noise and light pollution impacts,
16	2. THAT staff be directed to report back to Council on the items listed
16	above (a) in Q3 of 2027.
16	3. THAT staff be directed to prepare for Council’s consideration at the
16	Council meeting on September 28, 2026, an Interim Control By-
16	Law under Section 38 of the Planning Act, prohibiting the use of AI
16	data centres within the study area described in (a) above.
16	Councillor Jennifer Adams requested a recorded vote for this item.
