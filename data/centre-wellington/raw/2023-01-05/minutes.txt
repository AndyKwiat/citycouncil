2	5. Presentations and Announcements
2	5.1 2023 Budget Overview
2	Presentation by D. Wilson, Chief Administrative Officer
2	D. Wilson, Chief Administrative Officer welcomed everyone to Council’s
2	first budget meeting. He indicated the agenda is divided into 3 categories;
2	Budget Orientation/Introduction, Budget Policy Approvals and Project
2	Pre-Approvals. D. Wilson outlined the 2023 budget pressures, including
2	inflation, assessment growth, insurance premiums, utility and gas prices,
2	payroll costs, and Bill 23. He noted cumulatively, the Township owns,
2	operates and maintains assets with replacement values in excess of $1
2	billion dollars.
2	D. Wilson highlighted the state of Township assets, the asset
2	management strategy – risk management, factors impacting levels of
2	service, tax supported capital funding and a summary of capital
2	investment for tax supported assets, excluding water and wastewater
2	assets.

5	7.2 OLG Funding Allocation Policy
5	Report from A. McNabb, Managing Director of Corporate Services &
5	Treasurer
5	A. McNabb, Managing Director of Corporate Services & Treasurer
5	provided an overview, outlining the revisions to the allocations. A.
5	McNabb indicated staff is recommending the Arts, Culture & Heritage
5	category be split into a category for Community Impact Grants and a
5	category for Heritage. The anticipated funding for 2023 is as follows:
5	Community Impact (added to existing grant process) $108,000, Heritage
5	$81,000. The proposed allocation provides a total of 7% OLG allocated
5	to Community Impact Grants and Heritage combined, up from previous
5	annual allocations of 5%.
5	Staff responded to questions and comments associated with the removal
5	of the words “Arts and Culture”, the increase of the allocation to 90% to
5	support significant projects, and provided an explanation that additional
5	details on the Community Impact Grant and use of the allocated funds will
5	be brought forward at a future meeting.
5	Moved by Councillor Lisa MacDonald, Seconded by Councillor Barbara
5	Lustgarten-Evoy.
5	THAT the Council of the Township of Centre Wellington authorize the
5	Mayor and Clerk to sign a By-Law to amend the OLG Funding Allocation
5	Policy;
5	AND THAT the Council of the Township of Centre Wellington direct staff
5	to use the amended policy in the 2023 Budget, as outlined in report
5	COR2023-06 dated January 5, 2023, with the policy to be reviewed as
5	part of the 2024 Budget process.
5	CARRIED
