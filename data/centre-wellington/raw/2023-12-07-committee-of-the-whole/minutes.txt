5	8.1 Fergus Business Improvement Area (BIA) Budget Presentation
5	Councillor Jefferson, having declared a conflict of interest with respect to
5	the Fergus BIA, left the meeting.
5	Fred Gordon, Fergus BIA Administrator thanked and recognized Deputy
5	Mayor Wilton, Township staff Kristen Drexler, Kerri O’Kane, Mark
5	Bradey and others from Public Works and CW Hydro for their help and
5	assistance. F. Gordon outlined the various exciting and successful
5	events, including movie night, Tartan Day, Medieval Fair and many
5	others. He outlined the various beautification projects, including the
5	Provost pavilion, flowers, rotating flag program and public sculptures.
5	F. Gordon noted the importance of weekly garbage collection and the
5	significant costs associated with private garbage collection. The BIA is
5	grateful for the Township’s financial assistance and is committed to
5	working with both the Township and the County of Wellington to find a
5	permanent, cost-effective solution to garbage in the downtown.
5	F. Gordon indicated to continue to expand events, programs and
5	beautification projects, there will be a 4% increase to the levy.
5	Councillor Jefferson returned to the meeting.
5	8.2 Elora Business Improvement (BIA) Area Budget Presentation
5	MacLean Hann, Elora BIA Chair echoed the comments of Fred Gordon
5	and expressed his gratitude on behalf of the Elora BIA for the hard work
5	of Councillors and staff, making all efforts very worthwhile. He provided
5	an overview of the food events, including, Eloralisious, Elora Burger
5	Battle, and Food Day Canada. He highlighted other events and activities
5	such as Starlight Shopping, Candlelight Elora, and purchasing more
5	lights to brighten the streets.
5	M. Hann noted the burden of the garbage costs and missed pick ups.
5	He also noted the main focus for the BIA in 2024 will be the updating of
5	the Christmas lights for the hydro posts which is the rational for the
5	$50,000 loan from the Township.
