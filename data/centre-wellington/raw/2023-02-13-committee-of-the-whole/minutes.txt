2	5. Public Meetings to Consider Zoning Amendments
2	5.1 RZ016/22 - Zoning By-law Amendment re: 7197 Wellington Road 21,
2	Pilkington
2	Deputy Mayor Jefferson indicated the purpose of the public meeting is to
2	present and receive public input regarding a proposed zoning by-law
2	amendment on subject lands known as 7197 Wellington Road 21, former
2	Township of Pilkington.
2	She explained the zoning by-law amendment proposes to change the
2	zoning of the subject land from “A” Agriculture and “EP” Environmental
2	Protection to “A” Agriculture Exception and “EP” Environmental
2	Protection. The effect of the proposed Zoning By-law Amendment is to
2	implement zoning regulations necessary to facilitate a farm dwelling
2	surplus severance by restricting residential uses on the retained parcel.
2	The zoning amendment is a condition of approval of the County of
2	Wellington Land Division Committee Application B92-22.
2	Deputy Mayor Jefferson stated if a person or public body does not make
2	oral submissions at the public meeting, or make written submissions to
2	the Township of Centre Wellington before the by-law is passed, the
2	person or public body is not entitled to appeal the decision of the
2	Township of Centre Wellington to the Ontario Land Tribunal (OLT).
2	She further noted if a person or public body does not make oral
2	submission at the public meeting, or make written submissions to the
2	Township of Centre Wellington before the by-law is passed, the person
2	or public body may not be added as the party to the hearing of an appeal
2	before the Ontario Land Tribunal unless, in the opinion of the Tribunal,
2	there are reasonable grounds to do so.
2	She indicated if anyone wishes to be notified of the adoption of the
2	proposed zoning by-law amendment, a written request must be filed with
2	the Municipal Clerk, Township of Centre Wellington, 1 MacDonald
2	Square, Elora, ON N0B 1S0.
2	Deputy Mayor Jefferson asked the Deputy Municipal Clerk to indicate how
2	notice of the public meeting was given. The Deputy Municipal Clerk
2	indicated notice was mailed to prescribed persons, agencies and public
2	bodies on or before December 22, 2022 and a copy of the notice was
2	placed in the Wellington Advertiser on December 22, 2022.
2	The Deputy Municipal Clerk confirmed the requirement for giving notice
2	was completed on December 22, 2022.
3	Deputy Mayor Jefferson called on Mariana Iglesias, Senior Planner, to
3	introduce the application.
3	M. Iglesias indicated the rezoning application is for the property located
3	at 7197 Wellington Road 21, on the west side of Sixth Line East, in the
3	former Township of Pilkington. The subject land currently supports an
3	active farming operation and residential dwelling.
3	She explained the rezoning application seeks to rezone the 26 ha retained
3	farm parcel from Agricultural and Environmental Protection to add an
3	exception to the Agricultural zoning to prohibit residential uses as required
3	by Provincial and County policies for farm dwelling surplus resulting in no
3	new dwelling being constructed on the farm land. The severed parcel with
3	the dwelling would otherwise meet all the applicable zoning regulations
3	for size and setbacks. The associated severance was conditionally
3	approved by the County of Wellington through consent application
3	B92/22.
3	She further indicated no oral or written submissions were received from
3	the public to date and the County of Wellington and Grand River
3	Conservation Authority commented on the application with no objections
3	to the rezoning.
3	Deputy Mayor Jefferson called on Jeff Buisman, VanHarten Surveying,
3	agent for the applicant, who indicated this is a surplus farm severance
3	with one unique element for an easement for the existing gas line.
3	Mike Nagy, resident confirmed this rezoning application will not permit a
3	new subdivision and confirmed no change to the environmental
3	protection.
3	Deputy Mayor Jefferson seeing no further delegations and no questions
3	from Council declared the meeting adjourned.
3	5.2 RZ018/22 Zoning By-law Amendment re: 7158 Third Line, West
3	Garafraxa
3	Deputy Mayor Jefferson indicated the purpose of this public meeting is to
3	present and receive public input regarding a proposed zoning by-law
3	amendment on subject lands known as 7158 Third Line, former Township
3	of West Garafraxa.
3	She explained the purpose of the proposed Zoning By-law Amendment is
3	to change the current zoning “A” Agriculture to “A” Agriculture Exception,
3	and to permit a reduced rear yard setback from the existing barn on the
3	severed parcel to be 5.0 m instead of the required 18 m. The effect of the
4	proposed Zoning By-law Amendment is to implement zoning regulations
4	necessary to facilitate a farm dwelling surplus severance by restricting
4	residential uses on the retained parcel. The zoning amendment is a
4	condition of approval of the County of Wellington Land Division
4	Committee Application B74-22.
4	Deputy Mayor Jefferson stated if a person or public body does not make
4	oral submissions at the public meeting, or make written submissions to
4	the Township of Centre Wellington before the by-law is passed, the
4	person or public body is not entitled to appeal the decision of the
4	Township of Centre Wellington to the Ontario Land Tribunal (OLT).
4	She further noted if a person or public body does not make oral
4	submission at the public meeting, or make written submissions to the
4	Township of Centre Wellington before the by-law is passed, the person
4	or public body may not be added as the party to the hearing of an appeal
4	before the Ontario Land Tribunal unless, in the opinion of the Tribunal,
4	there are reasonable grounds to do so.
4	She indicated if anyone wishes to be notified of the adoption of the
4	proposed zoning by-law amendment, a written request must be filed with
4	the Municipal Clerk, Township of Centre Wellington, 1 MacDonald
4	Square, Elora, ON N0B 1S0.
4	Deputy Mayor Jefferson asked the Deputy Municipal Clerk to indicate how
4	notice of the public meeting was given. The Deputy Municipal Clerk
4	indicated notice was mailed to prescribed persons, agencies and public
4	bodies on or before December 22, 2022 and a copy of the notice was
4	placed in the Wellington Advertiser on December 22, 2022.
4	The Deputy Municipal Clerk confirmed the requirement for giving notice
4	was completed on December 22, 2022.
4	Deputy Mayor Jefferson called on Mariana Iglesias, Senior Planner to
4	introduce the application.
4	Mariana Iglesias, Senior Planner outlined the rezoning application for the
4	property located at 7158 Third Line, east of Sideroad 25, in the former
4	Township of West Garafraxa. She indicated the property currently
4	supports an active farming operation and residential dwelling.
4	She explained the rezoning application seeks to rezone the 33 ha retained
4	farm parcel from Agricultural to add an exception to prohibit residential
4	uses as required by Provincial and County policies for farm dwelling
4	surplus severances that are related to farm consolidation, so a new
5	dwelling cannot be constructed on the farm land. The severed parcel with
5	the dwelling and barn also requires an exception to the zoning to
5	recognize a reduced rear yard setback for the barn. The parcel will
5	otherwise meet all the applicable zoning regulations.
5	She further indicated no oral or written submissions were received from
5	the public for this application to date and the County of Wellington and
5	Grand River Conservation Authority commented on the application with
5	no objections to the rezoning.
5	Deputy Mayor Jefferson called on Jeff Buisman, VanHarten Surveying.
5	Jeff Buisman, VanHarten Surveying, agent for the applicant, indicated
5	there is currently an argi business operating on the retained parcel of land
5	and the intention is the business will continue on the retained parcel.
5	Seeing no delegations, Deputy Mayor Jefferson asked if there are any
5	questions from Council.
5	In response to questions from Council, staff indicated a nutrient
5	management plan has been submitted and clarified the business is an
5	existing business not a new use.
5	Deputy Mayor Jefferson declared the meeting adjourned.
