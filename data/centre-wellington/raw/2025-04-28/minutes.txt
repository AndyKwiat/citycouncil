3	9. Delegations
3	9.1 Marko Cirovic, Director of Sector Engagement, IESO re: item 11.1
3	Information Report - RZ05/24, 6235 Guelph Street, Nichol (Aypa Power
3	Battery Energy Storage System Facility)
3	M. Cirovic indicated the IESO aims to provide a fact-based view of the
3	province’s growing need for electricity and the important role securing
3	new electricity infrastructure plays in ensuring Ontario continues to have
3	a reliable, affordable and sustainable electricity system today and in the
3	future. He indicated the IESO maintains reliability by overseeing the
3	operation of the grid in real time – 24 hours a day, 7 days a week and
3	conducts ongoing planning and procurement processes to ensure
3	Ontario’s electricity system has the infrastructure and tools in place to
3	meet our needs 20-years into the future. M. Cirovic stated the demand
3	for electricity in Ontario is expected to increase 75 per cent by 2050.
3	M. Cirovic responded to questions regarding the demand for electricity
3	in Ontario, potential to facilitate the installation of home car chargers, the
3	Independent Electricity System Operator’s (IESO) Long-Term One (LT1)
3	procurement process, and the extent of the incentives and penalties.
3	9.2 Andrew Thiele, Energy Storage Canada re: item 11.1 Information Report
3	- RZ05/24, 6235 Guelph Street, Nichol (Aypa Power Battery Energy
3	Storage System Facility)
3	A. Thiele indicated Energy Storage Canada is a non profit organization
3	and a national trade association. They represent energy storage
3	developers from coast to coast to ensure a greener more affordable
3	grid. They are supportive of the Aypa Power Battery Energy Storage
3	System project advancing energy storages solutions, providing flexible
3	energy and reducing peak demand costs.

4	6235 Guelph Street, Nichol (Aypa Power Battery Energy Storage
4	System Facility) appeared in support of the proposal. Canadian Solar
