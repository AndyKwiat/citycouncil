1	5. Public Meeting to Consider A Zoning Amendment
1	5.1 Zoning Amendment Application (RZ005-2025), for lands known
2	municipally as 6949 Wellington Rd 7, Nichol.
2	For Information regarding the Application see our website
2	Mayor Watters indicated the purpose of this public meeting is to present
2	and receive public input regarding a proposed zoning by-law
2	amendment on the subject land known as 6949 Wellington Road 7, in
2	the former Township of Nichol.
2	He noted the purpose of the proposed zoning by-law amendment is to
2	change the zoning from “A” Agricultural and “EP” Environment
2	Protection to “A” Agricultural Exception and “EP” Environment
2	Protection.
2	The effect of the proposed zoning by-law amendment is to implement
2	zoning regulations necessary to establish a shop for the repair and
2	maintenance of farm drainage equipment as an agriculture-related use.
2	Mayor Watters indicated if a person or public body would otherwise
2	have an ability to appeal the decision of the Township of Centre
2	Wellington to the Ontario Land Tribunal (OLT) but the person or public
2	body does not make oral submissions at a public meeting or make
2	written submissions to the Township of Centre Wellington before the by-
2	law is passed, the person or public body is not entitled to appeal the
2	decision.
2	Mayor Watters stated if a person or public body does not make oral
2	submission at the public meeting, or make written submissions to the
2	Township of Centre Wellington before the by-law is passed, the person
2	or public body may not be added as the party to the hearing of an
2	appeal before the OLT unless, in the opinion of the Tribunal, there are
2	reasonable grounds to do so.
2	Mayor Watters advised if anyone wishes to be notified of the adoption of
2	the proposed zoning by-law amendment, a written request must be filed
2	with the Municipal Clerk, Township of Centre Wellington, 1 MacDonald
2	Square, Elora, ON N0B 1S0.
2	The Municipal Clerk advised notice was mailed to prescribed persons,
2	agencies and public bodies on or before July 10, 2025. She indicated a
2	copy of the notice was advertised in the Wellington Advertiser on July
2	10, 2025, and confirmed the requirement for giving notice was
2	completed on July 10, 2025.
2	Mayor Watters called on Deanna Maiden, Senior Development Planner,
2	to introduce the application.
3	Deanna Maiden, Senior Development Planner indicated provided an
3	overview of the proposal, indicating the application would implement
3	zoning regulations necessary to establish a shop for the repair and
3	maintenance of farm drainage equipment as an agriculture-related use..
3	She noted the subject property is a rural agricultural property located on
3	the east side of Wellington County Road 7, in the northwesterly part of
3	the Township, just southeast of the Hamlet of Alma. The subject lands
3	are approximately 4.5 hectares in size with 455 metres of frontage along
3	Highway 7, with a single detached dwelling and accessory buildings that
3	support a livestock operation
3	Mayor Watters called on Rob Stovel to make a presentation on the
3	application.
3	Rob Stovel, Stovel and Associates introduced the landowner, the
3	applicant and the consulting team and outlined the scope of the
3	proposal to establish a farm-related business. R. Stovel noted it is not a
3	commercial shop, the building will be used to store and maintain large
3	equipment and an office. He also noted the fuel onsite is for the use of
3	the business only and not a fuel storage facility.
3	R. Stovel noted the remaining lands, including the house and out-
3	buildings will be retained except for a barn that will be removed and will
3	continue to be used for agriculture. He suggested the proposal is
3	consistent with the Provincial Policy Statement, conforms to the
3	County’s official plan and supports the agricultural community.
3	Mayor Watters invited Heather Smith, 31 Queen Street South, Alma,
3	Ontario to speak as a delegation. She expressed concerns and posed
3	questions regarding measures to be taken given Alma falls within the
3	Wellhead Protection Area for Quantity (WHPA-Q) for the Township of
3	Centre Wellington and the fuel station.
3	Rob Stovel indicated he will prepare a formal response to the matters
3	raised.
3	Mayor Watters asked if anyone present wishes to speak to the
3	application., there being none, he offered Council an opportunity to ask
3	any questions of the applicant or staff.
3	Staff and the applicant responded to questions regarding the proposed
3	agricultural use for the balance of the subject lands, the extent of the
3	circulation of the application, and the conditional offer to purchase the
3	lands.
3	Mayor Watters thanked everyone for attending and advised Council will
4	be considering the by-law at a future Council meeting.
4	Mayor Watters declared the public meeting adjourned and called for a 2
4	minute recess.

8	9.3 Proposed Agreement: Communication Infrastructure Enhancement
8	Report from Dan Wilson, Chief Administrative Officer
8	Dan Wilson, Chief Administrative Officer provided an overview of the
8	report, indicating The Grand 101 FM has approached Township staff,
8	requesting assistance with funding the purchase and installation of a
8	backup generator. He noted the agreement would allow the Township to
8	secure dedicated airtime at a preferred fixed rate on The Grand 101 FM
8	by funding the backup generator purchase and installation with an
8	upfront investment of $90,230.50. Over ten years, The Grand 101 FM
8	would repay $116,852.63 (principal amount plus 5% annual interest)
8	through airtime, ensuring cost certainty and reliable emergency
8	communications. This initiative supports public safety, civic
8	engagement, and sustainable governance, with annual repayments
8	drawn from the Office of the CAO and Communications cost centre
8	budget annually.
8	Moved by Councillor Bronwynne Wilton, Seconded by Councillor
8	Barbara Lustgarten-Evoy.
8	THAT the Council of the Township of Centre Wellington delegate
8	authority to the CAO to execute an agreement between the Township of
8	Centre Wellington and The Grand 101 FM, for the purposes of providing
8	funds to be used for the purchase and installation of a generator at 7447
8	Wellington Road 18, Elora for The Grand 101 FM’s radio transmitter, in
8	exchange for secure dedicated airtime.
8	CARRIED
