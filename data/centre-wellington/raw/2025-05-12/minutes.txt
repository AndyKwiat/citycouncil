2	8. Public Meetings to Consider Zoning Amendments
2	8.1 RZ003-2025 - 7735 Sixth Line, Nichol
2	Mayor Watters indicated the purpose of this public meeting is to present
2	and receive public input regarding a proposed zoning by-law
2	amendment on the subject land known as 7735 Sixth Line in the former
2	Township of Nichol.
3	He noted the purpose of the proposed zoning by-law amendment is to
3	change the zoning from “A” Agricultural and “EP” Environment
3	Protection to “A” Agricultural Exception and “EP” Environment Protection
3	on the retained parcel to restrict residential uses.
3	The effect of the proposed zoning by-law amendment is to implement
3	zoning regulations necessary to facilitate a surplus farm dwelling
3	severance. The zoning amendment is a condition of approval of the
3	County of Wellington Land Division Committee Application B108-24.
3	Mayor Watters indicated if a person or public body would otherwise
3	have an ability to appeal the decision of the Township of Centre
3	Wellington to the Ontario Land Tribunal (OLT) but the person or public
3	body does not make oral submissions at a public meeting or make
3	written submissions to the Township of Centre Wellington before the by-
3	law is passed, the person or public body is not entitled to appeal the
3	decision.
3	Mayor Watters stated if a person or public body does not make oral
3	submission at the public meeting, or make written submissions to the
3	Township of Centre Wellington before the by-law is passed, the person
3	or public body may not be added as the party to the hearing of an
3	appeal before the OLT unless, in the opinion of the Tribunal, there are
3	reasonable grounds to do so.
3	Mayor Watters advised if anyone wishes to be notified of the adoption of
3	the proposed zoning by-law amendment, a written request must be filed
3	with the Municipal Clerk, Township of Centre Wellington, 1 MacDonald
3	Square, Elora, ON, N0B 1S0.
3	The Municipal Clerk advised notice was mailed to prescribed persons,
3	agencies and public bodies on or before April 17, 2025. She indicated a
3	copy of the notice was advertised in the Wellington Advertiser on April
3	17, 2025 and confirmed the requirement for giving notice was completed
3	on April 17, 2025.
3	Mayor Watters called on Deanna Maiden, Senior Development Planner,
3	to introduce the application, indicating the application is to prohibit
3	residential uses to facilitate a farm surplus dwelling.
3	Mayor Watters called on Hailey Keast, Planning Technician from Van
3	Harten Surveying to provide an overview of the application.
3	Mayor Watters indicated no delegations have registered and asked if
4	there is anyone present wishing to speak to the application.
4	There being none, Mayor Watters offered Council an opportunity to ask
4	questions of the applicant or staff.
4	There being no questions, Mayor Watters thanked everyone for
4	attending and advised Council will be considering the by-law at a future
4	Council meeting.
4	Mayor Watters declared the public meeting adjourned.

5	Report from Pat Newson, Managing Director of Community Services

5	McQueen outlined the four ongoing projects; a) Needs Assessment, b)
5	Parks Plan, c) Parks and Recreation Plan and d) Feasibility Study. She
5	outlined the project journey from the initial research phase to the
5	deliverables.
5	T. McQueen outlined the next steps in the process, including posting the
5	plans on Connect CW and continuing to receive feedback from
5	members of Council and the advisory committees. She noted the plan to
5	return to Council at the July 14th meeting to share the feedback
5	received and report on any refinements to the plans and seek final
5	adoption.
5	Staff and the consultants responded to questions regarding appropriate
5	service standards for parkland, renewal of existing parks, and plans to
5	incorporate park amenities at the new Operations Centre such as
5	walking tracks and picnics.

5	Lustgarten-Evoy.
