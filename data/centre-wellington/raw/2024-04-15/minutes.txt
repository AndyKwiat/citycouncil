6	8.3 Zoning Amendment RZ015/22 Decision Report - 961 St. David St. N.
6	Report from M. Iglesias, Manager of Planning Services
6	M. Iglesias, Manager of Planning Services indicated the property is a 1.4
6	hectare (3.4 acre) site, on the west side of St. David Street North (Hwy
6	6), mid-way between Sideroad 19 and 18 in the Fergus Urban Centre,
6	currently containing a residential dwelling and outbuildings to be
6	removed. She indicated the property is designated “Residential” in the
6	Official Plan and adjacent to the “Highway Commercial” designation to
6	the north, noting the purpose of the zoning amendment is to change the
6	zoning from R1A residential exception to higher density residential
6	zones, facilitating an infill development of 49 residential units.
6	M. Iglesias noted a public meeting was held on August 30, 2023 with
6	concerns raised by neighbours related to potential traffic, noise, wildlife
6	and vegetation impacts, and compatibility. She noted most of the
6	concerns raised have been addressed through recommendations in the
6	technical studies and will become conditions of draft approval of the plan
6	of condominium. In addition, other matters such as detailed
6	grading/drainage, tree removals, landscaping, fencing and noise
7	mitigation measures will be addressed through a future detailed design.
7	M. Iglesias indicated staff is recommending approval of the zoning
7	amendment to facilitate a 49 unit infill residential development proposal
7	Moved by Councillor Jennifer Adams, Seconded by Councillor
7	Kimberley Jefferson.
7	THAT the Council of the Township of Centre Wellington approves
7	application RZ015/22 and a by-law to amend the zoning by-law be given
7	three readings.
7	CARRIED
7	Councillor Wilton rejoined the meeting.
