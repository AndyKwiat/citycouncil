2	5.2 Wellington Federation of Agriculture - Janet Harrop
2	Re: Agri-Food System Study
2	Following the playing of a short video, Janet Harrop, Past President,
2	distributed hard copies of the Agri-Food System Study and provided an
2	overview of the key findings. She indicated Wellington County accounts
2	for 5.8% of Ontario’s agri-food value chain and covers 0.2% of Ontario’s
2	Land Base.
2	Councillor Lustgarten-Evoy indicated when appropriate, she would like
2	to put forward a motion.
2	Councillor Wilton then declared a conflict of pecuniary interest as her
2	consulting firm authored the report and left the meeting.
2	Mayor Watters expressed the importance of Centre Wellington and the
2	County of Wellington and the role we all play in local and regional food
2	security as well as balancing the need for housing.
2	Moved by Councillor Lustgarten-Evoy
2	Seconded by Councillor Adams
2	THAT the Council of the Township of Centre Wellington receive the
2	Wellington Federation of Agriculture’s Agri-Food Systems Study which
2	underscores the distinct economic, environmental, and social
2	significance of farmland in Centre Wellington for information;
2	AND THAT staff refer to the study during the development of policies
2	and strategies aimed at cultivating sustainable land use and farmland
2	preservation throughout the municipality.
2	Councillors Lustgarten-Evoy and Adams agreed to withdraw the motion
2	to provide an opportunity for additional public consultation.
2	Moved by Councillor MacDonald
2	Seconded by Councillor Lustgarten-Evoy
2	THAT Council defer consideration of the Wellinton County Agri-Food
2	System Study to the next council meeting.
2	CARRIED

4	8. Consideration of Reports
4	8.1 The Elora Rocks Hockey Club Interest Free Loan
4	Report from M. Tucker, Manager of Facilities Operations
4	M. Tucker, Manager of Facilities Operations provided an overview of the
4	report, indicating the Elora Rocks Hockey Club has shared with staff
4	their challenges to pay ice rentals annually each season and the
4	payments associated with an interest free loan of $25,149.20. He
4	explained staff has provided two options for Council’s consideration, one
4	of which includes the club’s request to forgive their past debt and allow
4	them to focus on success moving forward.
4	Staff and R. Wigmore responded to questions regarding the current and
4	past structure of the hockey club, potential community grants available,
5	and the requirement for payment prior to ice allocation.
5	After discussion, the following was put forward:
5	Moved by Councillor Kimberley Jefferson, Seconded by Councillor Lisa
5	MacDonald.
5	THAT the Council of the Township of Centre Wellington defer
5	consideration of the current agreement with the Elora Rocks Hockey
5	Club to staff to outline additional options related to restructuring of the
5	debt with the report to be brought back to Council by the June 10th
5	meeting.
5	CARRIED
