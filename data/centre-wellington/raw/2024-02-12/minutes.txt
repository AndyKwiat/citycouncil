5	8.5 Encroachment Agreement between the Township of Centre Wellington
5	and Kat Florence Canada Company Limited for a Proposed Balcony
5	over the Municipal Sidewalk at 43 West Mill Street in Elora
5	Report from C. Baker, Managing Director of Infrastructure Services
5	C. Baker, Managing Director of Infrastructure Services provided an
5	overview of the report and outlined the number of conditions proposed
5	such as lighting, inspections, insurance and indemnification.
5	Moved by Councillor Denis Craddock, Seconded by Councillor Lisa
5	MacDonald.
5	THAT the Council of the Township of Centre Wellington supports the
5	construction of a balcony on and over Township property related to the
5	redevelopment of the former "Commercial Hotel" building located at 43
5	West Mill Street in Elora as outlined in Report No. IS2024-01;
5	AND THAT Council authorizes the Managing Director of Infrastructure
5	Services to execute an Encroachment Agreement between the
5	Township of Centre Wellington and Kat Florence Canada Company
5	Limited for a balcony structure within the Township's right-of-way,
5	subject to the following conditions:
5	1. That the balcony is designed and constructed to cantilever over the
5	municipal sidewalk; and
5	2. That an unobstructed pedestrian clear zone of 3.25 m metres from
5	the sidewalk surface to the underside of the balcony and 2.00 metres
5	from the facade of the building to the inside face of the vertical columns
5	be maintained at all times;
6	3. That the five vertical columns connected to the sidewalk be designed
6	and constructed in a way that they are non-structural and can be easily
6	removed by the proponent at the Township's request to accommodate
6	future operation and maintenance activities related to the municipal and
6	utilities infrastructure within the right-of-way;
6	4. That the proponent provide adequate lighting within the pedestrian
6	area in accordance with Township standards;
6	5. That appropriate insurance coverage and indemnification be obtained
6	from the proponent in accordance with Township policy; and,
6	6. That the balcony be inspected by a qualified professional at a
6	minimum frequency of once every five years, as recommended by the
6	proponent's heritage consultant, with any recommendations or
6	deficiencies identified in the inspection report addressed within 12
6	months of date of the inspection.
6	CARRIED
6	8.6 Decision Report RZ013/22 - 223 St. Andrew St. E., Fergus
6	Report M. Iglesias, Manager of Planning Services
6	M. Iglesias, Manager of Planning Services provided an overview of the
6	report, noting the property is located on the north side of St. Andrew
6	Street E., just east of Gowrie Street N., in the downtown core of the
6	Fergus Urban Centre. She indicated the applicant is seeking a zoning
6	amendment to allow an increased height of 16.7m to facilitate
6	construction of a 5-storey mixed use building with 163m2 (approx.
6	1,800ft2) commercial use at the ground floor in 2 separate units and 17
6	residential units above with an elevator.
6	M. Iglesias responded to questions regarding the number of units on the
6	5th storey and provided clarification regarding the reduction in parking
6	requirements.
6	Mayor Watters asked for a recorded vote.
6	Moved by Councillor Jennifer Adams, Seconded by Councillor Lisa
6	MacDonald.
6	THAT the Council of the Township of Centre Wellington approves
6	application RZ013/22 for a zoning amendment and gives three readings
6	to the necessary by-law.

7	8.7 Electric Vehicle (EV) ChargeON Program
8	Report from D. Masiero, Manager of Environmental Services
8	D. Masiero, Manager of Environmental Services provided an overview of
8	the report indicating the Ontario Electric Vehicle (EV) ChargeON
8	Program provides funding for the installation of public electric vehicle
8	(EV) chargers in Ontario communities outside of major cities. The
8	Township has applied for grant funding to allow for the installation of
8	eight (8) charging stations in Centre Wellington.
8	C. Baker, Managing Director of Infrastructure services responded to
8	questions regarding ongoing operation and maintenance, future revenue
8	source and location of the various locations.
8	Moved by Councillor Barbara Lustgarten-Evoy, Seconded by Councillor
8	Denis Craddock.
8	THAT the Council of the Township of Centre Wellington commit to a
8	$21,500 contribution in support of a grant application to the Ontario
8	Electric Vehicle (EV) ChargeON Program.
8	CARRIED
