3	8. Consideration of Reports
3	8.1 Community Heritage Fund Program Establishment
3	Report from M. Iglesias, Manager of Planning Services
3	M. Iglesias, Manager of Planning Services provided an overview of the
3	report, indicating Heritage grant programs provide financial assistance
3	to property owners to help with the costs associated with conservation
3	efforts, which may include restoring, preserving and rehabilitating
3	heritage properties.
3	She outlined the details of the program which included:
3	• Grant available to owners of designated properties, 115 in Centre
3	Wellington,
3	• Matching grants would be given funding up to 50% of a project, up to
3	$10,000,
3	• Applications would have to meet generally accepted heritage practices
3	and principles as set out in Canada’s Standards and Guidelines,
3	• Applicants would have to adhere to rules as set out in the by-law and
3	meet specific requirements such as obtaining a heritage permit,
4	• One per year, can be stacked,
4	• Call for applications would generally open beginning of each year and
4	close end of February (March this year) so applications can be weighed
4	against each other,
4	• Authority would be delegated to Planning staff to administer the
4	program, but an existing sub-committee of Heritage will review the
4	applications and make recommendations, with support from planning
4	staff,
4	• Annual available funds will be confirmed through the budget process
4	and staff will report back annually on program uptake.
4	M. Iglesias indicated staff is requesting Council approval of the program
4	to encourage conservation of the Township’s heritage resources over
4	the long term, which is beneficial to the community, and also to
4	encourage additional designations.
4	M. Iglesias responded to questions regarding the impact of legislative
4	changes to the inventory of listed properties and the timing of the budget
4	process related to the allocation of the OLG funding.
4	Moved by Councillor Lisa MacDonald, Seconded by Councillor Jennifer
4	Adams.
4	THAT the Council of the Township of Centre Wellington authorizes the
4	Mayor and Clerk to execute a by-law to adopt a Community Heritage
4	Grant Program, as outlined in Report PLN2024-01 dated January 15,
4	2024; and
4	THAT the Council of the Township of Centre Wellington delegates to the
4	Managing Director of Planning and Development the authority to
4	administer the Community Heritage Grant Program.
4	CARRIED
