1	4. Consideration of Reports
1	4.1 Endorsement of Councillor Wilton to the Federation of Canadian
1	Municipalities
1	Report from D. Schellenberger, Legislative Coordinator
1	Councillor Wilton provided an overview of the advocacy work
1	undertaken by the Federation of Canadian Municipalities and her
1	interest in participating on committees that may benefit from her skills
1	and expertise.
1	Moved by Councillor Barbara Lustgarten-Evoy, Seconded by Councillor
2	Denis Craddock.
2	WHEREAS the Federation of Canadian Municipalities (FCM) represents
2	the interests of member municipalities on policy and program matters
2	that fall within federal jurisdiction; and
2	WHEREAS FCM’s Board of Directors is comprised of elected municipal
2	officials from all regions and sizes of communities to form a broad base
2	of support and provide FCM with the united voice required to carry the
2	municipal message to the federal government;
2	BE IT RESOLVED that Council of the Township of Centre Wellington
2	endorse Councillor Bronwynne Wilton to serve as a committee member
2	on committees of FCM’s Board of Directors, for the period starting in
2	June 2025 and ending with the term of Council, November 2026; and
2	BE IT FURTHER RESOLVED that Council assumes all costs associated
2	with Councillor Bronwynne Wilton attending FCM meetings.
2	CARRIED
