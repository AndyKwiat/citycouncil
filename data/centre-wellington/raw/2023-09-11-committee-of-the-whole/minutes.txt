2	8.1 Pierpoint Plaque Installation Approval
2	Report from L. Rogerson, Manager of Parks
2	L. Rogerson, Manager of Parks provided an overview, indicating Parks
2	Canada Agency is seeking permission to install a Richard Pierpoint
2	Commemorative Plaque at the Pierpoint Fly Fishing Nature Reserve.
2	Staff responded to questions regarding the proposed location of the
2	plaque, and the need for improved wayfinding signage.
2	Moved by Councillor Jennifer Adams, Seconded by Councillor
2	Kimberley Jefferson.
2	THAT the Council of the Township of Centre Wellington authorize the
2	Mayor and Clerk to sign an Agreement between Parks Canada Agency
2	and the Township to install a commemorative plaque at Pierpoint Fly
2	Fishing Nature Reserve to recognize Richard Pierpoint.
2	CARRIED
2	8.2 Victoria Park Stairs Update
2	Report from P. Newson, Managing Director of Community Services
2	P. Newson, Managing Director of Community Services provided an
2	overview of the report, explaining the design for the new stairs was
2	completed and the tender issued in July 2023 with results received in
2	August. The project cost is higher than the anticipated estimated at
3	$625,000 which exceeds the current budget. She indicated staff is
3	recommending placing a hold on the project to pursue alternative
3	sources of funding and report back to Council.
3	Staff responded to questions regarding safety, current and new funding
3	sources, utilizing the community partnership program, and maintaining
3	portages.
3	Members of Council requested a report from staff on the communication
3	plan for the community partnership program.
3	After questions and a brief discussion, Councillor Lustgarten-Evoy
3	suggested an amendment to the recommendation to secure the
3	information prior to the 2025 budget.
3	Moved by Councillor Barbara Lustgarten-Evoy, Seconded by Councillor
3	Jennifer Adams.
3	THAT the motion be amended to read, “AND THAT Council directs staff
3	to pursue alternative sources of funding and report back to Council prior
3	to the adoption of the 2025 budget”.
3	CARRIED
3	Moved by Councillor Barbara Lustgarten-Evoy, Seconded by Councillor
3	Denis Craddock.
3	THAT the Council of the Township of Centre Wellington direct staff not
3	to proceed with replacing the Victoria Park Elora Stairs at this time;
3	AND THAT Council directs staff to pursue alternative sources of funding
3	and report back to Council prior to the adoption of the 2025 budget.
3	CARRIED, as amended
