9	12.5 Request for Tender #43-26 / Reconstruction of Smith Street and Victoria
9	Crescent, Elora
9	Report from Adam McNabb, Chief Financial Officer & Treasurer
9	Adam McNabb, Chief Financial Officer & Treasurer provided an overview
9	of the report outlining the reconstruction of Smith Street and Victoria
9	Crescent in Elora, a multi-phase project that will renew underground
9	infrastructure and improve safety in the road corridor for all users.
9	He noted that, as part of the project, Smith Street will be upgraded from
9	David Street West to Henderson Street. The Request for Tender (RFT)
9	was issued in accordance with the Township’s Purchasing Requirements
9	By-law and was limited to prequalified contractors. In keeping with the
9	procurement process, the contract will be awarded to the lowest compliant
9	bidder.
9	Colin Baker, Managing Director of Infrastructure Services responded to
9	questions from council regarding the contractor prequalification process,
9	noting that more than 30 contractors expressed interest in the project,
9	with 22 ultimately meeting the qualification requirements and being
9	placed on the prequalified list.
9	He advised that project signage will be installed and that staff will
9	reconnect with the community following the contract award to provide
9	updated project information. Construction is scheduled to begin during the
9	current construction season, with the successful contractor responsible
9	for providing the schedule and official start date.
10	C. Baker also responded to questions related to detour routes, noting that
10	Smith Street will be restricted to local access only during construction.
10	Pedestrian signage will be installed, and pedestrians will be encouraged
10	to use Geddes Street as an alternate route. He commented on the
10	completion of County work on Geddes Street and plans to maintain
10	resident access and parking throughout the construction period.
10	A. McNabb responded to questions from Council regarding proposed
10	parkette funding, he commented the view would be to include parkette as
10	a level of service enhancement and the Township would be seeking
10	alternate sources of funding or community partners to support that
10	project.
10	Moved by Councillor Denis Craddock, Seconded by Councillor Lisa
10	MacDonald.
10	THAT the Council of the Township of Centre Wellington awards Request
10	for Tender #43-26 regarding the reconstruction of Smith Street and
10	Victoria Crescent, Elora to 410754 Ontario Limited, o/a Sousa Concrete,
10	at a contract price of $1,777,490.28, including contingency, excluding
10	HST.
10	AND THAT the Council of the Township of Centre Wellington direct staff
10	to explore options for funding the proposed parkette.
10	AND FURTHER THAT project funding be updated as shown in report
10	FIN2026-14 dated 29 June 2026.
10	CARRIED
10	12.6 Reserve and Reserve Fund Policy Update
10	Report from Adam McNabb, Chief Financial Officer & Treasurer
10	Adam McNabb, Chief Financial Officer & Treasurer provided an overview
10	of the report stating that municipal reserves and reserve funds are
10	important long term municipal financial planning tools used to set aside
10	funds to meet future funding requirements and further commented that
10	having a reserve and reserve funds policy in place provides guidance for
10	Township staff in preparing the annual operating and capital budgets and
10	noted that this report identifies proposed changes to the Reserve and
10	Reserve Fund Policy.
10	A. McNabb responded to comments and questions from Council
10	regarding the recommended new reserves, the establishment of the MAT
10	tax reserve fund, benefit to tourism initiatives and inclusion of economic
11	development initiatives and connection to the MSE Board budget still
11	supported through Township Council, and further commented on
11	recommended funding targets for WSIB ABO Reserve fund and
11	McDonald Trust reserve, those funds were directed back to the township
11	and are formulated as part of the Bissel park project.
11	D. Wilson, CAO commented that there will be opportunity to discuss the
11	reserve fund in more detail, will be bringing forward a more detailed policy
11	like the OLG policy, as part of the 2027 Budget deliberations.
11	Moved by Councillor Barbara Lustgarten-Evoy, Seconded by Councillor
11	Lisa MacDonald.
11	THAT the Council of the Township of Centre Wellington adopt the
11	Reserve and Reserve Fund Policy outlined in report FIN2026-04 dated
11	June 29, 2026.
11	AND THAT the Council of the Township of Centre Wellington authorize
11	the Mayor and Clerk to sign a by-law with respect to this Policy.
11	AND THAT any transfers between reserves required as a result of 2026
11	transactions relating to the updated policy be made by staff.
11	CARRIED
