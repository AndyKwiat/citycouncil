3	10. Delegations
3	10.1 Erika Montero – Current Chair, Elora BIA re: item 12.2 BIA Board
3	Composition expressed concerns about a lack of transparency within
3	the BIA and Township processes. She noted that a clerical error has
3	persisted in the by-law for 15 years. Additional concerns included
3	improper financial management, lack of communication, and staff
3	appointing themselves to the interim board without adequate
3	consultation.
3	Catherine Daultrey – Member, Elora BIA & Business Owner re: item
3	12.2 BIA Board Composition spoke to the Board’s efforts to align BIA
3	operations with open meeting requirements and legal compliance. She
3	highlighted a procedural correction in progress and referenced the
3	outdated by-law, noting that council has appointed more than three
3	members for over a decade. A general election has been called four
3	times since January to address gaps left by the previous Board. She
3	raised concerns about unauthorized spending and urged council to allow
3	the Board to continue their work transparently.
3	Cathi Bastien – Elora BIA Board Member & Business Owner re: item
3	12.2 BIA Board Composition argued that the recommendation to enforce
3	an outdated by-law is unnecessary, as the business environment has
3	significantly changed since its adoption. She emphasized that the
3	current Board passed a resolution requesting council to update the by-
3	law. She raised concerns about the financial and reputational cost of the
3	current process, stressed her commitment to supporting a future board,
3	and urged council to reject the recommendation for staff-led Interim
3	Board oversight.
3	Kathy Sullivan – Member, Elora BIA & Business Owner re: item 12.2
3	BIA Board Composition described a meeting with Township staff in
3	January and emphasized her support for a collaborative, forward-looking
3	approach. She stated the board acted in good faith with Township
4	guidance and has since voted to reduce its numbers by two members to
4	return to compliance. She believes the current Board is willing to
4	cooperate and that an Interim Board is unnecessary.
4	10.2 Darcy Saunders, Elora resident re: item 12.2 BIA Board Composition
4	shared concerns regarding by-law inconsistencies and enforcement,
4	citing past construction projects that proceeded without permits. He
4	questioned the allocation of Township staff resources to dissolving a
4	volunteer-led BIA board. He noted that replacing local volunteers with
4	high-level Township staff raises concerns with transparency and
4	governance. He urged Council to reflect on the precedent being set.

4	Report from D. Wilson, Chief Administrative Officer
4	D. Wilson, Chief Administrative Officer provided an overview of the

4	Solicitor has recently advised the original by-law establishing the Elora
4	Business Improvement Area (BIA) indicates the membership on the
5	Board shall consist of four (4) members appointed by Council, one (1) of
5	whom shall be a member of Council. The Township has appointed, in
5	error, more Elora BIA Board of Management Members than actual
5	existing Board positions allowable under the establishing by-law.
