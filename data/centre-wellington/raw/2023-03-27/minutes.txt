4	8. Delegations
4	8.1 Caroline Baker, MCIP, RPP, Baker Planning Group re: Zoning
4	Amendment for 950-960 St. David Street North (RZ011-22)
4	Caroline Baker indicated she is presenting to Council on behalf of Groves
4	Hospital Volunteer Association and Reid’s Heritage Homes. She
4	reviewed the proposed redevelopment to be a mixed use development
4	with minor modifications likely to be made to the concept being presented.
4	She indicated Groves Hospital Volunteer Association would like to rebuild
4	the current store “new to you” on the property with the rear portion
4	developed as a residential development with 112 stacked townhouses.
4	The concept includes 1.2 parking spaces per dwelling unit, 3.5 stories in
4	height, and blended parking between warehousing and retail required.
4	She further reviewed the updates as a result of the transportation impact
4	study and comments from MTO while outlining the pedestrian access and
4	accessible dwelling units.
4	The consultant responded to questions from Council regarding the
4	possibility of incorporating a play structure, bicycle parking and storage
4	and perhaps upgrading the sidewalk to accommodate active
4	transportation as well as consideration of including residential above the
4	commercial space.

7	9.8 Human Resources Services Agreement - Township of Puslinch
7	Report from R. Hasan, Chief Human Resources Officer
8	R. Hasan, Chief Human Resources Officer provided the background of
8	an agreement between the Townships of Centre Wellington and Puslinch
8	to share an employee 50/50 time and cost. As the requirements of both
8	municipalities have changed, staff is proposing a fee for service for a suite
8	of HR services in replacement of the shared position. He further indicated
8	this was one of the areas identified in the County’s shared services
8	review, to develop shared a Human Resources model.
8	Moved by Councillor Bronwynne Wilton, Seconded by Councillor Lisa
8	MacDonald.
8	THAT the Council of the Township of Centre Wellington authorize the
8	Mayor and Clerk to sign an Agreement for Human Resources Services
8	between the Township of Centre Wellington and the Township of Puslinch
8	as presented in report HR2023-02.
8	CARRIED

8	Report from P. Newson, Managing Director of Community Services
8	P. Newson, Managing Director of Community Services indicated at a
8	previous meeting Trade Tracks requested the waiving of the facility rental
8	fees. Previous Council’s have not entertained waiving the fees. As an
8	alternative, staff is recommending removing the cost of the space rented
8	that is not planned to be utilized by the event in addition to the 15%
8	discounts offered to large events.
8	Moved by Councillor Denis Craddock, Seconded by Councillor Jennifer
8	Adams.

8	remove from the Trade Tracks rental permit, the charge for the ball
8	diamond and soccer field for a savings of $896.07, and that these spaces
8	be blocked off by the Township and not booked during the event.
