3	8.2 Decision Report RZ06/22 (Fergus Golf Club)
3	Report from B. Salmon, Managing Director of Planning and Development
3	B. Salmon, Managing Director of Planning and Development provided an
3	overview of the report and responded to questions related to the well
3	monitoring program, the communal water system responsibility
3	agreement, environmental protection, details concerning the buffer and
3	fencing barrier, the functionality of the new storm water management
3	system with the abandonment of the Black Drain, and dust control
3	measures during construction.

3	Lisa MacDonald.
3	THAT the Council of the Township of Centre Wellington approves
3	application RZ06/22 and gives three readings to a by-law to amend the
3	Township of Centre Wellington Zoning By-law;
3	AND THAT the Township advises the County of Wellington that it
3	supports the granting of draft approval of the proposed plan of subdivision
3	23T-22001 and plan of condominium 23CD-22001, subject to the
3	conditions set out in Report PLN2023-34.
