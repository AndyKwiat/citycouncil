2	6. Presentations and Announcements
2	6.1 Carl Gray, South Wellington Community Lions Branch has created a new
2	event, Trade Tracks, which will take place in Fergus on the fairground.
2	Trade Tracks is intended to introduce young people to the trades. The
2	event includes participants from colleges, government organizations and
2	school boards. The event will offer students and the public an opportunity
2	to see what a career in the trades looks like. They are requesting Council
2	waive the rental charges for the facility in Fergus where the event is to be
2	held.
2	Mr. Gray responded to questions regarding the attendance of the Armed
2	Forces.
2	Moved by Councillor Bronwynne Wilton, Seconded by Councillor Lisa
2	MacDonald.
2	THAT the Council of the Township of Centre Wellington refer the request
2	from the South Wellington Community Lions to waive the rental fee for the
2	Trade Track event to staff for a report.
2	CARRIED

5	New Township Operations Centre Project Update
5	Report from A. Gilmore, Manager of Engineering
5	C. Baker, Managing Director of Infrastructure gave a presentation with
5	Adam Gilmore, Adam McNabb, and Dan Wilson outlining the background
5	of the project, the findings of the space needs assessment, the site
5	selection process, an overview of the site development, including
5	conceptual design options, heritage findings and sustainable and “green”
5	building opportunities. They further outlined the next steps including the
5	rational for the relocation for the Planning and Development department.
5	Staff responded to questions regarding partnership with the County, use
5	of the centre by the public, the number of staff relocated to the new centre,
5	timing of the heritage impact assessment, expansion of the work from
5	home program, use of 205 Queen Street in Fergus, additional
5	administrative spaces, and use of the existing farmhouse.
5	Councillor Wilton proposed a friendly amendment to have staff report
5	back on options / alternatives discussed, including future uses of the
5	farmhouse.
5	Moved by Councillor Bronwynne Wilton, Seconded by Councillor
5	Barbara Lustgarten-Evoy.
5	THAT the Council of the Township of Centre Wellington receive as
5	information an update on the New Township Operations Centre project,
5	as outlined in Report No. IS2023-05;
5	AND THAT the Council of the Township of Centre Wellington endorses
5	conceptual design Option 2 to add the Planning & Development
5	Department to the New Township Operations Centre.
5	AND THAT staff report back on options / alternatives discussed at the
5	Committee of the Whole meeting held March 13, 2023 including future
5	use of the farmhouse.
5	CARRIED
