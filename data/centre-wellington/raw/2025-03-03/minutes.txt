1	6.1 Demolition and Clean-Up of 25 West Mill Street Elora
1	Report from D. Wilson, Chief Administrative Officer
1	D. Wilson, Chief Administrative Officer provided an overview of the
2	report, indicating 25 West Mill Street, is the location of the Elora
2	Greenspace, a property owned by Suncor Energy Inc., and leased by
2	the Township. He indicated on February 24, 2025, at approximately 3:30
2	pm, the roof and portion of the walls of the stone structure located on
2	the property collapsed.
2	D. Wilson indicated the structure requires demolition due to its condition.
2	The owners of the adjacent property, Kat Florence Corporation, have
2	expressed interest in participating in the demolition and clean-up since
2	part of the debris is located on their property and the structure is
2	attached to their building at 43 West Mill Street. He explained the
2	involvement of the adjacent property owners in the demolition project
2	presents a practical solution to several challenges, including safety and
2	access related to the demolition. The terms of the agreement have been
2	negotiated to reflect these interests while ensuring all demolition and
2	clean-up work is conducted in compliance with municipal standards and
2	regulations.
2	D. Wilson indicated Kevin Thompson from SV Law is present to respond
2	to any questions.
2	D. Wilson responded to questions regarding responsibility for the
2	building and whether regular inspections were conducted, and if the
2	building was occupied.
2	Councillor Jefferson posed a question regarding the reimbursement of
2	legal and consulting engineering costs and suggested the importance of
2	the Township paying its own legal and consulting engineering costs to
2	maintain the division of private and public responsibility.
2	Moved by Councillor Denis Craddock, Seconded by Councillor Lisa
2	MacDonald.
2	THAT the Council of the Township of Centre Wellington delegates
2	authority to the Chief Administrative Officer to finalize and execute a
2	Temporary Access and Demolition Agreement on behalf of the
2	Township of Centre Wellington for the demolition and clean-up of the
2	structure located at 25 West Mill Street, Elora, generally in accordance
2	with the draft agreement appended to Report CAO2025-03;
2	AND THAT the Township’s associated legal and consulting engineering
2	costs be funded from the general capital reserve.
2	CARRIED
