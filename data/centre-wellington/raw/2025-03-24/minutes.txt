5	8.2 Asset Management Levels of Service Framework
5	Report from A. McNabb, Managing Director of Corporate Services &
5	Treasurer
5	Adam McNabb, Managing Director of Corporate Services & Treasurer
5	provided an overview of the report, indicating staff have been working in
5	concert with SLBC to develop a Levels of Service (LOS) framework that
5	would be compliant with legislation and facilitate the long-term service
5	delivery target setting for Centre Wellington.
5	Eric Goforth, Senior Associate, Advisory Services, SLBC Inc., provided
5	an overview of the proposed framework and outlined the three phases of
5	the project; Phase 1 develop the Levels of Service framework, Phase 2
5	Community Engagement Strategy and Phase 3 Proposed LOS and
5	Implementation Plan. He outlined the Levels of Service Approach, the
5	project outcomes, and the next steps in the project.
5	E. Goforth responded to questions regarding the historical performance
5	of the measures tracked, adjustment of the long-term metrix within the
5	framework, staff resources required to maintain, and use of the
5	marketing framework and graphics for community engagement.
5	Moved by Councillor Bronwynne Wilton, Seconded by Councillor Denis
5	Craddock.
5	THAT the Council of the Township of Centre Wellington endorse the
5	draft levels of service framework as presented in Report COR2025-10
5	and direct staff to utilize the framework for the purposes of drafting the
5	2025 asset management plan update.
5	CARRIED

6	8.5 2025 Debt Issue
6	Report from A. McNabb, Managing Director of Corporate Services &
6	Treasurer
6	Adam McNabb, Managing Director of Corporate Services & Treasurer
6	noted staff is anticipating the need for marketing a debt issue to satisfy
7	the Township’s long-term borrowing needs for 2025. He indicated staff
7	has investigated three opportunities and is seeking Council direction to
7	proceed with formalizing one of the options outlined in the report.
7	Adam McNabb responded to a question regarding the impact and
7	potential risk in the event of a reduction or elimination of development
7	charges.
7	Moved by Councillor Jennifer Adams, Seconded by Councillor Lisa
7	MacDonald.
7	THAT the Council of the Township of Centre Wellington direct staff to
7	work with Toronto Dominion Bank to formalize a debt issuance in the
7	form of a fixed rate borrowing with an interest rate swap up to $27.7
7	Million Dollars for the purposes of satisfying the long term borrowing
7	requirements of the 2025 calendar year for the Township.
7	CARRIED
