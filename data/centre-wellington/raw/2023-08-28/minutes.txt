3	Report from R. Maiden, Water and Wastewater Capital Project Manager

3	stakeholders of the formal start of the Municipal Class Environmental
3	Assessment through the issuance of a Notice of Commencement. He

3	increasing the capacity of the Union Street sewage pumping station.

3	Kimberley Jefferson.

3	information Report No. IS2023-19 : Municipal Class Environmental
3	Assessment Study for the Union Street Sewage Pumping Station
3	Expansion - Notice of Study Commencement.
