2	7. Mayor's Inaugural Address
2	Mayor Watters welcomed all to our Council Chamber for this Inaugural Council
2	Meeting and further welcomed those of you watching from home. He continued
2	with the following:
2	“I would like to acknowledge the support of my friends and family and especially
2	my wife Deb, whose love and guidance has made me a better person. I love you
2	Deb.
2	I want to recognize Mayor Linton and thank the 2018 to 2021 Council for their
2	dedicated service. I would also like to thank staff for their continued support and
2	professionalism as we make the transition to this new Council. Centre Wellington
2	is a better place because of your contribution and efforts.
2	My congratulations to all of you that are now sitting around this horseshoe. I am
2	immensely proud of your past community involvement and so pleased you have
2	found your way to be newly elected members of this constituted Council. Your
2	families and friends I am sure are equally proud of you. I thank you for putting
2	yourself out there. At times you will be scrutinized by the public and it may get
2	tough, however, you will get through these times because of the support you
2	have around you, especially as we support one another as a team. Remember
2	you are not an island.
2	These are challenging times, and they will test our mettle as a Council as we
2	move forward. Mutual respect, listening to one another and being supportive of
2	each other are keys to building a team that will be able to meet these challenges.
2	I also ask the public to be patient as the new council settles in and develops a
2	good understanding of our new roles and responsibilities. As we move forward
2	as a Council, our challenges will include some of the following:
2	- Managing our growth targets and working towards an attainable housing
2	strategy;
2	- Planning a sustainable, healthy and livable community;
2	- Providing a cost-effective Infrastructure which is timely and highlights our roads
2	and bridge network;
2	- Developing a Short-term rentals strategy;
2	- Managing our financial budgets which is mindful of these challenging times.
2	Developing a parking strategy for our downtown area.
2	- Protection of our water and farmland resources.
3	- Developing a climate adaptation strategy.
3	- Preserving our architecture, cultural heritage, and natural landscapes.
3	- Building a welcoming and inclusive community.
3	- Developing a respectful and accountable council.
3	For Council to meet these challenges, we must support each other as we work
3	with committees, staff, and the public. Finally, our relationships with First
3	Nations, County of Wellington and the Province are also extremely important as
3	we are partners and thus our mutual respect and our ability to work through
3	issues is of high importance and should be a common goal.
3	Centre Wellington can be a leader in rural community development if we are not
3	afraid to speak out and are willing to listen and work towards sustainable
3	solutions with our partners and the public. Lastly, Councillors, Relax! Smile! This
3	is your night and please enjoy. Good evening to you all. Meeting Adjourn. Be
3	proud!”
3	8. ADJOURNMENT
3	8.1 Mayor Watters called the meeting adjourned.
