2	8. Public Meetings to Consider Zoning Amendments
2	8.1 RZ007-24 6518 First Line, Fergus
2	Mayor Watters indicated the public meeting is to present and receive
2	public input regarding a proposed zoning by-law amendment on land
2	within the Centre Wellington Business Park in Fergus.
2	He noted the purpose of the proposed zoning by-law amendment is to
2	modify the zoning regulations for the Centre Wellington Business Park –
2	North Fergus, including changes to the permitted uses and the lot
2	regulations.
2	The effect of the proposed zoning by-law amendment is to implement
2	the recommendations of the Centre Wellington Business Park Strategy.
2	He indicated if a person or public body would otherwise have an ability
3	to appeal the decision of the Township of Centre Wellington to the
3	Ontario Land Tribunal (OLT) but the person or public body does not
3	make oral submissions at a public meeting or make written submissions
3	to the Township of Centre Wellington before the by-law is passed, the
3	person or public body is not entitled to appeal the decision.
3	Mayor Watters stated if a person or public body does not make oral
3	submissions at a public meeting, or make written submissions to the
3	Township of Centre Wellington before the by-law is passed, the person
3	or public body may not be added as a party to the hearing of an appeal
3	before the Ontario Land Tribunal (OLT) unless, in the opinion of the
3	Tribunal, there are reasonable grounds to do so.
3	Mayor Watters advised if anyone wishes to be notified of the decision of
3	the Township of Centre Wellington in respect of the proposed zoning by-
3	law amendment, a written request must be made to the Municipal Clerk
3	of the Township of Centre Wellington, 1 MacDonald Square, Elora,
3	Ontario, N0B 1S0.
3	The Municipal Clerk advised notice was provided to prescribed persons,
3	agencies and public bodies on or before December 19, 2024. A copy
3	was also placed in the Wellington Advertiser on December 19, 2024.
3	The Municipal Clerk confirmed the requirement for giving notice was
3	completed on December 19, 2024.
3	Mayor Watters asked Brett Salmon, Managing Director of Planning and
3	Development, to present the application.
3	Brett Salmon, Managing Director of Planning provided an overview of
3	the proposal, including the scope of the new M2.BP zone and the new
3	uses to be added as well as the permitted uses to be deleted. He
3	indicated the permitted uses to be added include the following: artisan
3	studio, communication and broadcasting establishment, emergency
3	service facility, laboratory, and business or professional office excluding
3	a medical clinic.
3	B. Salmon outlined additional lot and building regulations relief sought,
3	including the outdoor storage area, minimum lot frontage, side, front and
3	rear yard set backs, minimum landscaped area and buffer strip.
3	B. Salmon responded to questions regarding the restriction on outdoor
3	display, rationale for excluding medical office/clinic, inclusion of shared
3	space and/or multi-tenant units.
3	Mayor Watters indicated there are no delegations registered and asked
4	if there was anyone present who wished to speak to the application.
4	There being none, Mayor Watters thanked everyone for attending and
4	indicated the application will be considered later in the meeting. He
4	declared the public meeting adjourned.

7	12.2 Zoning Amendment RZ04-2024 Decision Report - 750 St David Street
7	N., Fergus
7	Report from D. Maiden, Senior Development Planner
8	D. Maiden, Senior Development Planner provided an overview of the
8	report, including the scope of the amendment. She noted the property
8	currently contains a single detached bungalow and detached garage
8	that will be removed to facilitate redevelopment. The application seeks
8	to amend the zoning from R1C - Residential to R4 – Residential with
8	exemptions, as outlined in the staff report. The redevelopment proposes
8	12, 2-bedroom purpose built rental units, with 18 parking spaces, along
8	with secure, exterior bicycle lockers.
8	D. Maiden indicated concerns raised related primarily to street parking,
8	traffic, location of a new access on Parkside Drive East, snow removal,
8	light pollution, servicing (water pressure) and retention of trees. She
8	noted staff are of the opinion most of the concerns raised have been
8	adequately addressed or can be addressed through a future site plan
8	process.
8	D. Maiden responded to questions regarding the location of the
8	driveway, and the various technical studies submitted in support of the
8	application.
8	Pierre Chauvin, MHBC responded to questions related to the
8	functionality of St. David Street as an arterial road, the intention to rent
8	the units, and the location of multi-residential and commercial uses
8	across the street.
8	Colin Baker, Managing Director of Infrastructure Services noted future
8	plans for bike lanes, traffic calming, improved signage and safety
8	measures.
8	Moved by Councillor Kimberley Jefferson, Seconded by Councillor
8	Barbara Lustgarten-Evoy.
8	THAT the Council of the Township of Centre Wellington approves
8	application RZ04/24 for a Zoning By-law amendment and gives three
8	readings to the necessary by-law.
8	CARRIED
