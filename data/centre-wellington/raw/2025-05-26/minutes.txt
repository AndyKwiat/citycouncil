5	8.2 Water and Wastewater Servicing Master Plan - Preliminary Preferred
5	Alternative
5	Report from Ryan Maiden, Water and Wastewater Capital Project
5	Manager
5	Adam Gilmore, Manager of Engineering and Ryan Maiden, Water and
5	Wastewater Capital Project Manager and John Tyrrell, RV Anderson
5	and Associates provided the overall approach, indicating the Water and
5	Wastewater Servicing Master Plan will identify capacity constraints and
5	identify and evaluate opportunities to increase system capacity to
5	accommodate for growth to the 2051 planning horizon. They provided
5	an overview of the 2051 wastewater requirements, the current site
5	constraints and provided two options: 1) Retain Fergus WWTP as a
5	Conventional Activated Sludge (CAS) facility and expand capacity via a
5	new 3rd Liquid Train; or 2) Convert Fergus WWTP to a Membrane Bio-
5	Reactor (MBR) facility.
5	John Tyrrell outlined the two options, noting Option 1 is the preferred
5	option and he outlined the next steps in the process. He responded to
5	questions regarding the reliability of the technology, the roll out of an
5	Annual Storm Drainage Disconnection Grant Program, funding
6	opportunities, and no expansion beyond OPA126.
6	Moved by Councillor Bronwynne Wilton, Seconded by Councillor
6	Barbara Lustgarten-Evoy.
6	THAT the Council of the Township of Centre Wellington receive for
6	information Report IS2025-18: Centre Wellington Water and Wastewater
6	Servicing Master Plan Preliminary Preferred Alternative.
6	CARRIED

6	8.4 New Township Operations Centre Update - Guaranteed Maximum Price
6	Contract Award
6	Report from Brandon Buehler, Operations Centre Project Manager
6	Colin Baker, Managing Director of Infrastructure Services, Brandon
6	Buehler, Operations Centre Project Manager and Adam Gilmore,
6	Manager of Engineering provided an update on; a) the progress,
6	including the works completed to date, b) the sustainability and green
6	building features incorporated into the design, c) the grant funding
6	opportunities, on the potential solar panel options under consideration
6	and d) to present the guaranteed maximum price and overall
6	construction budget.
6	Adam McNabb, Managing Director of Corporate Services & Treasurer
6	indicated the financial strategy to fund to project is structured around
6	growth components being funded through development charge serviced
6	debt, and non-growth components being serviced through reserve funds
6	and the proceeds of land sales.
7	Staff responded to questions regarding the potential impact of Bill 17,
7	the fluctuating real estate market, projects management costs, green
7	house capacity and future needs, debt financing and zero impact to
7	taxation.
7	Moved by Councillor Bronwynne Wilton, Seconded by Councillor
7	Kimberley Jefferson.
7	THAT the Council of the Township of Centre Wellington approves the
7	Guaranteed Maximum Price and Overall Construction Budget for the
7	new Operations Centre as outlined in Report IS2025-15, and authorizes
7	staff to reach agreement with the Design-Builder (Ball Construction Ltd.)
7	on the Guaranteed Maximum Price in the form of a change order to the
7	existing Design-Build Contract;
7	AND THAT Council directs staff to proceed with construction of the new
7	Township Operations Centre with substantial completion anticipated by
7	December 2026;
7	AND THAT Council directs staff to further investigate solar power
7	generation opportunities for the site and report back to Council with a
7	recommended option and financial analysis;
7	AND THAT Council directs staff to submit an application for grant
7	funding through the Municipal Fleet Electrification Grant Fund offered by
7	the Federation of Canadian Municipalities (FCM) to fund the cost of
7	proposed EV charging stations and electrification of the Township's
7	existing vehicle fleet;
7	AND THAT Council receives for information the staff recommendations
7	regarding potential amendments to the capital forecast with respect to
7	this project.
7	AND THAT Council direct staff to investigate a total debt issue of $33.9
7	million dollars, to be drawn as needed to satisfy the debt supported
7	capital needs for the 2025 and 2026 calendar years, and return to
7	Council with the necessary financing, debt policy, and any associated
7	by-laws once established.
