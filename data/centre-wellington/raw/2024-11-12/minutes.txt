4	11.2 Cemetery Product, Services and Fees Review report -
4	LEES+Associates
4	Report from L. Rogerson, Manager of Parks
4	Presentation from Kelsey Walker and Jennifer Thibert, LEES +
4	Associates
4	Lucas Rogerson, Manager of Parks introduced consultants Kelsey
4	Walker and Jennifer Thibert, LEES + Associates.
4	Kelsey Walker and Jennifer Thibert, LEES + Associates provided an
4	overview of the report, indicating the primary objectives in developing
4	this Cemetery Product, Services and Fees Review are a) ensure there
4	are relevant and appropriate products and services to serve Centre
4	Wellington families and b) ensure cemetery fees are in line with the local
4	market.
4	The consultants outlined the current offerings and key findings, including
4	a) the Township’s cemeteries currently offer a similar range of interment
4	services, inventory and memorialization options, b) Belsyde Cemetery’s
4	burial lots are nearly depleted and c) consider new offerings to attract
4	the interest of community members.
5	The consultants outlined their recommendations in the short term and
5	long term and responded to questions regarding various green burial
5	options, impact of prepaid funerals on the report recommendations,
5	revenue neutral approach vs alignment with other cemeteries, and
5	provided details on a non-resident premium.
5	Moved by Councillor Bronwynne Wilton, Seconded by Councillor Lisa
5	MacDonald.
5	THAT the Council of the Township of Centre Wellington approve the
5	recommendations of the LEES+Associates Cemetery Product, Services
5	and Fees Review report, summarized in Report CS2024-33, with the
5	exceptions of: 6 (a), (g), (h), (k) and 7;
5	AND THAT Council directs staff to include the new recommended rates
5	and fees in the 2025 Rates and Fees By-law to be approved as part of
5	the 2025 Budget deliberations.
5	CARRIED
5	11.3 2024 Urban Forestry Update Report
5	Report L. Rogerson, Manager of Parks
5	Lucas Rogerson, Manager of Parks provided an overview of the report,
5	outlining the division compliment, the objectives of the public forest
5	policy and the details regarding the public tree by-law.
5	Todd Fritzley, Urban Forestry Coordinator provided an overview of the
5	activities in 2024, including an educational workshop on pruning and
5	chainsaw use, the pruning and removal programs, the yearly plantings
5	and stump removals, and tree maintenance in parks and along
5	roadways.
5	Staff highlighted the tracking of service requests, the development of a
5	digital tree inventory, including the health of the tree, and outlined the
5	goals and objectives for 2025.
5	Staff responded to questions regarding the replacement of trees
5	removed and the planting policy, adequate budget, inclusion of new tree
5	species, and the replacement of trees in the rural area and roadways.
5	Moved by Councillor Barbara Lustgarten-Evoy, Seconded by Councillor
5	Bronwynne Wilton.
5	THAT the Council of the Township of Centre Wellington receive for
5	information, report CS2024-35 regarding the 2024 Urban Forestry
5	Update report.

6	11.4 My Main Street Grant
6	Report from K. Bettiol, Manager of Community Development
6	Kristen Bettiol, Manager of Community Development provided an
6	overview of the report and responded to questions. She indicated the
6	grant is through the Federal Economic Development Agency for
6	Southern Ontario to help support the revitalization of neighbourhoods. K.
6	Bettiol outlined the various projects to be undertaken including additional
6	live music at Twilight at Templin, more night markets, and heritage
6	building façade lighting. She responded to questions regarding the
6	challenges to meet the funding deadline, the agreements with property
6	owners and the additional funding.
6	Moved by Councillor Lisa MacDonald, Seconded by Councillor Barbara
6	Lustgarten-Evoy.
6	THAT the Council of the Township of Centre Wellington delegate
6	authority to the Managing Director of Corporate Services to enter into an
6	agreement with the funder, My Main Street from the Federal Economic
6	Development Agency for Southern Ontario (FedDev Ontario), for the
6	implementation of Night Markets, additions to Twilight in Templin and
6	façade lighting, in Fergus, as outlined in Report CS2024-34;
6	AND THAT the Council of the Township of Centre Wellington delegate
6	authority to the Managing Director of Community Services to identify the
6	appropriate buildings for the pilot lighting program and authorize the
6	agreements with the building owners and contractors to execute the
6	work;
6	AND THAT the Council of the Township of Centre Wellington direct to
6	staff to issue a media release that complies with the funder’s
6	communication plan, and include the project on Connect CW;
7	AND THAT the Council of the Township of Centre Wellington direct staff
7	to explore a second phase of the lighting project in Fergus, to proceed
7	once the My Main Street grant pilot initiatives are completed and
7	successful.
7	CARRIED
