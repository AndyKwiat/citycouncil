3	8. Consideration of Reports
3	8.1 Planning and Development Fee Study
3	Report from B. Salmon, Managing Director of Planning and Development
3	B. Salmon, Managing Director of Planning and Development introduced
3	Peter Simcisko, Watson and Associates. Mr. Salmon indicated the last
3	planning fee review was completed in 2004, and in the previous term of
3	Council a building fee analysis was presented and updated. The goal is
3	to produce a fee structure that will result in full cost recovery. He informed
3	Council the current fee structure from 2004 was set at 75% cost recovery
3	at the time as per Council’s direction and staff is recommending shifting
3	to 100% cost recovery.
3	Peter Simcisko, Watson and Associates gave a presentation outlining the
3	objective of the Comprehensive Development Application Fees Review
3	with regard for conformity with legislation, make if defensible, to balance
3	the Township’s need to maximize cost recovery with stakeholder
3	interests, affordability and competitiveness and follow industry best
3	practices. He reviewed the activity-based costing methodology including
3	direct costs, indirect costs and capital costs. The result of the study will
3	be a fee recommendation coming back to Council.
3	Moved by Councillor Bronwynne Wilton, Seconded by Councillor
3	Barbara Lustgarten-Evoy.
3	THAT the Council of the Township of Centre Wellington accepts the
3	Planning and Development Fee Study completed by Watson &
3	Associates Economists Ltd.,
4	AND THAT Council authorizes Township staff to prepare an amendment
4	to the Fees and Charges By-law.
4	CARRIED
4	8.2 Community Land Needs Review
4	Report from B. Salmon, Managing Director of Planning and Development
4	Sarah Wilhelm, Manager of Policy Planning, County of Wellington gave a
4	presentation regarding the land needs assessment for the County of
4	Wellington as a whole. She reviewed the growth plan from the Province
4	for the growth plan for the Greater Golden Horseshoe, indicating the
4	County is part of the outer ring. She indicated a major change in the
4	legislation is growth now being defined as a minimum instead of a
4	maximum and an increase share of predicted growth has been assigned
4	to the County of Wellington.
4	Councillor MacDonald raised concerns about the services available in
4	Centre Wellington, such as daycare which are required to support growth.
4	In response to a question, Ms. Wilhelm explained the methodology for
4	calculating intensification rates throughout the County.
4	Brett Salmon, Managing Director of Planning & Development, outlined the
4	report and indicated staff would like to complete this study to look at the
4	options to mitigate land needed for growth options and present all options
4	to Council; however, he pointed out all land outside the existing urban
4	boundary is agricultural.
4	Councillor Wilton requested this scenario be added to the
4	recommendations to include the scenario with 30% intensification with
4	55% density in the Community Land Needs Review study.
4	Moved by Councillor Denis Craddock, Seconded by Councillor Jennifer
4	Adams.
4	THAT Council of the Township of Centre Wellington authorize staff to
4	retain Watson & Associates Economists to prepare a Community Land
4	Needs Assessment as per staff report PLN2023-06 dated January 30,
4	2023;
4	AND THAT the scenario for 30% intensification with 55 people and jobs
4	per hectare be included in the Community Land Needs Assessment.
4	CARRIED, as amended
