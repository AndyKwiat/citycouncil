4	8. Consideration of Reports
4	8.1 Transit Feasibility Study - Progress Report
4	Report from C. Baker, Managing Director of Infrastructure Services
4	C. Baker, Managing Director of Infrastructure Services introduced
4	Dennis Kar from Dillon Consulting and provided highlights of recent
4	consultation activities.
5	Dennis Kar indicated the Township has commenced a Transit Feasibility
5	Study (TFS) to identify a preferred transit solution that considers
5	population and employment growth in the Township to the 2051
5	timeframe. He outlined the study objectives, the community engagement
5	conducted to date, a progress report and the next steps in the project.
5	D. Kar responded to questions regarding the on-demand services and
5	alignment with local services, opportunities to integrate with existing
5	services such as Ride Well, phase II of the community engagement,
5	expansion of the shuttle program, and future funding opportunities.
5	Moved by Councillor Barbara Lustgarten-Evoy, Seconded by Councillor
5	Bronwynne Wilton.
5	THAT the Council of the Township of Centre Wellington receive for
5	information a progress report on the Transit Feasibility Study.
5	CARRIED
5	8.2 2025 Water and Wastewater Rate Study and Financial Plans
5	Report from D. Wilson, Chief Administrative Officer
5	D. Wilson, Chief Administrative Officer introduced Daryl Abs, Managing
5	Partner, Watson & Associates Economists Ltd.
5	Daryl Abs, Managing Partner, Watson & Associates Economists Ltd.,
5	provided an overview of the report and outlined the following
5	recommendations:
5	· Minor increases to other rates (base charges, sprinkler systems, bulk
5	water, etc.);
5	· Removing water and wastewater connection charges as of 2026; and
5	· Requiring properties to connect to both water and wastewater systems.
5	Volume Rates
5	· Water Rates - 3.0% annual increase
5	· Wastewater Rates - 2.0% annual increase
5	· Blended Rate - 2.5% annual increase
5	Daryl Abs and Colin Baker responded to questions regarding requiring
5	properties to connect to both water and wastewater systems, the ground
5	water collection system in some neighbourhoods, and provided
5	clarification regarding water rates average of 2% and sprinkler rates of
5	3%.
5	Councillor MacDonald requested consideration to reducing the
6	wastewater rates from 2% to 1%.
6	Mayor Watters indicated there would be an opportunity during the
6	budget deliberations to consider a reduction in the wastewater rates.
6	Moved by Councillor Lisa MacDonald, Seconded by Councillor Denis
6	Craddock.
6	THAT the Council of the Township of Centre Wellington approve the
6	2025 Water and Wastewater Rate Study prepared by Watson &
6	Associates Economists Ltd;
6	AND THAT the proposed water and wastewater rates be incorporated
6	into future draft budgets for consideration;
6	AND THAT the Council of the Township of Centre Wellington approve
6	the 2025 Water and Wastewater Financial Plans prepared by Watson &
6	Associates Economists Ltd;
6	AND THAT the Water and Wastewater Financial Plans be submitted to
6	the Ministry of the Environment, Conservation, and Parks.
6	CARRIED
