3	8. Public Meeting re Proposed Amendment to the Community Improvement
3	Plan
3	8.1 Mayor Watters indicated the purpose of the public meeting is to present
3	and receive public input regarding a proposed update to the Township
3	Urban Centre Community Improvement Plan prior to making a decision.
3	He noted the Township has recently completed a review, finishing the
3	preparation of an updated CIP, referenced as the 2023-CIP. The 2023-
3	CIP provides financial incentive programs for properties within Township
3	boundaries.
3	He indicated if a person or public body does not make oral submissions
3	at the public meeting, or make written submissions to the Township of
3	Centre Wellington before the update is adopted, the person or public
3	body is not entitled to appeal the decision of the Township of Centre
3	Wellington to the Ontario Land Tribunal (OLT).
3	Mayor Watters stated if a person or public body does not make oral
3	submission at the public meeting, or make written submissions to the
3	Township of Centre Wellington before the update is passed, the person
3	or public body may not be added as the party to the hearing of an
3	appeal before the OLT unless, in the opinion of the Tribunal, there are
3	reasonable grounds to do so.
3	Mayor Watters advised if anyone wishes to be notified of the adoption of
3	the proposed update to the CIP, a written request must be filed with the
3	Municipal Clerk, Township of Centre Wellington, 1 MacDonald Square,
4	Elora, ON N0B 1S0.
4	The Municipal Clerk advised notice was mailed to prescribed persons,
4	agencies and public bodies on or before October 19, 2023. A copy was
4	published in the Wellington Advertiser on October 19, 2023 and October
4	26, 2023. She confirmed the requirement for giving notice was
4	completed on October 19, 2023.
4	Sally Litchfield, Economic Development Coordinator provided an
4	overview of the CIP update and introduced the consultant, Luciano
4	Piccioni, President, RCI Consulting.
4	Luciano Piccioni provided a project update and outlined the community
4	improvement project area, the incentive programs, the implementation
4	and administration process, the monitoring and marketing plans, and the
4	final steps, including notice of adoption and the 20 day appeal period.
4	There being no registered delegations, Mayor Watters provided an
4	opportunity for Members of Council to ask questions.
4	L. Piccioni responded to questions regarding any current applications
4	and the evaluation process for the incentive programs.
4	Mayor Watters thanked everyone for attending and advised Council will
4	be considering the matter later on in the meeting.
4	Mayor Watters declared the meeting adjourned.

7	13.2 Battery Energy Storage Systems - Request for Municipal Support
7	Resolutions
7	Report from S. Litchfield, Economic Development Coordinator
7	Delegations
7	Dave Anders, Alectra Energy Solutions and Trevor Tempson,
7	Convergent provided an overview of the Belwood Energy Storage
7	project and a request for a municipal support resolution. D. Anders
7	provided background on the joint venture between Alectra and
7	Convergent. He indicated the Battery Energy Storage Systems (BESS)
7	are safe, affordable, long-lasting energy resources that will make
7	Ontario’s electricity grid cleaner, more reliable and more resilient. He
7	noted the project and community benefits, indicating the Alectra
7	Convergent joint venture recognizes the importance of partnership with
7	the Township and is offering a meaningful long-term investment in the
7	community.
8	The representatives responded to questions regarding community
8	outreach conducted to date, the connection to Hydro One as a
8	transmission interconnection and the importance to protect agricultural
8	lands.
8	Ondrej Benjik, Director of Development, Aypa Power provided an
8	overview of Aypa, details regarding the Elora Battery Energy Storage
8	System, feedback from community engagement held and shared visual
8	concept renderings of the Elora project and community endorsements.
8	He responded to a question regarding studies associated with the
8	impact on agricultural lands.
8	Council recessed and returned at 8:30 pm.
8	S. Litchfield, Economic Development Coordinator provided an overview
8	of the report, indicating staff is of the opinion battery energy storage
8	system facilities should be located on employment land and not in prime
8	agricultural area.
8	Mayor Watters noted staff has suggested two recommendations for
8	Council consideration. Councillor Wilton suggested a third option to split
8	Option 1 and consider the requests of Aypa and Alectra separately.
8	Discussion ensued and staff responded to questions.
8	Moved by Councillor Kimberley Jefferson, Seconded by Councillor Lisa
8	MacDonald.
8	THAT the Council of the Township of Centre Wellington grants a
8	Municipal Council Support Resolution for battery energy storage system
8	project bid Elora BESS by Aypa Power, with a condition that the
8	proposed development be subject to all applicable Municipal and
8	Provincial by-laws and Standards as determined by the Municipality,
8	including any applicable Official Plan policies and Zoning By-law
8	provisions.
8	THAT the Council of the Township of Centre Wellington grants a
8	Municipal Council Support Resolution for battery energy storage system
8	project bid Belwood Energy Storage by Alectra Energy Solutions, with a
8	condition that the proposed development be subject to all applicable
8	Municipal and Provincial by-laws and Standards as determined by the
8	Municipality, including any applicable Official Plan policies and Zoning
8	By-law provisions.
8	CARRIED
