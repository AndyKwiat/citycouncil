2	8. Public Meeting to Consider an Official Plan Amendment & Zoning
2	Amendment
2	8.1 OP01/25 and RZ01/25
2	Mayor Watters indicated the purpose of this public meeting is to present
2	and receive public input regarding proposed official plan amendment
2	and zoning by-law amendment broadly affecting lands in the Township.
2	He noted the purpose of the proposed official plan amendment is to
2	change Section C.5- Housing Policies to include a new section C.5.8-
2	Additional Residential Units.
2	The effect of the official plan amendment is to include policies regarding
2	Additional Residential Units (ARUs) on land where residential uses are
2	permitted.
2	Mayor Watters indicated the purpose of the proposed zoning by-law
2	amendment is to allow Additional Residential Units (ARUs), both
2	attached and detached, on lands where residential uses are permitted
2	and provide associated regulations. And further to permit “M1-Service
2	Industrial” uses to be added to the list of permitted uses in “M2 –
3	General Industrial”.
3	The effect of the amendment is to implement zoning regulations
3	necessary to facilitate Additional Residential Units and to add permitted
3	uses in the M2 - Industrial zone.
3	Mayor Watters indicated if a person or public body would otherwise
3	have an ability to appeal the decision of the County of Wellington to the
3	Ontario Land Tribunal (OLT) but the person or public body does not
3	make oral submissions at a public meeting or make written submissions
3	to the Township of Centre Wellington before the official plan amendment
3	is adopted, the person or public body is not entitled to appeal the
3	decision.
3	He indicated if a person or public body does not make oral submissions
3	at a public meeting, or make written submissions to the Township of
3	Centre Wellington before the proposed official plan amendment is
3	adopted, the person or public body may not be added as a party to the
3	hearing of an appeal before the Ontario Land Tribunal (OLT) unless, in
3	the opinion of the Tribunal, there are reasonable grounds to add the
3	person or pubic body as a party.
3	He indicated if a person or public body would otherwise have an ability
3	to appeal the decision of the Township of Centre Wellington to the
3	Ontario Land Tribunal (OLT) but the person or public body does not
3	make oral submissions at a public meeting or make written submissions
3	to the Township of Centre Wellington before the by-law is passed, the
3	person or public body is not entitled to appeal the decision.
3	Mayor Watters stated if a person or public body does not make oral
3	submission at the public meeting, or make written submissions to the
3	Township of Centre Wellington before the by-law is passed, the person
3	or public body may not be added as the party to the hearing of an
3	appeal before the OLT unless, in the opinion of the Tribunal, there are
3	reasonable grounds to do so.
3	Mayor Watters advised if anyone wishes to be notified of the decision of
3	the Township of Centre Wellington in respect of the proposed zoning by-
3	law amendment, a written request must be filed with the Municipal Clerk
3	of the Township of Centre Wellington, 1 MacDonald Square, Elora,
3	Ontario, N0B 1S0.
3	He advised if the official plan amendment is adopted, it will be forwarded
3	to the County of Wellington for approval. If you wish to be notified of the
3	decision of The Corporation of the County of Wellington in respect of the
4	proposed official plan amendment, you must make a written request to
4	the Director, Planning and Development Department, County of
4	Wellington, 74 Woolwich Street, Guelph, Ontario, N1H 3T9.
4	The Municipal Clerk advised notice was mailed to prescribed persons,
4	agencies and public bodies on or before March 20, 2025. She indicated
4	a copy was also placed in the Wellington Advertiser on March 20, 2025.
4	She further confirmed the requirement for giving notice was completed
4	on March 20, 2025.
4	Mayor Watters called on Brett Salmon, Managing Director of Planning
4	and Development to present the application. He outlined the legislative
4	requirements and changes to current regulations regarding additional
4	residential units (ARUs). He illustrated the various ARU configurations
4	permitted.
4	B. Salmon also outlined the M2 zoning permitted use changes,
4	indicating any M1 industrial zone permitted uses than are not already
4	permitted by the M2 zone will be added to the M2 zone list of permitted
4	uses.
4	Mayor Watters indicated there are no delegations registered and asked
4	if there is anyone present wishing to speak to the application. There
4	being none, Mayor Watters asked if Members of Council had any
4	questions.
4	Staff responded to questions related to short term rentals associated
4	with additional rental units, wording changes from accessory apartments
4	to additional residential units, and the difference between garden suites
4	and additional residential units.
4	Mayor Watters thanked everyone for attending and advised Council will
4	be considering the by-law later this evening.
4	Mayor Watters declared the meeting adjourned.

5	10. Delegations
5	10.1 Melinda Croft, resident and business owner expressed concerns with
5	the draft short-term rental by-law. She indicated she supports licensing
5	and regulating short-term rentals (STRs) but is not supportive of the
5	draft by-law under consideration. M. Croft feels the draft by-law is an
5	extreme transition from the current status and is not in favour of over
5	regulation. She is supportive of the revenue generated from licensing to
5	be applied towards parking, garbage, etc., and not staff resources. She
5	feels the regulations/inspections will result in a loss of revenue and
5	place a heavy burden on STR owners.
5	10.2 Mark Reilly, STR owner, Elora appeared in support of the by-law and
5	feels the Township should improve communication around the benefits
5	of tourism and STRs and monies generated should be put back into
5	community programming.
5	He supports moderate regulation and feels regulations designed to
5	control the bad behavior of some STR owners shouldn’t apply to all
5	STRs.
