2	8. Presentations and Announcements
2	8.1 Mike Schreiner, MPP Green Party of Ontario and Bobby Ann Brady,
2	MPP for Haldimand-Norfolk appeared to speak to their proposal to
2	introduce legislation to protect Ontario’s farmland through the creation of
2	a “Foodbelt”. He indicated protecting Ontario farmlands through the
2	establishment of an “Ontario Food Belt' would help create the conditions
2	for economic prosperity through enhanced food security and food
2	sovereignty during times of economic tariffs. It also ensures Ontario's
2	Agri- Food industry will continue to thrive and improve the health and
2	wellness of the community.
2	Mayor Watters thanked M. Schreiner and Bobby Ann Brady and the
2	following was put forward:
3	Moved by Councillor Bronwynne Wilton, Seconded by Councillor
3	Barbara Lustgarten-Evoy.
3	WHEREAS, Ontario farmers produce a wide variety of nutritious and
3	safe food for both domestic and export markets;
3	WHEREAS, Ontario, and Centre Wellington in particular, is home to
3	some of the highest quality agricultural lands and optimal agri-climatic
3	conditions and Ontario is currently losing approximately 319 acres (130
3	hectares) of farmland every day;
3	WHEREAS, we need to acknowledge that food security and food
3	sovereignty are both critical from a national security perspective and
3	noting Food Security is the ability of individuals to receive adequate
3	nourishment while Food Sovereignty refers to the ability of a country to
3	supply its own food needs;
3	WHEREAS, MPP’s Bobbi Ann Brady and Mike Schreiner have jointly
3	presented a Private Member Bill at the Ontario Legislature to protect
3	Ontario farmlands through the establishment of an ‘Ontario Food Belt.
3	WHEREAS, the Bill would make farmlands within a designated Food
3	belt permanently protected from development;
3	WHEREAS, the Bill is supported by the Ontario Farmland Trust, the
3	Ontario Federation of Agriculture, the Christian Farmers and the
3	National Farmers Union;
3	THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED THAT:
3	(a) THAT the Township of Centre Wellington gives it’s support to the
3	Bill’s intent for the Province of Ontario, to create an agricultural working
3	group to more fully outline the contours of an Ontario Food Belt;
3	(b) That the Township of Centre Wellington request, through our
3	representative at the Rural Ontario Municipalities Association (ROMA),
3	that ROMA support this Bill; and,
3	(c) That this decision be circulated to all other lower tier
3	municipalities in Wellington County as well as to the County of
3	Wellington.
3	CARRIED

4	9. Delegations
4	9.1 Erika Montero, former Chair of the Elora BIA, requested Council not
4	support the Elora Green Space Lighting proposal. She indicated the
4	project was privately funded for only 1 year and feels taxpayers should
4	not have to financial support this project. She also noted the
4	environmental impact of the excessive illumination.
4	9.2 Cathy Daultrey, business owner appeared before Council in objection to
4	the Elora Green Space Lighting proposal. She suggested the lights
4	could be used throughout the Township of Centre Wellington and not
4	only in the Elora Green Space.
4	9.3 Maclean Hann, Chair Elora BIA, noted after learning of the loss of the
4	private donor to fund the lighting of the green space, the Elora BIA
4	worked on communicating and building consensus from the business
4	catchment area whether to support lighting in the Green Space,
4	recognizing the significance of creating a seasonal atmosphere and
4	promoting tourism and economic development. He noted he was able to
4	garner feedback from the businesses and indicated the Board meets
4	next week where a wholesome discussion will take place to consider
4	funding a portion of the project this year and to perhaps find a
4	sustainable mechanism for funding in future years.
