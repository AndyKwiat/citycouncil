2	7. Matters Arising from Closed Session
2	7.1 Mayor Watters indicated a matter regarding a potential acquisition of
2	land was discussed in Closed Session this evening.
2	Moved by Councillor Jennifer Adams, Seconded by Councillor
2	Bronwynne Wilton.
2	THAT the Council of the Township of Centre Wellington authorizes staff
2	to release details of the acquisition of the property at 8055 Wellington
2	Road 18 in the form of a media release.
2	CARRIED
2	Mayor Watters indicated Council is excited to announce the Township
2	has purchased approximately 90 acres of land adjacent to the Centre
2	Wellington Community Sportsplex in Fergus for a cost of $4.8 million.
2	He noted the acquisition of the property at 8055 Wellington Road 18 is a
2	significant and welcome addition to our community and this land will
2	serve as future parkland and facilitate the expansion of the sportsplex
2	property, creating more space for recreation, events, and community
3	activities.
3	Mayor Watters suggested the timing of this purchase is ideal, aligning
3	with the launch of the 2024 Parks and Recreation Master Plan project,
3	currently underway, and will outline Centre Wellington's parks and
3	recreation needs for the next 30 years. He thanked staff and members
3	of Council for seeing the need to expand our parkland next to a well-
3	used integral recreational hub in our community. Event space, passive
3	parkland space, sports fields, and courts will all be needed as our
3	community grows.

5	11.3 Request for Proposal #06-24: Consulting Services - Assessment of
5	Options and Preparation of Technical Studies - Bissell Park Multi-Use
5	Surface
5	Report from S. Wiles, Purchasing & Risk Supervisor
5	Pat Newson, Managing Director of Community Services provided an
5	overview of the report, including background related to the project.
5	Adam McNabb, Managing Director of Corporate Services & Treasurer
5	provided an overview of the procurement process.
5	Staff responded to questions pertaining to the condition of the existing
6	surface, extent of the fund-raising, importance of unorganized play,
6	implications of adding another scenario to the request for proposal,
6	potential other sources of funding, timing of the project and the
6	requirement for GRCA permits.
6	Discussion ensued and the following was put forward:
6	Moved by Councillor MacDonald
6	Seconded by Councillor Adams
6	THAT the Council of the Township of Centre Wellington award Request
6	for Proposal #06-24 / Bissell Park - Consulting Services - Assessment of
6	Options and Preparation of Technical Studies - Bissell Park Multi-Use
6	Surface to ATA Architects Inc. at a contract price of $89,670.00,
6	excluding HST.
6	Councillor Wilton proposed the following amendment:
6	THAT Council direct staff to cancel the current RFP due to increasing
6	costs, scope and unknown future staffing and operating budget impacts;
6	AND THAT staff bring a report back on the best next steps to replace
6	the existing multi use pad with an inclusive and accessible alternative
6	that is in keeping with the original spirit of the Jack MacDonald funding
6	by the end of August 2024;
6	AND THAT the budget for the new multi use pad remain within the
6	currently approved budget of $418,000;
6	AND THAT the location of a refrigerated ice pad in Centre Wellington be
6	included for consideration in the current Parks and Recreation master
6	planning process with recommendations for the best location for a
6	refrigerated ice pad in Centre Wellington taking into account staffing and
6	operational resources and budget including ice resurfacing equipment.
6	The Chair ruled the proposed motion was not an amendment and
6	Councillor Wilton withdrew.
6	Deputy Mayor Lustgarten-Evoy re-read the original proposed motion as
6	follows:
6	Moved by Councillor Lisa MacDonald, Seconded by Councillor Jennifer
6	Adams.
6	THAT the Council of the Township of Centre Wellington award Request
7	for Proposal #06-24 / Bissell Park - Consulting Services - Assessment of
7	Options and Preparation of Technical Studies - Bissell Park Multi-Use
7	Surface to ATA Architects Inc. at a contract price of $89,670.00,
7	excluding HST.
