3	7. PRESENTATIONS/DELEGATIONS
3	a) Order of Canada - Mayor’s Congratulations
3	Mayor McCabe offered remarks and her congratulations to Dr. Donna Strickland and
3	Paul Born for their appointment to the Order of Canada:
3	We live in a special place. Waterloo is home to two highly ranked universities and
3	a college. We have a thriving business and technology community. We have non-
3	profit and community organizations that are second to none. We have a thriving
3	urban centre with award-winning architecture, unique shops, a vibrant arts and
3	culture sector and fantastic restaurants.
3	All of this attracts some of the best and brightest talent from across Canada and
3	from our neighbouring communities. They come to Waterloo to make their home
3	and their careers here.
3	Tonight, we’re taking a few minutes to honour three of the best and the brightest
3	of our community. On behalf of Council and staff, I’m honoured to welcome and
3	to acknowledge our recipients from Waterloo to Council Chambers tonight. Paul
3	Born, from Tamarack Institute and Dr. Donna Strickland, from the University of
3	Waterloo are with us tonight. Dr. Feridun Hamdullahpur, who was the University
3	of Waterloo's sixth president and vice-chancellor, is travelling and unable to join
3	us tonight. Hopefully he can join us another time.
3	For clarification, Dr. Donna Strickland and Paul Born were named to the Order of
3	Canada in 2019. However, they were only recently, because of Covid, able to
3	accept their awards. I felt it important to ensure, to welcome them to Council
3	chambers, that they know how honoured and excited we are as a city to have
3	residents like them in our community. Thank you for accepting our invitation to
3	join us tonight so that, on behalf of all of our 140,000 residents, we can recognize
3	your achievements. I’ll briefly introduce them and then invite them to come to the
3	front and provide a certificate, and then we’ll return to our regular Council.
3	So Paul Born: Paul has long understood that there is a direct correlation between
3	people’s sense of community and their willingness to improve the conditions of
3	those who live around them. He is a thought leader, founder of ground-breaking
4	organizations, and an early pioneer in the field of community engagement and
4	collective impact for good.
4	He is the author of four books, the co-founder of the Tamarack Institute and
4	Canada’s foremost expert on intentional and purposeful community engagement.
4	Paul and his work have focused on working collaborative with community to
4	reduce poverty, support youth and deepen community connectedness. His is a
4	significant contributor to the reduction of poverty of approximately 1 million
4	people in Canada, bringing poverty to the lowest level in our history.
4	Paul – Congratulations on your well-deserved honour.
4	Thank you for your contributions to our city and region, to Canada, the US and
4	beyond. And thank you again, on behalf of those you have helped to lift out of
4	poverty.
4	Dr. Donna Strickland needs no introduction. You would have to be living under a
4	rock since 2018 if you didn’t know that she was awarded the Nobel Prize for
4	Physics. She is a professor in the Department of Physics and Astronomy at the
4	University of Waterloo and was awarded the Nobel Prize for developing chirped
4	pulse amplification as a PhD student.
4	Together with her PhD supervisor, they published their Nobel-winning research in
4	1985. This discovery enabled further research into intense laser pulses - the
4	most intense ever created. We recognize this research and how it was applied
4	today in industry and medicine — including in laser eye surgery, and the
4	machining of small glass parts for use in cell phones.
4	In addition to her numerous awards, Dr. Strickland was appointed as a
4	Companion of the Order of Canada. This recognizes outstanding achievement
4	and merit of the highest degree, especially in service to Canada or to humanity at
4	large.
4	Dr. Strickland – thank you for finding time in your busy schedule to join us
4	tonight. Thank you on behalf of Council, on behalf of our residents and certainly
4	on behalf of our Country.
