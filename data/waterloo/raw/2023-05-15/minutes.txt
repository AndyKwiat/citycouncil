7	11. DELEGATIONS
7	a) All Saints' Anglican Church
8	Rev. Marty Levesque, Priest in Charge, All Saints' Anglican Church, requested that
8	Council provide a grant of $120,000 from the Council Community Priority Contingency
8	Reserve to fund the construction a new community centre at the All Saints’ Anglican
8	Church at 400 Northfield Drive West.
8	Rev. Levesque noted that the current model of providing City-funded community spaces
8	provides service gaps in locally accessible spaces. Rev. Levesque noted that the
8	Lakeshore area has a high proportion of the population that is low income and/or
8	comprised of visible minorities and that outcomes improve when community members
8	lead the development of programming.
8	Jane Mitchell, President, Lakeshore South Neighbourhood Association, informed
8	Council that the cost of renting space and transit time are barriers for the public to book
8	rooms at centralized community centres such as the Waterloo Memorial Recreation
8	Complex or RIM Park.
8	Rev. Levesque responded to questions from Council.
8	Mark Dykstra, Jim Bowman and Sanjay Govindaraj also responded to questions from
8	Council.
8	Councillor Bodaly left the meeting. (Time: 7:17 p.m.)
8	Councillor Bodaly joined the meeting. (Time: 7:19 p.m.)
8	Moved by Councillor Roach, seconded by Councillor Hanmer:
8	That Council direct staff to investigate and report back on the economic impacts
8	and equity, inclusion and programming considerations associated with the
8	request from All Saints’ Anglican Church.
