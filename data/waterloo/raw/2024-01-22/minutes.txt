4	8. NOTICE OF MOTION
4	Social and Economic Prosperity Review Motion – Councillor Freeman
4	Motion to be tabled January 22, 2024 for Council consideration January 29,
4	2024.
4	Motion:
5	WHEREAS Current provincial-municipal fiscal arrangement are undermining
5	Ontario’s economic prosperity and quality of life; and,
5	WHEREAS nearly a third of municipal spending in Ontario is for services in areas
5	of provincial responsibility and expenditures are outpacing provincial
5	contributions by nearly $4 billion a year;
5	WHEREAS municipal revenues, such as property taxes, do not grow with
5	economy or inflation; and,
5	WHEREAS unprecedented population growth and the clear need for more
5	affordable, attainable and sustainable housing will require significant investments
5	in municipal infrastructure; and
5	WHEREAS municipalities are being asked to take on complex health and social
5	challenges such as homelessness, the mental health and addictions crisis and
5	support for asylum seekers and newcomers; and,
5	WHEREAS inflation, rising interest rates, and provincial policy decisions are
5	sharply constraining municipal fiscal capacity; and
5	WHEREAS property taxpayers, including those on fixed incomes, seniors, small
5	businesses and more cannot afford to subsidize income re-distribution programs;
5	and
5	WHEREAS the province can, and should, invest more in the prosperity of
5	communities across Ontario; and
5	WHEREAS municipalities and the provincial government have a strong history of
5	collaboration.
5	NOW THEREFORE BE IT RESOLVED that:
5	1) The Province of Ontario commit to undertaking with the Association of
5	Municipalities of Ontario a comprehensive social and economic prosperity
5	review to promote the stability and sustainability of municipal finances
5	across Ontario;
5	2) a copy of this resolution be sent to the Premier of Ontario, local area
5	MPPs and the Association of Municipalities of Ontario for further
5	consideration.
