3	6. NOTICE OF MOTION
3	Combatting Hate in Local Communities – Councillor Wright
3	Moved by Councillor Wright, Seconded by Councillor Hanmer:
3	WHEREAS the Supreme Court of Canada has defined “hatred” in the
3	landmark R. v. Keegstra case, as referring to an “extreme emotion that, if
3	exercised against members of an identifiable group, implies that those individuals
3	are to be despised, scorned, denied respect, and made subject to ill treatment on
3	the basis of group affiliation”; and
3	WHEREAS the Ontario Human Rights Code affirms the right to safety from
3	discrimination because of race, ancestry, place of origin, colour, ethnic origin,
3	citizenship, creed, sex, sexual orientation, gender, identity, gender expression,
3	age, marital status, family status or disability; and,
3	WHEREAS a recent Statistics Canada report shows that the combined census
3	metropolitan area of Kitchener-Cambridge-Waterloo is experiencing the highest
3	rate of police-reported hate crimes in Canada; and
3	WHEREAS Police-reported hate crimes have increased dramatically over the
3	past few years and continue to disproportionately target people based on their
3	race, ethnicity, sexual orientation, religion, gender identity, and disabilities; and
3	WHEREAS 35% of the local population is racialized; and
3	WHEREAS the Coalition of Muslim Women K-W’s online reporting tool
3	ReportHate.ca acts as a portal to support the reporting of hate and provide
3	resources to local residents experiencing hate incidents; and
4	WHEREAS the 2019 General Social Survey on Victimization identified that
4	police-reported hate crime increases because of the general awareness among
4	the community and the relationship between the community and police services
4	can impact whether a hate crime comes to the attention of the police; and
4	WHEREAS municipal cooperation across the Region of Waterloo is vital to the
4	community-wide efforts to combat hate and racism; and
4	WHEREAS work has been long underway across the broader community by
4	many organizations and grassroots initiatives to encourage a sense of belonging
4	and support local residents experiencing hate incidents and racism; and
4	WHEREAS Canada’s Action Plan on Combatting Hate has committed $273.6
4	million over six years, and $29.3 million ongoing, in part to fund grassroot
4	organizations for capacity building; fund awareness, public education, and media
4	campaigns that showcase positive and authentic narratives of affected
4	communities; and enhance federal, provincial, territorial and municipal
4	cooperation to tackle hate; and
4	WHEREAS The Ontario government is investing more than $1.7 million to help
4	stop hate-motivated crimes across the province through a two year renewal of
4	the Safer and Vital Communities (SVC) Grant program; and
4	WHEREAS the Region of Waterloo, in partnership with numerous community
4	organizations and the Waterloo Regional Police Service is leading the
4	development of the Community Safety and Wellbeing Plan; and
4	WHEREAS the City of Waterloo’s Neighbourhoods Team has published a
4	Responding to Hate Motivated Incidents guide in consultation with community
4	partners including Community Justice Initiatives, Coalition of Muslim Women K-W
4	and Waterloo Regional Police Services; and
4	WHEREAS the City of Waterloo is finalizing the “Get READI Plan” which focuses
4	on improving Indigenous relations and fostering Reconciliation; enhancing the
4	built environment; cultivating a culture of belonging; increasing diversity of staff
4	and leadership; focusing on equitable distribution of resources; and building trust
4	through systems of accountability;
4	NOW THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED THAT the City of Waterloo reaffirms its
4	condemnation of racism and hate-motivated incidents;
4	AND commits to prioritizing a preventative and community-led approach to
4	combating racism and hate, internally and in collaboration with external partners;
4	AND commits to support the work underway by all staff led by its READI and
4	Neighbourhoods teams;
5	AND commits to collaborate with local organizations to amplify and enhance the
5	work they are undertaking to combat hate and promote belonging;
5	AND that this Council calls on the residents of the Region of Waterloo to join us
5	in the work to oppose racism and hate and in fostering a sense of belonging for
5	all community members;
5	AND FURTHER THAT a copy of this motion be sent to local and area MPs,
5	MPPs and Heads of Council, AMO and the Presidents of Conestoga College, the
5	University of Waterloo and Wilfrid Laurier University.
5	Carried Unanimously
