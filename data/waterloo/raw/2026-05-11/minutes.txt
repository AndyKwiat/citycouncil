9	12. NEW BUSINESS
9	Councillor Vasic attended the Button Factory’s Bloom & Grow Open House fundraiser
9	on Saturday, May 9. She mentioned that it’s been a long road for the Button Factory,
9	with the lease and accessibility upgrades, but that they and City staff have worked hard
9	to get them where they are. The programming is taking off, there are accessible
9	washrooms upstairs, and it’s really starting to have a community arts centre feel. They
9	are currently fundraising for theatre space on the main floor, and the silent auction is still
9	open.
9	Councillor Vasic also mentioned that the KW Water Polo Teams, the Kraken, did really
9	great in the last few weeks. The 16U Girls team played at Nationals, May 1-3, and
9	placed sixth overall, and second for Ontario. This past weekend, May 8-10, the 10U and
9	12U teams had their Provincials, and played really well. The 10U were enthusiastic and
9	placed sixth, while the 12U Girls team received silver and the 12U Coed team received
9	bronze.
9	Councillor Roe attended the Reception House Waterloo Region recognition banquet on
9	Wednesday, May 6, in the evening. It was really fantastic to recognize community
9	organizations and faith groups that support Reception House and the work they do, as
9	well as the incredible contribution that newcomers make to our community.
9	Councillor Hanmer attended the Adventure 4 Change Celebration gala along with
9	Councillor Roach, which was a celebration of newcomers and entrepreneurs. They
9	talked about what started as a women’s sewing circle that’s now a thriving business
9	operating out of St. Jacobs and their own site. It’s been great seeing the community
9	support for the organization.
9	Councillor Wright mentioned that she had been to the Grand Philharmonic Choir’s
9	fundraising event on the weekend, a walk and run in Waterloo Park. They exceeded
9	their fundraising goal before the run/walk began, which was really encouraging.
9	Councillor Wright also gave a shoutout to the U14 local basketball teams, who were in
9	Ottawa the weekend before last for Provincials. Kitchener and Waterloo faced off before
9	the championship round, but the Waterloo Wildhawk eventually won bronze.
