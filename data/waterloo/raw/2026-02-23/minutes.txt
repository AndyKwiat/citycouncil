7	12. CONSIDERATION OF NOTICE OF MOTION GIVEN AT PREVIOUS MEETING
7	a) Mayor Dorothy McCabe – Provincial Plan for Consolidation of
7	Ontario’s Conservation Authorities
7	Motion tabled February 9, 2026 for Council consideration February 23, 2026.
7	Mayor McCabe left the Chair. (Time: 7:17 p.m.)
7	Councillor Roe in the Chair. (Time: 7:17 p.m.)
7	Moved by Mayor McCabe, Seconded by Councillor Bodaly:
7	WHEREAS the Conservation Authorities Act, 1990 (the “Act”), originally
7	enacted in 1946, was established to allow municipalities to form
7	conservation authorities equipped to deliver local, watershed-based
7	conservation, restoration, and natural resource management programs on
7	behalf of both the province and municipalities; and,
7	WHEREAS Ontario’s thirty-six (36) conservation authorities are each
7	distinct, reflecting the unique environmental, geographic, and community
7	needs of their respective watersheds; and,
7	WHEREAS municipalities have long relied on their conservation
7	authorities to tailor programs to local watershed priorities while ensuring
7	fair and transparent costs for ratepayers; and,
7	WHEREAS municipalities are best positioned to understand the needs of
7	their local communities and environments, and the existing watershed-
7	based structure enables effective, collaborative approaches to water
7	conservation and resource management that cannot be replicated through
7	centralized oversight; and,
8	WHEREAS the Province of Ontario has not provided any information to
8	municipalities regarding the financial and operational impact of the
8	consolidation of Conservation Authorities to municipalities nor the
8	conservation authorities; and,
8	WHEREAS Bill 68 (Schedule 3) and ERO Posting 025-1257 propose
8	consolidating Ontario’s 36 conservation authorities into seven regional
8	authorities and creating the Ontario Provincial Conservation Agency, a
8	Crown agency that would assume oversight and possess the authority to
8	levy conservation authorities for its costs meaning municipalities may
8	ultimately contribute all or a significant share of OPCA’s budget without
8	having any meaningful role in shaping its governance, priorities, or
8	strategic direction.
8	NOW THEREFORE BE IT RESOLVED:
8	THAT the City of Waterloo Council requests that the Minister of
8	Environment, Conservation and Parks and the Chief Conservation
8	Executive meaningfully consult with municipalities including the City of
8	Waterloo to allow municipalities the opportunity to review the full financial,
8	reserves and operational impacts that could affect local control and
8	management of the floodplain areas watersheds and natural resources
8	which Conservation Authorities were established to manage;
8	AND THAT the Minister of Environment, Conservation and Parks and the
8	Chief Conservation Executive meet with the GRCA leadership to consult
8	regarding the regional model proposed which more closely aligns with
8	existing Source Protection Regions;
8	AND THAT a copy of this motion be forwarded to The Honourable Doug
8	Ford, Premier of Ontario; The Honourable Todd McCarthy, Minister of
8	Environment, Conservation and Parks; local and area MPPs; the Grand
8	River Conservation Authority (GRCA); the Association of Municipalities of
8	Ontario (AMO); the Association of Municipal Managers, Clerks and
8	Treasurers of Ontario (AMCTO); and to the Region of Waterloo and local
8	area municipalities.
