1	2. MOMENT OF REFLECTION
1	Mayor McCabe provided Council with moments of reflection.
1	At the beginning of this Council meeting, we pause to think about the needs of our
1	community. May we show wisdom and compassion in all our decisions.
1	a) Eid Mubarak to all who celebrate! On April 21, Muslims around the world
1	came together to celebrate Eid al Fitr, marking the end of the holy month
1	of Ramadan.
1	Members of the local Muslim community came together in a City of
1	Waterloo space to celebrate Eid al Fitr on April 21. We look forward to
2	continuing this tradition with our Muslim colleagues, family, friends, and
2	neighbours.
2	b) The first week of May has been recognized as mental health week for
2	more than 70 years. The Canadian Mental Health Association has led
2	these efforts to bring awareness to mental health, challenge societal
2	beliefs and reduce stigma.
2	We each feel the effects of mental health differently. Together we are
2	learning more about how the many aspects of our identity, including race,
2	ability, class and age, have an impact on our experiences and therefore
2	our mental health. Historical and ongoing impacts of oppression and
2	trauma have a significant cumulative effect on mental health and
2	wellbeing. We must ensure that supports and treatment that take into
2	account the different aspects of identity and acknowledge the harmful
2	effects of discrimination and inequities.
2	While there is still much work to be done, it is important to acknowledge
2	the work of the many people in our community who work hard to provide
2	compassionate, culturally relevant and trauma-informed mental health
2	services. Today we acknowledge Camino Wellbeing and Mental Health, a
2	newly formed organization that unites existing services to better serve our
2	community.
2	As Mental Health Week approaches, let’s commit to creating a community
2	that prioritizes good mental health for all by stamping out all forms of
2	oppression and ensuring that people have access to the supports and
2	services they need.

8	a) Mayor McCabe – Bill 5 Stopping Harassment and Abuse by Local Leaders
8	Moved by Councillor Vasic, seconded by Councillor Hanmer:
8	Request to waive Notice of Motion to consider the following at the April 24, 2023
8	Meeting of Council.
8	Carried Unanimously
9	Moved by Mayor McCabe, seconded by Councillor Vasic:
9	WHEREAS the Province undertook consultations in 2021 to obtain
9	feedback on how to strengthen municipal codes of conduct; and,
9	WHEREAS the Associate Minister of Children and Women’s Issues at the
9	time was quoted as stating “Our government has been absolutely clear
9	that we will not tolerate workplace harassment or discrimination of any
9	kind”; and,
9	WHEREAS we were pleased to see the introduction of Bill 260 first in 2021
9	and then the introduction of Bill 5 – Stopping Harassment and Abuse by
9	Local Leaders Act, 2022 in the Ontario Legislature by MPP Stephen Blais
9	through a Private Members’ Bill on August 10, 2022; and,
9	WHEREAS THE City of Waterloo is committed to providing a workplace
9	free of harassment and discrimination and regularly reviews and reaffirms
9	commitment to corporate policies relating to respectful behaviour,
9	workplace violence, health and safety and is currently updating the Council
9	Code of Conduct; and,
9	WHEREAS the Respectful Workplace Policy H-002 states the “City of
9	Waterloo is committed to providing and maintaining a psychologically safe
9	workplace that is respectful, inclusive, and where all individuals are
9	valued. The City will not tolerate disrespectful workplace behaviour(s),
9	workplace harassment, including sexual harassment, and/or discrimination
9	from any person in a City workplace.”
9	NOW THEREFORE BE IT RESOLVED THAT the City of Waterloo
9	endorses Bill 5 – Stopping Harassment and Abuse by Local Leaders Act,
9	2022 which would require the Code of Conduct for municipal Councillors
9	and members of local boards to include a requirement to comply with
9	workplace violence and harassment policies and permit municipalities to
9	direct the Integrity Commissioner to apply to the court to vacate a
9	member’s seat if the Commissioner’s Inquiry determines that the member
9	has contravened this requirement; and,
9	THAT the Council of the Corporation of the City of Waterloo expresses its
9	support for Bill 5 and request the Government of Ontario to approve this
9	legislation as soon as operationally practical and that a copy of this motion
9	be sent to the Premier of Ontario, the Ontario Minister of Municipal Affairs
9	and Housing, the Association of Municipalities of Ontario, local and area
9	Members of Parliament and Members of Provincial Parliament.
9	Carried Unanimously
