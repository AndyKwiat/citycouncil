3	7. STAFF REPORTS
3	a) Title: Waterloo Park Plan Update – Project Launch
3	Report No.: COM2025-023
3	Prepared By: Anna lee Sangster
3	Anna lee Sangster gave a presentation with the brief history of Waterloo Park and the
3	Park Plan, as well as highlighting the launch of the Update to the Waterloo Park Plan
3	including the future community engagement, and the timeline of the upcoming plan.
3	Gordon Greavette, Chair, Waterloo Park Advisory Committee gave a presentation
3	thanking the committee for their accomplishments over the life of the committee,
3	thanking the staff for their advice and the enhancements made to the park, and thanking
3	the many past volunteers for their assistance in implementing the many objectives of
3	the plan.
3	Hannah Gardiner, Resident of Waterloo gave a presentation sharing their experiences
3	of coordinating events in the Park, and highlighted the importance of small scale events.
3	Hannah Gardiner also offered solutions to the issues they have faced in planning
3	events. Hannah Gardiner responded to questions of Council.
3	Moved by Councillor Freeman, Seconded by Councillor Roe:
3	1. That Council approve report COM2025-023.
3	2. That Council approve the release of the 2025 non-routine capital
3	funding for Waterloo Park - Master Plan Update project 250013
3	(ref#306) in the amount of $143,000 from the 2024-2026 Approved
3	Capital Budget and 2027-2033 Capital Forecast.
3	3. That Council approve the transfer $57,000 from capital project
3	150011 Waterloo Park Master Plan Implementation to capital
3	project 250013 Waterloo Park Master Plan Update.
3	4. That Council approve the dissolution of the Waterloo Park Advisory
3	Committee due to having met its mandate.
3	Carried Unanimously

4	8. PUBLIC MEETINGS
4	Informal Public Meeting
4	a) Title: Zoning By-Law Amendment Z-25-23
4	VanMar Developments
4	171 King GP Corp
4	167-171 King Street South
4	Prepared by: Wendy Fisher and Kaitlyn Bisch
4	Ward No.: Ward 7 – Uptown Ward
4	Kaitlyn Bisch gave a presentation outlining the proposed development, including the site
4	context, background information on Official Plan Amendment 58, proposed site plans
4	and renderings, and the requested zoning by-law amendments. She then responded to
4	questions of Council.
4	Paul Leveck, Representative, VanMar Developments 171 King GP Corp spoke about
4	the developer’s perspective on the project, including the appreciation of the scale of the
4	project and the people that it’ll bring to the core, as well as the importance of the
4	heritage building on the site. Andrea Sinclair, Partner, MHBC Planning continued the
4	presentation further detailing the development, including the site statistics, the heritage
4	considerations, and the potential amenities for the building. Andrea Sinclair responded
4	to questions of Council. Kaitlyn Bisch also responded to questions of Council. Paul
4	Leveck also responded to questions of Council. Vanessa Hicks, Associate, MHBC
4	Planning also responded to questions of Council.
4	Councillor Bodaly joined the meeting. (Time: 4:30 p.m.)
4	Bob Publicover, Resident of Waterloo spoke about the changes coming to Uptown, the
4	potential timing of the development, and whether there is a need for two towers on the
4	site. Kaitlyn Bisch responded to questions of Council.
5	Geoff Bell, Resident of Waterloo spoke about living in the Uptown core, the height of the
5	development, the need for housing that supports the needs of the community, and
5	concerns with wind speeds and noise surrounding the development.
5	Councillor Hanmer joined the meeting. (Time: 5:02 p.m.)
5	Councillor Roe left the meeting. (Time: 5:08 p.m.)
5	Igor Rayt, Resident of Waterloo spoke about concerns living in the neighbourhood,
5	specifically surrounding privacy, sunlight, and wind. Wendy Fisher responded to
5	questions of Council.
5	Brenda Dunbar, Resident of Waterloo spoke about the size of the building compared to
5	the neighbourhood, as well as the wind issues and the lack of landscaping at ground
5	level.
5	Councillor Roe joined the meeting. (Time: 5:11 p.m.)
5	As no one else was present to speak to the application, the Chair concluded the
5	Informal Public Meeting and indicated that staff will review the issues and report back to
5	Council at a later date.

5	10. NEW BUSINESS
5	Councillor Vasic wanted to highlight two Ward 5 businesses. She and the Mayor visited
5	Mamma’s Pizza on Lexington Road by Davenport Road, which is their new location. It’s
5	a woman-owned Toronto chain that has been running for more than 50 years. As well,
5	Snugglers Furniture on Weber Street North at Bridgeport Road East celebrated their
5	50th anniversary. It was a great celebration of local entrepreneurship.
5	Councillor Wright offered her thanks to the City for helping with the street closure on
5	King Street for the Mayor’s Oktoberfest Market on Sunday, October 5 in partnership
5	with the Uptown BIA. It was a hot day, but so many people came out and had a great
5	time.
5	Councillor Roe wanted to say kudos to staff for the amazing paving work they did in
5	Thorndale Park. It’s fantastic and the neighbourhood loves it.
