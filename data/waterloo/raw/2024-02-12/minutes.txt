1	2. OPENING REMARKS/PRESENTATIONS
1	a) Opening Remarks, Mayor McCabe
1	Mayor McCabe brought opening remarks. Mayor thanked staff, Council, the Budget
1	Committee members, Councillor Freeman for their work on 2024-2026 budget. She also
1	thanked the residents who participated in the budget engagement process. Mayor
1	stressed that a municipal budget process is a transparent process. Mayor McCabe
1	noted that she would not use strong Mayor’s powers for the budget adoption purposes
1	as she saw them as a measure eroding democracy and being disrespectful to residents,
1	staff and Council. Mayor McCabe stressed that 2024-2026 budget had been especially
1	challenging due to inflation and price escalation – pressures equally faced by business,
1	families and individuals. Mayor also noted that municipalities were experiencing
1	pressure of the downloading from the other levels of government and the outdates fiscal
1	funding model. Mayor McCabe called on the federal and provincial governments to work
1	with municipalities to create a new fiscal model. Mayor highlighted that 2024-2026
1	budget reflected the priorities set forth in 2023-2026 Strategic Plan. Mayor indicated that
2	Council must look at the big picture and balance community input, different perspectives
2	and information when making final decisions. Mayor punctuated that future Waterloo
2	requires investments to reduce greenhouse gas emissions from City buildings and
2	operations, increasing availability and supply of affordable and attainable housing,
2	continuing to address the infrastructure gap, and providing the services that make
2	Waterloo one of the best communities for anyone and everyone to work, live, play and
2	learn. Mayor concluded by thanking everyone involved in the budget process and
2	residents who shared their thoughts with staff and Council.
2	b) Budget Day Overview, Councillor Diane Freeman, Finance Liaison
2	Councillor Freeman opened by emphasizing that Council was facing tough decisions, when
2	municipalities were asked to do more for less, and noted that Council would not be making
2	these decisions lightly. She stressed that municipalities had taken on things that had been
2	the responsibility of other levels of governments, without a lot of revenue tools that
2	municipalities had in the past. Councillor Freeman promised that the City would continue to
2	lobby with Associations of Municipalities of Ontario and other partners a review of municipal
2	finances by the province. She spoke about inflationary drivers that put pressure on the
2	budget. Councillor Freeman recognized that 2024-2026 budget was not an easy one and
2	thanked staff for introducing upfront savings of $1,600,000 in efficiencies for Council
2	consideration. She then briefly spoke about the agenda for the day.
2	c) Menu Reductions Options Presentation, Brad Witzel, Director, Financial
2	Planning & Asset Management
2	Brad Witzel, Director, Financial Planning & Asset Management, spoke about goals of
2	2024-2026 budget and provided Council with an overview of the budget approval
2	process. He then spoke about reduction menu options for Council consideration
2	proposed to lower the tax value.
