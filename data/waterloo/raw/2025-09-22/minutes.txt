8	12. NOTICE OF MOTION
8	Mayor McCabe – Ontario Regulation 391/2, Blue Box Motion
8	Motion to be tabled September 22, 2025 for Council consideration September 29,
8	2025.
8	WHEREAS under Ontario Regulation 391/2, Blue Box producers are fully
8	accountable and financially responsible for their products and packaging
8	once they reach their end of life and are disposed of for ‘eligible’ sources
8	only; and
8	WHEREAS ‘ineligible’ sources, which producers are not responsible for,
8	include business improvement areas, places of worship, daycares, camp
8	grounds, public facing and internal areas of municipal-owned buildings,
8	and not-for-profit organizations, such as shelters and food banks; and
8	WHEREAS should a municipality continue to provide services to the
8	‘ineligible’ sources, the municipality will be required to oversee the
8	collection, transportation, and processing of the recycling, assuming 100%
8	of the costs; and
8	THEREFORE BE IT RESOLVED THAT City of Waterloo Council request
8	that the province amend Ontario Regulation 391/21, Blue Box so that
8	producers are responsible for the end-of-life management of recycling
8	products from all sources as stated above;
8	AND THAT Council request the support of all Ontario Municipalities;
9	AND THAT this resolution be forwarded to the Honourable Doug Ford,
9	Premier of Ontario, the Honourable Todd McCarthy, Minister of the
9	Environment, Conservation and Parks, the Honourable Mike Harris,
9	Minister of Natural Resources, the Honourable Rob Flack, Minister of
9	Municipal Affairs and Housing, to local and area MPPs, the Association of
9	Municipalities of Ontario (AMO), the Association of Municipal Managers,
9	Clerks and Treasurers of Ontario (AMCTO), and to the Region of Waterloo
9	and local area municipalities.
