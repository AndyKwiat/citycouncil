5	10. QUESTIONS
5	Councillor Hanmer directed a question to Mark Dykstra regarding the Fireworks By-law,
5	and the different initiatives the City has taken to reduce the instances of fireworks in the
5	City, including what the City is considering or might be considering going forward
5	regarding fireworks. She was also curious about a coordinated approach to
5	communication strategies regarding fireworks.
6	Mark Dykstra responded that it’s a cross-section divisional support effort, and that the
6	team has been doing research to see what municipalities across the country are doing,
6	to look at more stringent considerations around regulation. He mentioned that staff
6	could work with other municipalities if directed by Council, and that they have been
6	working with Communications on a strategy surrounding public safety and how best to
6	share that.
6	Moved by Councillor Hanmer, Seconded by Councillor Wright:
6	That Council direct City Staff to work with Area Municipalities regarding
6	updates to the Firework By-law, and report back in April 2025.
6	Carried Unanimously
6	Mayor McCabe spoke about the 2025 University Ave E project site and that the
6	deadline for submissions has passed. There were a number of applications, and the
6	project is possible due to the Federal Government’s Housing Accelerator Fund. She
6	asked staff what the next steps are because of the significance of the project.
6	Ron Ormson responded with an update on the projects happening due to the Housing
6	Accelerator Fund grant, including the Official Plan Review and 2025 University Ave E.
6	There are technical evaluations of the submissions that have to take place, and staff will
6	look at compliance with the City’s goals. After which they will report back to Council.
6	11. NEW BUSINESS
6	Councillor Vasic wanted to highlight two events. Textile Magazine held their first event:
6	Black Talk. It was a conversation between the authors Hanif Abdurraqib and Antonio
6	Michael Downing, and offered insight on the public record of Black Culture and Black
6	Communities. The MC of the event really thanked City and Waterloo Public Library staff
6	for the funding and also for working together with Textile, and how open, curious and
6	responsive staff were to activism and advocacy. She wanted to highlight that the City
6	should be proud of how well they worked together.
6	Councillor Vasic also mentioned the Planning Department and had a small story of good
6	customer service, despite how busy they are. The Planner Aminu Bello gave her a
6	heads up that there was an Informal Public Meeting coming up for something in her
6	ward, and gave her an opportunity to review it and ask questions in advance. She
6	offered her thanks to Planning for working so well with Council.
6	Councillor Wright wanted to acknowledge that Council did not sit last Monday,
6	September 30 for the National Day of Truth and Reconciliation. Many Indigenous-led
6	organizations across the community engaged with settlers to continue their education
6	and encourage commitment to action towards reconciliation. She attended the Sunrise
6	Ceremony at the University of Waterloo, and the opportunities to gather and commit to
6	action are so important.
7	Mayor McCabe spoke about the Giraffe Parade in Waterloo Park this past Saturday,
7	October 5 in tribute of Dr. Anne Innis Dagg, the renowned zoologist who was local to
7	Waterloo and worked at the University. The event was helped by City staff through
7	Community Services and the Community Grant Committee. Dr. Anne Innis Dagg wrote
7	60 journals on giraffes as well as a book on feminism in academia. TheMuseum in
7	downtown Kitchener has an exhibit called, “Giraffe: A Heightened Experience” that is in
7	honour of her and her research.
7	Councillor Roe mentioned that Saturday, October 5 was also the Ride for Refuge, which
7	is a ride in solidarity with those seeking hope, safety, and freedom when fleeing conflict
7	and persecution in other parts of the world. United College at the University of Waterloo
7	partnered with Reception House for a ride in Waterloo, and Compass Refugee Centre
7	had a ride at Doon Golf Club. Both were successful. Reception House brought 50
7	newcomers to the event, which was encouraging to see in a welcoming and supportive
7	environment.
7	Councillor Roach wanted to congratulate Waterloo local Steven Lorentz for signing with
7	the Toronto Maple Leafs.
