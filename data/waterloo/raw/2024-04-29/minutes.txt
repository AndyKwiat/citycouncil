8	12. QUESTIONS
8	Councillor Roe had a question for staff about the increase of “renovictions” and
8	“demovictions”, and whether the City has the right tools to protect tenants and the
8	existing affordable rental housing stock. She was wondering if staff are considering
8	renovictions in the rental housing by-law review.
8	Mark Dykstra confirmed that staff are currently working on it, including doing some
8	research and looking across jurisdictions at other municipalities. They’re working with
8	planning and seeing what can be strengthened in the by-laws around these topics, and
8	there’s more to come in the coming months.
