5	9. CONSIDERATION OF NOTICE OF MOTION GIVEN AT PREVIOUS MEETING
5	a) Solve the Crisis Campaign Motion – Mayor McCabe
6	Moved by Mayor McCabe, Seconded by Councillor Hanmer:
6	WHEREAS there is a humanitarian crisis in cities, large and small, urban
6	and rural, across Ontario. We need immediate action at all levels of
6	government, starting with the Province; and
6	WHEREAS the homelessness, mental health and addictions crisis
6	continues to grow with 3432 drug related deaths in Ontario in 2023 as well
6	as with an estimated 234,000 Ontarians experiencing homelessness and
6	over 1400 homeless encampments across Ontario communities in 2023;
6	and
6	WHEREAS the province has provided additional funding for mental health,
6	addictions and homelessness programs, it does not adequately address
6	the growing crisis and the financial and social impact on municipalities and
6	regions across the province; and
6	WHEREAS municipalities and regions are stepping up and working with
6	community partners to put in place community-specific solutions to
6	address this crisis, but municipalities and regions lack the expertise,
6	capacity, or resources to address these increasingly complex health care
6	and housing issues alone; and
6	WHEREAS this is primarily a health issue that falls under provincial
6	jurisdiction and municipalities and regions should not be using the property
6	tax base to fund these programs; and
6	WHEREAS there is no provincial Minister focused on this crisis leading to
6	unanswered questions that span over a dozen ministries, and a lack of
6	support to manage the increasing needs of those who are unhoused.
6	NOW THEREFORE BE IT RESOLVED THAT THE CITY OF WATERLOO
6	supports the Solve the Crisis Campaign;
6	AND calls on provincial and federal governments to commit to immediate
6	action to solve the Humanitarian Crisis that Ontario is facing as the
6	numbers of unhoused individuals and those suffering with mental health &
6	addictions grows exponentially;
6	AND that the province officially makes Homelessness a Health Priority;
6	AND appoints a responsible Minister and Ministry with the appropriate
6	funding and powers as a single point of contact to address the full spectrum
6	of housing needs as well as mental health, addictions and wrap around
6	supports;
7	AND request that the provincial government strike a task force with broad
7	sector representatives including municipalities, regions, healthcare, first
7	responders, community services, the business community and the tourism
7	industry to develop a Made in Ontario Action Plan;
7	AND that this provincial task force reviews current programs developed by
7	municipalities, regions and community partners that have proven
7	successful in our communities, to ensure that solutions can be
7	implemented quickly and effectively to tackle this crisis.
7	AND provides the adequate, sufficient and sustainable funding to ensure
7	that municipalities have the tools and resources to support individuals
7	suffering with mental health and addictions, including unhoused people
7	and those from vulnerable populations that may be disproportionately
7	impacted;
7	AND that this Council calls on the residents across the City and Region of
7	Waterloo to join us in appealing to the provincial and federal governments
7	for support by visiting SolveTheCrisis.ca and showing your support;
7	AND further that a copy of this motion be sent to:
7	● The Right Honourable Justin Trudeau, Prime Minister of Canada
7	● The Honourable Sean Fraser, Minister of Housing, Infrastructure
7	and Communities of Canada
7	● The Honourable Doug Ford, Premier of Ontario
7	● The Honourable Sylvia Jones, Deputy Premier and Minister of
7	Health
7	● The Honourable Paul Calandra, Minister of Municipal Affairs and
7	Housing
7	● The Honourable Michael Parsa, Minister of Children, Community
7	and Social Services
7	● The Honourable Michael Tibollo, Associate Minister of Mental Health
7	and Addictions
7	● Local and area MPs, MPPs and Heads of Council
7	● The Association of Municipalities of Ontario and
7	● Ontario’s Big City Mayors
7	● Mayors and Regional Chairs of Ontario
7	Carried Unanimously
