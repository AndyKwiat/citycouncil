8	6. NEW BUSINESS
9	Councillor Vasic wanted to highlight a new business by a first year University of
9	Waterloo Student called Just Barely Baked, and provided a treat for Council. They met
9	earlier today, and their story aligns with a book Councillor Vasic has been reading by
9	Robin Wall Kimmerer, an Indigenous environmental biologist. The book talks about how
9	the economy is based on transaction, and there is even an inherent reciprocity with
9	gifts. The student’s business model is based on connecting with people, information
9	sharing, and a kind of community.
9	Councillor Vasic also wanted to highlight an email they received from the THEMUSEUM
9	about a talk with Alex Sarian, author of The Audacity of Relevance, and president and
9	CEO of Arts Commons in Calgary. The talk is on October 21, 2025, and tickets are by
9	donation, or you can connect with the CEO if you want a table. He talks about the social
9	impact and importance of the arts, especially in times of crisis.
9	Councillor Freeman noticed that Engage WR doesn’t always include all the studies for
9	planning applications, likely due to their size. She wanted to know if that’s something
9	that can change, as she likes to read them before Council Meetings. Joel Cotter,
9	Director of Planning, offered to take this away and report back.
9	Mayor McCabe mentioned that Jazz Fest is this weekend at the Waterloo City Centre,
9	as well as Afro Vibes in downtown Kitchener.
