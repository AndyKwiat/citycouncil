4	9. NOTICE OF MOTION
4	Councillor Julie Wright – Support the Need for Provincial Action on Salt
4	Pollution from Road Salt
4	Motion to be tabled March 24, 2025 for Council consideration April 14, 2025.
4	WHEREAS road salt is a known toxic substance designated under the
4	Canadian Environmental Protection Act because of tangible threats of
4	serious or irreversible environmental and health damage from road salt;
4	and
4	WHEREAS salt levels in Ontario’s groundwater aquifers, creeks, rivers,
4	and lakes have increasingly worsened since the 1970s, seriously affecting
4	municipal drinking water sources and aquatic life; and
4	WHEREAS the City of Waterloo, in collaboration with the Regional
4	Municipality of Waterloo, is actively attempting to mitigate salt pollution
4	using existing policies, programs and site design measures that would be
4	significantly more effective with supporting Provincial legislation; and
4	WHEREAS the Ontario and Canadian governments have taken many
4	actions over the past 25 years including setting water quality guidelines,
4	developing voluntary codes of practice, signing the Canada-Ontario Great
4	Lakes Agreement, and holding workshops, yet still the salt problem
4	continues to grow; and
5	WHEREAS numerous situation analyses have recommended salt
5	solutions involving liability protection, contractor certification, government-
5	approved Best Management Practices (BMPs) and salt management
5	plans; and
5	WHEREAS increased numbers of slips and falls claims, and other
5	injury/collision claims related to snow and ice, are resulting in salt
5	applicators overusing salt beyond levels considered best practices; and
5	WHEREAS unlimited contractor liability is making it difficult or expensive
5	for snow and ice management contractors to obtain insurance coverage,
5	resulting in contractors leaving the business, thereby making it difficult for
5	municipalities and private owners to find contractors; and
5	WHEREAS the Snow and Ice Management Sector (SMS) of Landscape
5	Ontario is working with the Ontario government to institute a limited liability
5	regime for snow and ice management, including enforceable contractor
5	training/certification and government-approved BMPs for salt application;
5	and
5	WHEREAS many Ontario municipalities have Salt Management Plans, but
5	these often require updating considering improved science and better salt
5	management practices now available.
5	THEREFORE BE IT RESOLVED THAT:
5	1. The City of Waterloo urges the province of Ontario to work on a
5	priority basis with key stakeholders to develop limited liability
5	legislation, including enforceable contractor training and a single set
5	of provincially-endorsed standard BMPs for snow and ice
5	management; and
5	2. The City of Waterloo urges the province of Ontario to create and fund
5	an expert stakeholder advisory committee to advise the province and
5	municipalities on the best courses of action to protect freshwater
5	ecosystems and drinking water from the impacts of salt pollution; and
5	3. This resolution be sent to all municipalities in the Regional
5	Municipality of Waterloo, Association of Municipalities of Ontario
5	(AMO), local MPPs, Conservation Ontario, Minister of the
5	Environment Conservation and Parks (MECP), the Attorney General,
5	and Premier Doug Ford.
