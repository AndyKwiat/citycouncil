2	3. MOMENT OF REFLECTION
2	Mayor McCabe provided Council with a moment of reflection.
2	Each year on April 28th we pause at 11:00 a.m. to remember workers who have died,
2	were injured or became ill from their jobs. At the City of Waterloo, we honour the
2	memory of Don Klassen, a Building Inspector, who passed from a work-related injury in
2	1990; Grant Bettke, a Fire Fighter who passed from work-related cancer in 2015; and
2	Donald Schmitt, a Fire Fighter who passed from work-related cancer in 2025. We also
2	take this time to reflect on all former and current City employees who have been, and
2	continue to be, impacted by workplace injuries and illnesses. This day serves as an
2	opportunity for all workers to collectively renew our commitment to improving health and
2	safety, so we can prevent workplace injuries, illnesses, and fatalities.
2	Please join us in observing the Day of Mourning to remember those impacted by
2	workplace tragedy and to re-commit to improving physical and psychological health and
2	safety at the City. All flags at City facilities will be half-masted tomorrow from sunrise to
2	sunset.

5	10. CONSIDERATION OF NOTICE OF MOTION GIVEN AT PREVIOUS MEETING
5	a) Councillor Roe – Heritage Helping Housing Motion
5	Motion tabled April 20, 2026 for Council consideration April 27, 2026.
5	Kae Elgie, Government and Community Relations Committee Member, Architectural
5	Conservancy Ontario gave a presentation on proposed provincial grant programs to
5	help convert heritage properties into affordable housing units, which would fund a
5	portion of renovation costs for adaptive reuse projects. Kae Elgie then responded to
5	questions of Council.
5	Moved by Councillor Roe, Seconded by Councillor Vasic:
5	WHEREAS Architectural Conservancy Ontario (ACO) is the province’s
5	leading non-governmental organization dedicated to the conservation and
5	reuse of Ontario’s built and cultural heritage; through education and
5	advocacy ACO encourages the prevention and adaptive reuse of buildings
5	and landscapes of architectural, historic and cultural significance;
5	WHEREAS ACO has emphasized that strong heritage protections are
5	compatible with and supportive of efforts to address Ontario’s housing
5	crisis, particularly through the retention, rehabilitation and repurposing of
5	older building for new residential uses;
5	WHEREAS heritage buildings can help solve Ontario’s housing crisis by
5	offering solutions that are affordable, equitable, sustainable and uniquely
5	Canadian; including the repair and reuse of existing buildings which
5	provides faster, cheaper and a more sustainable path to creating
5	affordable housing and by enabling the delivery of homes faster than new
5	construction while also supporting a stronger jobs market;
6	WHEREAS older and heritage buildings represent an important and often
6	underutilized opportunity to create additional housing units, provided that
6	appropriate financial incentives exist to support renovation and code-
6	compliance upgrades;
6	WHEREAS ACO has proposed that the Province of Ontario implement a
6	new housing-focused “Heritage Helping Housing Building Grant” of $10
6	million per year to encourage property owners and developers to
6	rehabilitate and make adaptive reuse of heritage buildings that creates
6	new housing units;
6	WHEREAS such a provincial grant program would support municipal
6	housing objectives, help revitalize main streets, encourage private
6	investment, reduce demolition waste, and strengthen the economic and
6	cultural vitality of Ontario communities;
6	NOW THEREFORE BE IT RESOLVED that this Council request the
6	Mayor send a letter to the Hon. Doug Ford, Premier of Ontario, with copies
6	to the Minister of Municipal Affairs and Housing, The Hon. Rob Flack, the
6	Minister of Finance, the Hon. Peter Bethlenfalvy, the Minister of
6	Citizenship and Multiculturalism, the Hon. Graham McGregor and John
6	Ecker, Chair, Ontario Heritage Trust, requesting that the Province of
6	Ontario establish the Heritage Helping Housing Building Grant as
6	proposed by the ACO, and that a copy of this resolution be circulated to
6	local and area MPPs and heads of council.
6	Carried Unanimously
