5	10. NOTICE OF MOTION
5	a) Mayor McCabe - AMO’s “Municipalities Under Pressure” Motion (2025)
5	Motion to be tabled January 20, 2025 for Council consideration January
5	27, 2025.
5	Motion:
5	WHEREAS the Association of Municipalities of Ontario in partnership the
5	Ontario Municipal Social Services Association (OMSSA) and the Northern
5	Ontario Service Deliverers Association (NOSDA) collaborated with
5	HelpSeeker Technologies to research and release a comprehensive study
5	that reveals the massive growth of homelessness in Ontario, and
5	WHEREAS the City of Waterloo is a member municipality of the
5	Association of Ontario Municipalities and works in partnership with them
5	including having the Mayor as a member of AMO’s Board of Directors, and
6	WHEREAS this comprehensive report supports OBCM’s ongoing Solve
6	The Crisis campaign and its key requests of the provincial and the federal
6	government to address the homelessness crisis,
6	NOW THEREFORE BE IT RESOLVED that:
6	1) The City of Waterloo endorse the “Municipalities Under Pressure –
6	the Human and Financial Cost of Ontario’s Homelessness Crisis”
6	report and work in collaboration with AMO to advance the key
6	findings and recommendations of this report and OBCM’s Solve the
6	Crisis Campaign.
6	2) And that a copy of this motion be sent to:
6	• The Hon, Justin Trudeau, Prime Minister of Canada
6	• The Hon. Nathaniel Erskine-Smith, Minister of Housing,
6	Infrastructure and Communities
6	• The Hon. Doug Ford, Premier of Ontario
6	• The Hon. Paul Calandra, Minister of Municipal Affairs and
6	Housing
6	• The Hon. Vijay Thanigasalam, Associate Minister of Housing
6	• MPP Matthew Rae, Parliamentary Assistant to the Minister of
6	Municipal Affairs and Housing
6	• Local area MPPs and MPs
6	• Robin Jones, President, AMO and Mayor of Westport
6	• Karen Redman, Chair, MARCO and Chair of the Region of
6	Waterloo
6	• Neighbouring municipalities
6	• The Association of Municipalities of Ontario
6	• The Ontario Big City Mayors Caucus
6	Carried Unanimously
