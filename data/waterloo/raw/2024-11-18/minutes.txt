8	11. QUESTIONS
8	Councillor Wright asked staff about a potential vacant buildings policy, as the City does
8	not currently have one. She requested an update on it and the timeline. Ron Ormson
8	responded stating that the policy would be a joint effort between Building Standards and
8	Municipal Enforcement. He went over some of the risks of leaving vacant buildings and
8	mentioned that while the conversation had been on pause due to staff workloads, it
8	should be reactivated again in early Q1 next year.
8	12. NEW BUSINESS
8	Councillor Vasic had a chance to go to the Child and Youth Planning Table Conference
8	at the Kitchener Public Library the previous week, November 14-15. The topic was,
8	“Forward Together: Activating Change, Amplifying Belonging.” There were great
8	speakers, local and otherwise, speaking about youth belonging in the City and youth
8	engagement. She’s been thinking about what activates belonging, how overlooked
8	youth are, especially those who don’t play sports in facilities, and how we oversee
8	certain equity-denied groups. She offered thanks to the Child and Youth Planning Table
8	and staff that were in attendance, including most of the READI team and some of the
8	Neighbourhoods team.
8	Councillor Roach mentioned that this coming Saturday, November 23, the Lakeshore
8	South Community was hosting a free skate at Albert McCormick at 2:00pm. He reflected
8	on a friend whose uncle would have turned 100 this month, Father David Bauer, a
8	famous Waterloo resident who helped establish the men’s hockey national team.
8	Councillor Wright wanted to highlight that the Urban Canopy Tree Consultation was held
8	on November 19, at 7:30pm, at the Community Pavilion in the Waterloo Memorial
8	Recreation Complex. It is organized by Uptown West and brings residents who want to
8	be able to provide feedback on the City’s engagement on tree canopy. She also shared
8	that the Powering the Energy Transition event on the High Performance Development
8	Standards was taking place at the Walper Hotel in Downtown Kitchener on Thursday,
8	November 21 from 3:30-5:30pm.
