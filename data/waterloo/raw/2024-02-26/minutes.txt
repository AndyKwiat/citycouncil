6	10. NEW BUSINESS
6	Councillor Vasic spoke about Winterloo Lite in Uptown Square on February 17th, with
6	the Clay and Glass Gallery also hosting an event. The event was really animated, and
6	there were many things for people to enjoy, such as maple syrup taffy, hot chocolate,
6	skating and DJ King Kadeem. She wanted to highlight this event because Lumen, which
6	is put on by the same staff team, has been nominated for a Business Excellence Award
6	in the category of Service Excellence and Hospitality in Tourism by the KW Chamber of
6	Commerce.
6	Councillor Wright mentioned the Coldest Night of the Year walk on Saturday, February
6	24, in support of SHOW, Supportive Housing of Waterloo. She also mentioned she was
6	with the Mayor at Mount Zion Lutheran Church to celebrate Black History Month and to
6	connect with members of the Black community across the Region.
6	Mayor McCabe continued that they had a record number of walkers, over 240, and a
6	record amount of funds raised for Coldest Night of the Year, beating their goal of
6	$65,000 and raising approximately $80,000.
