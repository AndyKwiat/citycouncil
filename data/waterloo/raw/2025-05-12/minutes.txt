9	Councillor Roach congratulated Taylor Elgersma, former Wilfrid Laurier Golden Hawks
9	quarterback, who was signed to the Green Bay Packers. He is the fourth quarterback
9	from a Canadian University signed to the NFL.
9	Mayor McCabe highlighted some information from the Region. They’re currently working
9	on their long-term waste management strategy, which is a 25-year strategy that they’re
9	looking to develop. There are two virtual meetings on May 14th and May 15th. Residents
9	can check EngageWR for details about ongoing engagement during the summer.
9	Mayor McCabe also mentioned that the Children and Youth Planning Table and Smart
9	Waterloo Region are receiving pitches for the Youth Impact Project, for youth looking to
9	make positive change in their communities. Ideas can receive up to $5,000.
9	Councillor Roe mentioned that the Waterloo Region District School Board held a
9	multilingual speech competition this past Friday, May 9 at Waterloo Collegiate Institute.
9	The Mayor was also in attendance for a portion of it. It was exciting to see newcomers
9	engage in the competition, making friends and adapting to the school.
