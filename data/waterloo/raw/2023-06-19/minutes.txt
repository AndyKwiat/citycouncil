3	8. STAFF REPORTS
3	a) Title: Debt Policy Update
3	Report No.: CORP2023-018
3	Prepared By: Kim Reger
3	Kim Reger gave a presentation offering some context on the policy update, policy
3	revision items, and external vs. internal borrowing. She then responded to question of
3	Council. Brad Witzel also responded to questions of Council.
3	Moved by Councillor Hanmer, Seconded by Councillor Roach:
3	1. That Council approve report CORP2023-018.
3	2. That Council approve the following administrative changes to the
3	debt management Policy FC-009:
3	a. The per capital project debt limit of less than 30% of total
3	project funding be removed from the policy and that no debt
3	limit be placed on individual capital projects.
3	b. That debt be managed at an aggregate level versus on an
3	individual capital project level.
4	c. That the amortization period of debentures may not exceed
4	20 years, with 10 years being the standard debenture term.
4	3. That Council direct staff to utilize debt primarily for strategic
4	priorities including but not limited to; infrastructure renewal projects
4	and climate action projects.
4	4. That Council approve Enterprises be included in the updated debt
4	policy.
4	5. That Council approve inter-reserve borrowing as an additional
4	financing resource, in accordance with the administrative guidelines
4	outlined in the updated debt management policy.
4	a. Payback of Council approved inter-reserve borrowing is to be
4	facilitated through the Capital Reserve Fund for new assets,
4	the Climate Action Reserve Fund for climate change
4	mitigation projects and the Capital Infrastructure
4	Reinvestment Reserve Fund for rehabilitation projects.
4	b. Payback of Council approved inter-reserve borrowing is to be
4	facilitated through each respective Enterprise for Enterprise
4	projects.
4	c. That Council set an internal inter-reserve borrowing limit of
4	$10,000,000.
4	Carried Unanimously
