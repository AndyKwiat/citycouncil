1	2. MOMENT OF REFLECTION
1	Councillor Freeman provided Council with a moment of reflection.
1	At the beginning of this Council meeting, we pause to think about the needs of our
1	community. May we show wisdom and compassion in all our decisions.
1	On December 6, this past weekend, we remembered the 14 women whose lives were
1	taken at l’École Polytechnique in Montreal and we honour all those who have been lost
1	to gender-based violence.
1	This day is a reminder of the ongoing impacts of gender-based violence in our
1	communities. Women, girls, Two-Spirit and gender-diverse people continue to face
1	disproportionate risk simply because of their gender.
2	In Waterloo, we reaffirm our commitment to ending gender-based violence in all its
