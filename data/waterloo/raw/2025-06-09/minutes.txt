5	9. NEW BUSINESS
5	Councillor Freeman mentioned that on June 10 there is a public meeting regarding
5	changes on the Lexington Road corridor from Davenport Road to University Avenue,
5	especially regarding active transportation. More information can be found on
5	EngageWR.
5	Councillor Freeman also mentioned that she was concerned about wind impacts,
5	especially with the environmental implications of Bill 5. She asked if there was more to
5	comment on, and Mayor McCabe mentioned that staff had provided comments to the
5	environmental registry on Bill 5. Mayor was hopeful to work with the Elected Officials at
5	the Province on the environmental regulations.
5	Councillor Vasic wanted to recognize community and staff for hosting longstanding
5	events, such as KW Bookfest on the weekend of May 31. She offered thanks to the KW
5	Writers Alliance and the CreateWaterloo Arts and Creative Industries team, who do a lot
5	with very little. She mentioned the Croyden-Southwick BBQ in Ward 5, celebrating their
5	31st Anniversary, on the weekend of June 14-15. There would be a pig roast and
5	games. Councillor Vasic thanked the Lincoln Hights neighbourhood for the yard sale
5	that was also becoming a staple in the neighbourhood.
5	Councillor Freeman wanted to highlight the Service Centre Open House, on Saturday,
5	June 7. Councillor Wright followed up, mentioning that she stayed until they presented
5	the cheque to Food4Kids. It was the 30th anniversary, and it was a great opportunity for
5	kids and for the public to look “under the hood” at the City. The event raised over $2,000
5	for Food4Kids.
5	Councillor Roe mentioned the City Pride kick-off celebration in Waterloo Town Square
5	on Wednesday, June 5. She highlighted that in the times of concerning rhetoric, it was
5	great to be part of this Council and this City that prioritizes diversity and inclusion. She
5	offered thanks to staff for organizing it.
