4	Lot Land Matters, Inquiry Report and
4	Approval of Expropriation
4	Report No.: CORP2026-015
4	Prepared By: Christina Marina
5	Moved by Councillor Wright, Seconded by Councillor Vasic:
5	1. That the Council (“Council”) of The Corporation of the City of
5	Waterloo (the “City”), as approving authority under the
5	Expropriations Act, R.S.O., 1990, c. E26 (the “Expropriations Act”),
5	consider the Report dated December 24, 2025 issued by the
5	Ontario Land Tribunal (“Tribunal”) associated with a Hearing of
5	Necessity that was heard under Section 7 of the Expropriations Act
5	on December 13, 2024 in connection with the expropriation referred
5	to below and identified as OLT Case No: OLT-24-000819 attached
5	as Schedule A to this Staff Report.
5	2. That Council approve the expropriation of all right, title and interest
5	in and to the land legally described as:
5	PT MILL SQUARE AND PART OF ERB STREET PL 385 CITY OF
5	WATERLOO being Part of PIN 22411-0263 (LT) designated as
5	Parts 1 and 2 on the draft Reference Plan prepared by James
5	M. Laws of Van Harten Surveying Inc., Project No. 29240-21
5	dated March 13, 2024 and bearing revision date March 20, 2024
5	attached as Schedule “B” to this Staff Report (the “Lands”)
5	for the municipal purpose(s) of the advancement of a master plan
5	for the short-term, medium-term and long-term use and
5	development of lands at and in the vicinity of PIN 22411-0263, in
5	the City of Waterloo in furtherance of the economic and social well-
5	being and parking rationalization of the City, implementation of
5	community improvement objectives, realization of the Uptown
5	Public Realm Strategy (March 19, 2019), promotion of good land
5	use planning principles and Official Plan policies, implementation of
5	Provincial Policy Statement policies, facilitation of required housing
5	and considerations under Royal Assent of Bill 23 (More Homes Built
5	Faster Act), together with completion of any future acquisitions
5	and/or dispositions of land including transactions involving any or all
5	of the Lands in furtherance of the foregoing municipal purposes and
5	objectives.
5	3. That Council authorize the Mayor and City Clerk or their designates
5	to execute the appropriate Certificate of Approval in a form
5	satisfactory to the City Solicitor.
5	4. That Council authorize the City to take all necessary steps to
5	complete the expropriation and comply with the Expropriations Act
5	including, but not limited to:
6	a. cause the decision and written reasons for the approval to
6	expropriate to be executed by the City Clerk and be served
6	upon all necessary parties insubstantial conformity with
6	Schedule D to this Staff Report;
6	b. prepare and register in the proper land registry office a
6	Certificate of Approval, as necessary;
6	c. prepare and register in the proper land registry office a Plan
6	of Expropriation; and
6	d. execute and serve Notices of Expropriation, Notices of
6	Election and Notices of Possession, as may be required.
6	5. That Council authorize the City Solicitor, and/or designate, to
6	execute applicable documents and notices associated with the
6	expropriation including, without limitation:
6	a. Notices of Expropriation;
6	b. Notices of Possession;
6	c. Section 25 Offers of Compensation (and to obtain an appraisal
6	report pursuant to and in accordance with Section 25 of the
6	Expropriations Act in connection therewith); and
6	d. Applications or any other legal materials with respect to
6	securing possession of the Lands under and pursuant to
6	Section 39 of the Expropriations Act;
6	6. That Council authorize the City to serve Offers of Compensation
6	pursuant to and under Section 25 of the Expropriations Act in
6	connection with the expropriation.
6	7. That Council Authorize the City to pay compensation upon
6	acceptance of any Offer of Compensation.
6	8. That Council authorize the City Solicitor to discontinue expropriation
6	proceedings or any part thereof, in respect of the above described
6	lands, or any part thereof should it be determined in the City
6	Solicitor’s opinion that the expropriation is no longer necessary by

6	the advancement of the interests of the City.
