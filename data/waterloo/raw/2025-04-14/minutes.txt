9	11. CONSIDERATION OF NOTICE OF MOTION GIVEN AT PREVIOUS MEETING
9	Councillor Julie Wright – Support the Need for Provincial Action on Salt
9	Pollution from Road Salt
9	Dani Lindamood, Campaigns & Communications Director, Water Watchers spoke in
9	support of this motion, emphasizing the need for water protection.
9	Councillor Roe left the meeting. (Time: 6:02 p.m.)
9	Councillor Roe joined the meeting. (Time: 6:05 p.m.)
9	Joe Salemi, Executive Director, Landscape Ontario spoke in support of this motion,
9	providing information on the need for road salt management to reduce freshwater salt
9	pollution.
9	Moved by Councillor Wright, Seconded by Councillor Hanmer:
9	WHEREAS road salt is a known toxic substance designated under the
9	Canadian Environmental Protection Act because of tangible threats of
9	serious or irreversible environmental and health damage from road salt;
9	and
9	WHEREAS salt levels in Ontario’s groundwater aquifers, creeks, rivers,
9	and lakes have increasingly worsened since the 1970s, seriously affecting
9	municipal drinking water sources and aquatic life; and
9	WHEREAS the City of Waterloo, in collaboration with the Regional
9	Municipality of Waterloo, is actively attempting to mitigate salt pollution
9	using existing policies, programs and site design measures that would be
9	significantly more effective with supporting Provincial legislation; and
9	WHEREAS the Ontario and Canadian governments have taken many
9	actions over the past 25 years including setting water quality guidelines,
9	developing voluntary codes of practice, signing the Canada-Ontario Great
9	Lakes Agreement, and holding workshops, yet still the salt problem
9	continues to grow; and
9	WHEREAS numerous situation analyses have recommended salt
9	solutions involving liability protection, contractor certification, government-
9	approved Best Management Practices (BMPs) and salt management
9	plans; and
9	WHEREAS increased numbers of slips and falls claims, and other
9	injury/collision claims related to snow and ice, are resulting in salt
9	applicators overusing salt beyond levels considered best practices; and
10	WHEREAS unlimited contractor liability is making it difficult or expensive
10	for snow and ice management contractors to obtain insurance coverage,
10	resulting in contractors leaving the business, thereby making it difficult for
10	municipalities and private owners to find contractors; and
10	WHEREAS the Snow and Ice Management Sector (SMS) of Landscape
10	Ontario is working with the Ontario government to institute a limited liability
10	regime for snow and ice management, including enforceable contractor
10	training/certification and government-approved BMPs for salt application;
10	and
10	WHEREAS many Ontario municipalities have Salt Management Plans, but
10	these often require updating considering improved science and better salt
10	management practices now available.
10	THEREFORE BE IT RESOLVED THAT:
10	1. The City of Waterloo urges the province of Ontario to work on a
10	priority basis with key stakeholders to develop limited liability
10	legislation, including enforceable contractor training and a single set
10	of provincially-endorsed standard BMPs for snow and ice
10	management; and
10	2. The City of Waterloo urges the province of Ontario to create and fund
10	an expert stakeholder advisory committee to advise the province and
10	municipalities on the best courses of action to protect freshwater
10	ecosystems and drinking water from the impacts of salt pollution; and
10	3. This resolution be sent to all municipalities in the Regional
10	Municipality of Waterloo, Association of Municipalities of Ontario
10	(AMO), local MPPs, Conservation Ontario, Minister of the
10	Environment Conservation and Parks (MECP), the Attorney General,
10	and Premier Doug Ford.
10	Carried Unanimously
