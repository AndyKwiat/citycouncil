10	11. NOTICE OF MOTION
10	Mayor Dorothy McCabe – Explore the feasibility of building a High
10	Performance Aquatics facility with a 50 metre competition ready pool,
10	practice pool, appropriate training amenities and meeting spaces in
10	Waterloo with support from the Government of Canada and the
10	Government of Ontario.
10	Motion to be tabled November 17, 2025 for Council consideration November 24,
10	2025.
10	WHEREAS the City of Waterloo Council is committed to the health and
10	wellness of all of its residents by funding and operating recreation centres,
10	pools, parks, trails and active programming for children, youth and adults
10	of all abilities across the City; and,
10	WHEREAS thousands of residents participate in learn-to-swim,
10	recreational and competitive swimming programs, and current programs
10	are regularly at or near capacity; and,
10	WHEREAS the 50 metre pool at Wilfrid Laurier University - the only 50
10	metre pool in Waterloo Region - is nearing the end of its life and non-
10	student users make up 65% of the total hours booked; and,
11	WHEREAS the City of Waterloo’s Indoor Community Space Strategy’s
11	recommendations include that Council and staff prioritize “the construction
11	or facilitate the access to a new indoor pool”; and,
11	WHEREAS the City of Waterloo’s Recreation Services Division regularly
11	works in partnership with local swim clubs, the University of Waterloo,
11	Wilfrid Laurier and Conestoga College’s Recreation and Athletics Directors
11	and other recreation partners and affiliates; and,
11	WHEREAS building a new or renovating an existing aquatic facility would
11	enhance access to recreational programming as well as provide improved
11	opportunities for training elite swimmers in the City of Waterloo and in
11	neighbouring communities within the Region of Waterloo; and,
11	WHEREAS population and housing growth in the City and Region
11	continues to grow towards 1 million people by 2050 and the City must
11	continue to explore the need for new or renovated recreation facilities to
11	ensure adequate capacity to serve our current and future population; and,
11	WHEREAS a new high performance aquatic centre with a 50 metre
11	competition ready pool, practice pool, training spaces and meeting rooms
11	would significantly increase the capacity and availability of learn-to-swim,
11	competitive and elite level programming for city and regional residents and
11	post-secondary students while also increasing the opportunities for sport
11	hosting tourism for Explore Waterloo Region and local municipalities which
11	will generate a positive economic impact; and,
11	WHEREAS the City of Waterloo is already planning for and needs a new,
11	multi-use recreation facility in the west side of the city; and,
11	WHEREAS an Aquatics Feasibility Study was completed by Explore
11	Waterloo Region in 2023 and the information remains relevant; and,
11	WHEREAS provincial sport organizations and local municipalities need
11	financial support from federal and provincial governments to build
11	regionally significant sport facilities; and,
11	WHEREAS Ontario’s swimming infrastructure across the province is not
11	keeping up with population growth and the only 50 metre pool in Waterloo
11	and Waterloo Region is nearing its end of life; and,
11	WHEREAS Swim Ontario, the provincial governing body is very interested
11	in the development of a high performance aquatics facility with a 50 metre
11	competition ready pool, practice pool, training spaces and meeting rooms
11	in Waterloo or Waterloo Region; and,
12	WHEREAS the City’s participation in this vision will be contingent upon
12	confirmed financial commitments from the Federal and/or Provincial
12	governments and other like-minded institutional partners to ensure the
12	project is financially sustainable and regionally supported; and,
12	WHEREAS the federal and provincial governments have previously
12	provided funding opportunities for municipalities to build and operate such
12	facilities.
12	THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED:
12	THAT any potential, future indoor aquatic centre being considered in the
12	City of Waterloo be updated to include the capital and operating costs of
12	the development of a high performance aquatic centre with a 50 metre
12	competition ready pool, practice pool, training spaces and meeting rooms
12	that includes appropriate spectator seating; and,
12	THAT the City of Waterloo Mayor write to the federal Minister of Housing,
12	Infrastructure and Communities, the Secretary of State for Sport, the
12	Ontario Minister of Infrastructure and the Minister of Sport indicating that
12	the City of Waterloo is interested in discussing potential federal and
12	provincial funding opportunities to build regionally significant sports
12	infrastructure, including a high performance aquatic centre with a 50 metre
12	competition ready pool, practice pool, training spaces and meeting rooms
12	with spectator seating to allow local and regional athletes to train and
12	compete locally; and,
12	THAT the federal and provincial governments create and maintain a
12	robust funding stream to significantly contribute to the cost of the
12	infrastructure and operations of regionally significant sport infrastructure;
12	and,
12	THAT when exploring options for new recreational facilities on the west
12	side of Waterloo, staff consider developing a high performance aquatic
12	centre with a 50 metre competition ready pool, practice pool, training
12	spaces and meeting rooms and appropriate spectator seating
12	incorporating best practices in environmental sustainability, energy
12	efficiency and AODA accessibility best practices in the design; and,
12	THAT the City of Waterloo continue to work with all local and regional
12	sport organizations, all local municipalities in Waterloo Region including
12	the Region of Waterloo to address the need for regionally significant sport
12	infrastructure;
13	AND FURTHER THAT a copy of this motion be forwarded to the Right
13	Honourable Mark Carney, Prime Minister of Canada; The Honourable
13	Gregor Robertson, Federal Minister of Housing and Infrastructure; The
13	Honourable Adam van Koeverden, Secretary of State (Sport); The
13	Honourable Doug Ford, Premier of Ontario; The Honourable Neil
13	Lumsden, Provincial Minister of Sport; The Honourable Kinga Surma,
13	Provincial Minister of Infrastructure; to local and area MPPs; the
13	Association of Municipalities of Ontario (AMO); the Ontario Big City
13	Mayors (OBCM); the Association of Municipal Managers, Clerks and
13	Treasurers of Ontario (AMCTO); the Federation of Canadian Municipalities
13	(FCM); and to the Region of Waterloo and local area municipalities.
