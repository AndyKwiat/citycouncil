9	12. NEW BUSINESS
9	Councillor Hanmer gave a shoutout to the Hockey Helps the Homeless group who held
9	their annual tournament at RIM Park on Friday, October 24. They raised over $350,000
9	for six local charities who serve the homeless population. It was a tremendous effort,
9	and she offered her thanks to everyone.
9	Councillor Bodaly highlighted that the Vista Hills community rented the Forbes Hall at
9	RIM Park for Diwali. There were 100 people or more celebrating Diwali just from that
9	one community and he said that it’s always wonderful to see so many people come out
9	and celebrate their culture. Happy Diwali.
9	Mayor McCabe mentioned that her, Councillor Wright and Councillor Roe attended the
9	Spectrum Dance Party in the Waterloo Public Square with One Big Gay Disco today at
9	about 5:30 p.m. It was a wonderful celebration and she offered congratulations to
9	Spectrum for pulling it off so quickly. She finished with saying that hate has no place in
9	the Waterloo Region, and that Council supports and stands behind the 2SLGBTQIA+
9	community.
9	Mayor McCabe highlighted that Game 4 of the Major League Baseball World Series
9	would be broadcast tomorrow, October 28, in the Waterloo Public Square in partnership
9	with the City’s Economic Development team and the Uptown BIA.
