4	8. CONSIDERATION OF NOTICE OF MOTION GIVEN AT PREVIOUS MEETING
4	Mayor Dorothy McCabe – Explore the feasibility of building a High
4	Performance Aquatics facility with a 50 metre competition ready pool,
4	practice pool, appropriate training amenities and meeting spaces in
4	Waterloo with support from the Government of Canada and the
4	Government of Ontario.
4	Motion tabled November 17, 2025 for Council consideration November 24, 2025.
4	Dean Boles, CEO, Swim Ontario, and Aquatic Sport Council – Ontario offered personal
4	reflections on living in the Region and being part of the aquatics community, and spoke
4	about the reasons why the community and Region needed this facility. Dean Boles
4	responded to questions of Council.
4	Liz Akeroyd, Chair, Board of Directors, Region of Waterloo Swim Club spoke about the
4	importance of aquatics in their life as well as with their family. Liz Akeroyd spoke further
4	about the challenges with the aging infrastructure, and the opportunities that this facility
4	would create for people of all ages and abilities.
4	Leanne Dowdall, President, Waterloo Region Artistic Swimming Club spoke in strong
4	support for the motion, and offered some details about artistic swimming and the
4	importance of a more adequately-sized pool space in the Region. Leanne Dowdall
4	responded to questions of Council.
4	Allister Scorgie, Director of Sport Hosting, Explore Waterloo Region spoke about
4	hosting sport events and the infrastructure needed to do so. Allister Scorgie also spoke
4	about the challenges they face with hosting large aquatics events in the Region, and the
4	earlier feasibility study Explore Waterloo Region commissioned on this topic. Allister
4	Scorgie responded to questions of Council.
4	Councillor Bodaly left the meeting. (Time: 3:29 p.m.)
4	Councillor Bodaly joined the meeting. (Time: 3:32 p.m.)
4	Councillor Vasic left the meeting. (Time: 3:45 p.m.)
4	Councillor Vasic joined the meeting. (Time: 3:48 p.m.)
4	Tracy Miller, Incoming President, Club Warriors (CW) Swimming offered reflections on
4	their personal connection to swimming and the community, as well as the reasons why
4	a new facility would serve the Region.
4	Councillor Roe left the meeting. (Time: 4:02 p.m.)
4	Councillor Roe joined the meeting. (Time: 4:06 p.m.)
5	Peter Baxter, Chair, Kitchener-Waterloo Sports Council Facilities Committee introduced
5	the committee and spoke about the sport and recreation facility shortage in the Region.
5	Peter Baxter also spoke about the importance of a multi-sport stadium for the Region
5	and responded to questions of Council.
5	Councillor Bodaly left the meeting. (Time: 4:12 p.m.)
5	Councillor Bodaly joined the meeting. (Time: 4:14 p.m.)
5	Noor Bansal, Resident of Waterloo spoke about why the pool matters for athletes and
5	the community, as well as the limitations of the current pool availability. Noor Bansal
5	responded to questions of Council.
5	Jillian Fleming responded to questions of Council.
5	Moved by Mayor McCabe, Seconded by Councillor Hanmer:
5	WHEREAS the City of Waterloo Council is committed to the health and
5	wellness of all of its residents by funding and operating recreation centres,
5	pools, parks, trails and active programming for children, youth and adults
5	of all abilities across the City; and,
5	WHEREAS thousands of residents participate in learn-to-swim,
5	recreational and competitive swimming programs, and current programs
5	are regularly at or near capacity; and,
5	WHEREAS the 50 metre pool at Wilfrid Laurier University - the only 50
5	metre pool in Waterloo Region - is nearing the end of its life and non-
5	student users make up 65% of the total hours booked; and,
5	WHEREAS the City of Waterloo’s Indoor Community Space Strategy’s
5	recommendations include that Council and staff prioritize “the construction
5	or facilitate the access to a new indoor pool”; and,
5	WHEREAS the City of Waterloo’s Recreation Services Division regularly
5	works in partnership with local swim clubs, the University of Waterloo,
5	Wilfrid Laurier and Conestoga College’s Recreation and Athletics Directors
5	and other recreation partners and affiliates; and,
5	WHEREAS building a new or renovating an existing aquatic facility would
5	enhance access to recreational programming as well as provide improved
5	opportunities for training elite swimmers in the City of Waterloo and in
5	neighbouring communities within the Region of Waterloo; and,
6	WHEREAS population and housing growth in the City and Region
6	continues to grow towards 1 million people by 2050 and the City must
6	continue to explore the need for new or renovated recreation facilities to
6	ensure adequate capacity to serve our current and future population; and,
6	WHEREAS a new high performance aquatic centre with a 50 metre
6	competition ready pool, practice pool, training spaces and meeting rooms
6	would significantly increase the capacity and availability of learn-to-swim,
6	competitive and elite level programming for city and regional residents and
6	post-secondary students while also increasing the opportunities for sport
6	hosting tourism for Explore Waterloo Region and local municipalities which
6	will generate a positive economic impact; and,
6	WHEREAS the City of Waterloo is already planning for and needs a new,
6	multi-use recreation facility in the west side of the city; and,
6	WHEREAS an Aquatics Feasibility Study was completed by Explore
6	Waterloo Region in 2023 and the information remains relevant; and,
6	WHEREAS provincial sport organizations and local municipalities need
6	financial support from federal and provincial governments to build
6	regionally significant sport facilities; and,
6	WHEREAS Ontario’s swimming infrastructure across the province is not
6	keeping up with population growth and the only 50 metre pool in Waterloo
6	and Waterloo Region is nearing its end of life; and,
6	WHEREAS Swim Ontario, the provincial governing body is very interested
6	in the development of a high performance aquatics facility with a 50 metre
6	competition ready pool, practice pool, training spaces and meeting rooms
6	in Waterloo or Waterloo Region; and,
6	WHEREAS the City’s participation in this vision will be contingent upon
6	confirmed financial commitments from the Federal and/or Provincial
6	governments and other like-minded institutional partners to ensure the
6	project is financially sustainable and regionally supported; and,
6	WHEREAS the federal and provincial governments have previously
6	provided funding opportunities for municipalities to build and operate such
6	facilities;
6	THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED:
7	THAT any potential, future indoor aquatic centre being considered in the
7	City of Waterloo be updated to include the capital and operating costs of
7	the development of a high performance aquatic centre with a 50 metre
7	competition ready pool, practice pool, training spaces and meeting rooms
7	that includes appropriate spectator seating; and,
7	THAT the City of Waterloo Mayor write to the federal Minister of Housing,
7	Infrastructure and Communities, the Secretary of State for Sport, the
7	Ontario Minister of Infrastructure and the Minister of Sport indicating that
7	the City of Waterloo is interested in discussing potential federal and
7	provincial funding opportunities to build regionally significant sports
7	infrastructure, including a high performance aquatic centre with a 50 metre
7	competition ready pool, practice pool, training spaces and meeting rooms
7	with spectator seating to allow local and regional athletes to train and
7	compete locally; and,
7	THAT the federal and provincial governments create and maintain a
7	robust funding stream to significantly contribute to the cost of the
7	infrastructure and operations of regionally significant sport infrastructure;
7	and,
7	THAT when exploring options for new recreational facilities on the west
7	side of Waterloo, staff consider developing a high performance aquatic
7	centre with a 50 metre competition ready pool, practice pool, training
7	spaces and meeting rooms and appropriate spectator seating
7	incorporating best practices in environmental sustainability, energy
7	efficiency and AODA accessibility best practices in the design; and,
7	THAT the City of Waterloo continue to work with all local and regional
7	sport organizations, all local municipalities in Waterloo Region including
7	the Region of Waterloo to address the need for regionally significant sport
7	infrastructure;
7	AND FURTHER THAT a copy of this motion be forwarded to the Right
7	Honourable Mark Carney, Prime Minister of Canada; The Honourable
7	Gregor Robertson, Federal Minister of Housing and Infrastructure; The
7	Honourable Adam van Koeverden, Secretary of State (Sport); The
7	Honourable Doug Ford, Premier of Ontario; The Honourable Neil
7	Lumsden, Provincial Minister of Sport; The Honourable Kinga Surma,
7	Provincial Minister of Infrastructure; to local and area MPs and MPPs; the
7	Association of Municipalities of Ontario (AMO); the Ontario Big City
7	Mayors (OBCM); the Association of Municipal Managers, Clerks and
7	Treasurers of Ontario (AMCTO); the Federation of Canadian Municipalities
7	(FCM); and to the Region of Waterloo and local area municipalities.
7	Carried Unanimously

8	Crisis Services of Waterloo Region was on Saturday, November 22. They walked 6 km
8	to represent that, in Canada, a woman is killed by an intimate partner every six days.
8	They had roughly 300 walkers participate, raising awareness and contributing to
8	approximately $140,000 in funds. Saturday, December 6 is the National Day of
8	Remembrance and Action on Violence Against Women.
