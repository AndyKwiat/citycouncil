3	b) Title: Disposition of Part of Sunview Street
3	Report No.: IPPW2026-045
3	Prepared By: Joel Cotter
3	Moved by Councillor Wright, Seconded by Councillor Freeman:
4	1. The Council approve report IPPW2026-045.
4	2. That Council declare as surplus the segment of Sunview Street
4	(formerly Unger Street) located south of Seagram Drive as shown
4	on Map 1 to IPPW2026-045, legally described as:
4	UNGER ST PL 507 CITY OF WATERLOO BEING SUNVIEW
4	ST ABUTTING LT 25 & 29 PL 507; WATERLOO,
4	being PIN 22377-0164(LT) save and except a 3.048 metre widening
4	to Seagram Drive (the “Lands”).
4	3. That Council approve the stop up and closure of the Lands as a
4	municipal highway pursuant to section 34 of the Municipal Act.
4	4. That Council authorize the Chief Administrative Officer and City
4	Solicitor to prepare an Agreement of Purchase and Sale to convey
4	the Lands to Wilfrid Laurier University (“Laurier”) substantially
4	consistent with the following terms:
4	a) for the nominal sum of $2.00 plus transfer, administrative and
4	legal costs incurred by the City of Waterloo;
4	b) a condition that obligates Laurier, if it no longer has an interest
4	in the Lands, to reconvey the Lands to the City free and clear
4	of encumbrances and in a safe and usable condition for the
4	nominal sum of $2.00; and
4	c) the transfer of a minimum 750 square metres of land from
4	Wilfrid Laurier University to The Corporation of the City of
4	Waterloo for highway widening purposes, to the satisfaction of
4	the City’s Chief Administrative Officer, for the nominal sum of
4	$2.00. Further:
4	• the lands shall be free and clear of encumbrances
4	• all costs including reference plans and legal costs shall
4	be borne by Laurier
4	• form and content of all transfer related documents shall
4	be to the satisfaction of the City Solicitor
4	d) a condition that obligates Laurier to consent to the relocation
4	of easement WR833293 and the right-of-way as in Instrument
4	No. 1126672 if required by The Corporation of the City of
4	Waterloo, to permit the relocation and/or realignment of the
4	westerly driveway to Laurier Stadium, provided further that:
5	i. any such relocation shall constitute a relocation only, and
5	not a discharge or removal, of the easement and right-of-
5	way, unless otherwise agreed to by the parties; and
5	ii. equivalent or improved access rights are maintained at
5	all times.
5	For clarity of recommendation d.)ii., the relocated easement
5	and right-of-way shall maintain all existing functional
5	requirements of the existing driveway, including maintaining
5	uninterrupted fire route access to Laurier Stadium, in a
5	manner satisfactory to The Corporation of the City of Waterloo
5	and applicable fire safety authorities.
5	5. That the Mayor and Clerk be authorized to execute the Agreement
5	of Purchase and Sale and all other documents necessary to
5	complete the transactions, subject to the approval of such
5	documents by the City Solicitor.
5	Carried Unanimously
