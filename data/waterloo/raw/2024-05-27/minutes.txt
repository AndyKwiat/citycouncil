8	10. QUESTIONS
8	Councillor Wright asked if staff can consider the following: the City’s NetZero by 2050
8	commitment, the Corporate Climate Action Plan, the development of a Green Building
8	Policy, the upcoming rental housing report, and the ongoing urban forest management
8	plan consultation. Considering that, can we look at an all-city approach to extreme heat
8	in partnership with Public Health and emergency response partners? Tim Anderson
8	responded to say staff will take that one away.
8	Councillor Freeman wanted to thank the transportation team for their work in the
8	Colonial Acres area to resurface the roads throughout the subdivision. There is a large
8	gap between asphalt and the properties and she was wondering if there were plans for
8	the City to come back and reseed those areas. Bob Henderson replied that he believes
8	it’s the City’s responsibility to come back, but that he would look into it and follow up.
8	11. NEW BUSINESS
8	Councillor Bodaly wanted to thank the Vista Hills Community Association for the
8	community picnic they hosted yesterday, May 26. Neighbourhood events are a great
8	way to connect to the community and do some community building. It’s important to
8	recognize that many volunteers put these events together.
8	Councillor Vasic participated in the Easter Seals Dancing with the Stars event on
8	Friday, May 24. There were 250 people who attended and they raised $80,000 for
8	Easter Seals, which will send 32 kids to camp that is fully accessible. The event was a
8	beautiful representation of community and connection. Two dance studios took part:
8	The Underdog Dance Corp and TenC Dance Corp.
8	Councillor Vasic also wanted to highlight Green Light Arts and the production they
8	recently put on titled Late Company. Green Light often takes difficult subjects and
8	theatre and makes people think about them through different lenses to connect to the
8	story. Green Light Arts has been putting on shows for 10 years and is going to be
8	launching a fundraising campaign soon.
8	Councillor Roe wanted to highlight Special Olympics Ontario that came to town from
8	Friday May 24 to Saturday evening, May 25. She attended the opening ceremonies on
8	Thursday night which was incredible. The energy and excitement of the participants was
8	fantastic. She gave a shout out to all the volunteers who put this on and the Waterloo
8	Regional Police Services, who did a lot of work and really showed up for the athletes.
