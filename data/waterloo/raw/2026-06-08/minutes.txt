2	5. MOMENT OF REFLECTION
2	Councillor Roe provided Council with moments of reflection.
2	a) On June 6 we remembered Yumnah Afzaal, her mother Madia Salman,
2	her grandmother Talat Afzaal and her father, Salman Afzaal, who were
2	tragically killed 5 years ago while out for a walk in London, Ontario. We
2	also remember their young brother, son and grandson who were seriously
2	injured but survived. This painful anniversary is a reminder of the dangers
2	of Islamophobia as well as the racism, hatred and violence that our Muslim
2	neighbours continue to face.
2	As a City, we reaffirm our commitment to join with Muslim communities in
2	the face of hate, and to challenge racism and discrimination in all its forms.
3	b) This past Friday we opened the month of celebrations for Indigenous
3	History Month and Pride with our Beats in the Square community event.
3	Through music, dance, performances and community connection, we
3	celebrated 2SLGBTQIA+ people and Indigenous history and identity.
3	Waterloo celebrates Pride and Indigenous History month with the belief
3	that an inclusive city is one that recognizes differences and ensures
3	residents have equal opportunities and benefits. We support an enhanced
3	sense of belonging for all residents and a commitment to reconciliation.
