7	a) Title: Zoning By-law Amendment Z-24-20,
7	Inclusionary Zoning within Protected
7	Major Transit Station Areas, City of Waterloo
7	Report No.: IPPW2025-014
7	Prepared By: Douglas Stewart
8	Ward No.: Ward 3 (Lakeshore), 4 (Northeast), 6 (Central-Columbia)
8	and 7 (Uptown)
8	Douglas Stewart gave a presentation outlining the changes to the Zoning By-law as well
8	as the rationale for the changes. He then responded to questions of Council. Adam
8	Lauder, Joel Cotter and Michelle Lee also responded to questions of Council.
8	Paul Leveck, Urban Legend Developments, Resident of Woolwich spoke in support of
8	staff’s recommendations and offered additional context for the market. He then
8	responded to questions of Council.
8	As no one else was present to speak to the application, the Chair concluded the Formal
8	Public Meeting and advised the application is now open to Council for a motion and
8	debate.
8	Moved by Councillor Bodaly, Seconded by Councillor Vasic:
8	1. That Council not approve report IPPW2025-014.
8	2. That Council not approve Zoning By-law Amendment Z-24-20,
8	Inclusionary Zoning within Protected Major Transit Stations Areas,
8	City of Waterloo, as set out in Appendix ‘A’ of IPPW2025-014.
8	3. That Planning staff not be directed to continue to consult with the
8	City of Kitchener, the City of Cambridge, and the Region of
8	Waterloo to evaluate the timing of implementation of Inclusionary
8	Zoning.
8	4. That Planning staff not be directed to undertake an additional
8	market assessment no later than September 30, 2025.
8	5. That Planning staff not provide an update to Council no later than
8	November 30, 2025, with the results of the consultation and the
8	market assessment, and to provide recommendations on the timing
8	of implementation of Inclusionary Zoning.
8	And instead:
8	1. That Council receive report IPPW2025-014.
8	2. That Council approval Zoning By-law Amendment Z-24-20,
8	Inclusionary Zoning within Protected Major Transit Station Areas,
8	with modifications:
8	A. By-law Number 2018-050 is amended by striking out the
8	regulation in Section 3.I.2.c) and replacing it with:
9	Commencing on March 31, 2025, on a LOT that is located
9	within a MAJOR TRANSIT STATION AREA as identified on
9	Schedule ‘A2’ of this BY-LAW, a percentage of GROSS
9	LEASABLE RESIDENTIAL FLOOR AREA shall be set aside
9	for AFFORDABLE DWELLING UNITS in accordance with
9	Table 3C-2, net any rental replacement DWELLING UNIT
9	required under an applicable Rental Replacement By-law, with
9	the following exclusions:
9	i. Development for which a complete Site Plan application
9	is submitted prior to March 31, 2025, pursuant to Section
9	41 of the PLANNING ACT, provided that a building
9	permit for the development is issued under the
9	BUILDING CODE on or before March 31, 2027.
9	ii. The disposition of lands by the CITY.
9	iii. The disposition of lands by the REGION.
9	B. Section 3.I.2.e) of By-law 2018-050 is amended by deleting the
9	words “by by-law” therein.
9	For the following reasons:
9	• Councillor Bodaly believes that Council should move ahead
9	with Inclusionary Zoning now as there are uncertainties in the
9	market, and market feasibility is not guaranteed
9	• Councillor Bodaly’s alternative motion looks to tackle some of
9	the challenges faced by the development community to offer
9	some flexibility and incentives
9	• Councillor Bodaly looks to address some of the comments
9	received by the development community surrounding fairness
9	and certainty, and moving forward with Inclusionary Zoning,
9	rather than deferring it, would offer that certainty
