3	6. CONSIDERATION OF NOTICE OF MOTION GIVEN AT PREVIOUS MEETING
3	Mayor McCabe – Ontario Regulation 391/2, Blue Box Motion
3	Motion tabled September 22, 2025 for Council consideration September 29,
3	2025.
3	Moved by Mayor McCabe, Seconded by Councillor Hanmer:
3	WHEREAS under Ontario Regulation 391/2, Blue Box producers are fully
3	accountable and financially responsible for their products and packaging
3	once they reach their end of life and are disposed of for ‘eligible’ sources
3	only; and
3	WHEREAS ‘ineligible’ sources, which producers are not responsible for,
3	include business improvement areas, places of worship, daycares,
3	campgrounds, public facing and internal areas of municipal-owned
3	buildings, and not-for-profit organizations, such as shelters and food
3	banks; and
3	WHEREAS the province amended Ontario Regulation 391/2 to not expand
3	services for new public space recycling; and
3	WHEREAS should a municipality continue to provide services to the
3	‘ineligible’ sources, the municipality will be required to oversee the
3	collection, transportation, and processing of the recycling, assuming 100%
3	of the costs; and
3	THEREFORE BE IT RESOLVED THAT City of Waterloo Council request
3	that the province amend Ontario Regulation 391/21, Blue Box so that
3	producers are responsible for the end-of-life management of recycling
3	products from all sources as stated above;
3	AND THAT Council request the support of all Ontario Municipalities;
4	AND THAT this resolution be forwarded to the Honourable Doug Ford,
4	Premier of Ontario, the Honourable Todd McCarthy, Minister of the
4	Environment, Conservation and Parks, the Honourable Mike Harris,
4	Minister of Natural Resources, the Honourable Rob Flack, Minister of
4	Municipal Affairs and Housing, to local and area MPPs, the Association of
4	Municipalities of Ontario (AMO), the Association of Municipal Managers,
4	Clerks and Treasurers of Ontario (AMCTO), and to the Region of Waterloo
4	and local area municipalities.

4	7. NEW BUSINESS
4	Councillor Wright noted that we often talk about firekeeping when discussing the First
4	Nations presence in the community. She wanted to draw attention to the All Nations
4	Grand River Water Walk, which just concluded, running from September 21-27.
4	Waterkeepers are stewards who promote the sacredness of water, safeguard local
4	waters, and they play a really important role in the community, centring watershed
4	issues. Councillor Wright wanted to highlight waterkeepers and the work they have
4	done to raise attention to the Grand River over the last many days.
4	Councillor Wright also gave a shout out to William Martin, who has been attending the
4	chambers for the past two years, initiating conversations between local First Nations
4	groups and the local institutions. Seeing that no community event was booked in the
4	Waterloo Public Square to recognize the National Day of Truth and Reconciliation, he
4	organized an event in partnership with local First Nations groups and the Waterloo
4	Regional Police Service. It takes place tomorrow, September 30, at 2:00 p.m.
4	Councillor Roe wanted to bring awareness to Immigration, Refugees and Citizenship
4	Canada (IRCC) halting a program tomorrow, September 30, wherein refugees arriving
4	in Canada would be housed in hotels. With housing already precarious for everyone,
4	this adds another burden and barrier on newcomers to Canada.
