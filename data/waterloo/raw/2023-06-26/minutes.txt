4	Report No.: CAO2023-022
4	Prepared By: Sandy Little
4	Mayor McCabe offered introductory remarks.
4	Sandy Little offered further remarks, noting three key points: engaging with equity-
4	deserving groups, building on previous strategic plans, and making sure this wasn’t a
4	“one and done” approach. She wanted to make sure that they were having ongoing
4	conversations, and following an implementation process that was always evolving and
4	bringing people together.

4	including the Vision, Mission, Guiding Principles and Strategic Priorities. She discussed

6	d) Title: Winter Sidewalk Maintenance Service
6	Enhancement Recommendations
6	Report No.: IPPW2023-040
6	Prepared By: Bob Henderson, Robin Milne,
6	Nicole Papke, Cari Van Niekerk
6	Bob Henderson offered some introductory remarks to the report.
6	Daisy Arseneault, Resident of Waterloo spoke about the work done by the City,
6	reminded Council that the conversation around sidewalk snow clearing is citizen-led and
6	citizen-fueled, and spoke about the importance of equitable and inclusive active
6	transportation, and sustainability.
7	Leslie Maxwell, School Travel Planning Supervisor, Student Transportation Services of
7	Waterloo Region spoke in support of the recommendations provided, and the
7	importance of active transportation. She then responded to questions of Council.
7	Aswin Annamalai, Resident of Waterloo talked about how the report was a good start,
7	and asked Council to support the report.
7	Janice Jim, Resident of Waterloo thanked Council and staff for listening to the citizen
7	concerns, and mentioned the importance of an accessible City.
7	Craig Sloss, Resident of Waterloo gave his feedback on the recommendations before
7	Council. Some of the initiatives he recommended the City take on included: clearing
7	sidewalks on Regional Roads, and looking at whether clearing Regional Roads and
7	increased by-law funding could have their timelines switched. He lastly spoke about the
7	greenhouse gas emissions associated with sidewalk snow clearing, and the balance
7	between that and accessibility.
7	Bob Henderson, Nicole Papke, Brad Witzel and Cari Van Niekerk responded to
7	questions of Council. Julie Finley-Swaren and Mark Dykstra also responded to
7	questions of Council.
7	Moved by Councillor Hanmer, Seconded by Councillor Wright:
7	1. That Council approve report IPPW2023-040.
7	2. That Council approve the winter sidewalk service enhancement
7	recommendations as outlined in report IPPW2023-040.
7	3. That Council direct staff to add the winter sidewalk maintenance
7	service enhancements to the 2024-2026 operating and capital
7	budgets for consideration.
