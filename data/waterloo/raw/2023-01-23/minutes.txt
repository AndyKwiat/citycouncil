4	a) Title: Expanded After Hours Call Centre
4	Recommended Solutions
4	Report No.: IPPW2023-004
4	Prepared By: Leslie Hepditch
5	Leslie Hepditch and Sandra MacGillivray, Managing Director, Valency Inc. gave a
5	presentation on the recommended solutions for expanded after hours service. These
5	include a centralized after-hours call centre to handle urgent after hours calls, and
5	utilizing software already used by fire services to dispatch staff and reduce response
5	time. They then responded to questions of Council. Leigh McDermott also responded to
5	questions of Council.
5	Moved by Councillor Vasic, Seconded by Mayor McCabe:
5	1. That Council approve report IPPW2023-004.
5	2. That Council direct staff to report back by the second quarter of
5	2023 on funding options and next steps.
5	3. That the Final report from Valency Inc. be posted on the City’s
5	website by February 1, 2023, as per the Province’s Audit and
5	Accountability Fund requirements.
5	4. That Council direct staff to forward this staff report to the Province
5	of Ontario.
5	Carried Unanimously
