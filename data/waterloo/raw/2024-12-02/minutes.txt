2	4. MOMENT OF REFLECTION
2	Mayor McCabe provided Council with moments of reflection:
2	At the beginning of this Council meeting, we pause to think about the needs of our
2	community. May we show wisdom and compassion in all our decisions.
2	a) The International Day of Persons with Disabilities is a day for celebration
2	and reflection.
2	In our own community, we celebrate the 20th anniversary of the Grand
2	River Accessibility Committee (GRAAC). Members of GRAAC have
2	enriched our community by identifying and overseeing the removal of
2	barriers for people with disabilities in Waterloo and across the Region.
3	Their commitment to inclusion benefits us all, whether we are disabled or
3	temporarily able-bodied.
3	Beyond GRAAC, disabled people continue to advocate and educate. A lot
3	has been accomplished, but there is still so much to do. Today, we reflect
3	on the impact of ableism and the many barriers that continue to keep
3	disabled people from participating fully at work, school, play and in civic
3	life.
3	Today, we re-commit ourselves to the long-term work of creating an
3	inclusive and accessible community where everyone can participate,
3	contribute, belong and thrive.
3	b) On Friday, we will remember the 14 women who were killed at l’École
3	Polytechnique in Montreal 35 years ago and all those who have lost their
3	lives to gender-based violence.
3	This solemn anniversary is a time to reflect on the harmful effects of
3	patriarchy that put women, girls, two spirit and gender-diverse people at
3	disproportionate risk of violence simply because of their gender. We are
3	reminded that other aspects of identity further increase this risk. The crisis
3	of missing and murdered Indigenous women, girls, Two Spirit and gender-
3	diverse people has made this clear. As do increased incidents of violence
3	against young women, trans people and women with disabilities.
3	As a City, we re-commit ourselves stopping gender-based violence in all
3	its forms, and to creating communities that are safe for women, girls, two-
3	spirit, trans and gender diverse people.
