3	a) Title: Facility Accessibility Audits
3	Report No.: COM2023-009
3	Prepared By: Amy Ross and Swati Vekariya
3	Swati Vekariya gave a presentation outlining the summary of the work done for the
3	audit. She displayed a slideshow of the different facilities throughout the City, and the
3	accessibility needs for them. Amy Ross continued the presentation and outlined the
3	processes required to complete the work. She then responded to questions of Council.
3	Kevin Van Ooteghem also responded to questions of Council.
3	Moved by Councillor Hanmer, Seconded by Councillor Roe:
3	1. That Council approve report COM2023-009.
3	2. That Council direct staff to provide an annual update on the status
3	of Accessibility Improvements at City owned facilities.
3	Carried Unanimously

3	Request for Additional Funding
3	Report No.: COM2023-011
3	Prepared By: Swati Vekariya

3	methods, as opposed to traditional cremation and burial. Some of these include

3	questions of Council.

3	questions of Council. Sunda Siva and Kevin Van Ooteghem also responded to
3	questions of Council.
