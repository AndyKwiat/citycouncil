3	6. PRESENTATIONS/DELEGATIONS
3	a) Wampum Belt Presentation
3	Sohuwesyoklati (Brian Hill) offered his thanks to Council and the City and introduced
3	Martin Powless, Knowledge Keeper and Wampum Belt Maker, and Rita VanEvery.
3	In the spirit of Reconciliation, Relationship Building and Indigenous History Month, the
3	City of Waterloo commissioned three replica Wampum belts. Kahlunyunti (Martin
3	Powless) of Oneida Nation of the Thames, the artist who recreated the belts, presented
3	the belts to Council and provided some teaching on their history.
3	The three belts are the Hiawatha Wampum, the Two-Row Wampum and the Dish with
3	One Spoon Wampum. These belts are a physical representation of the oral history of
3	treaties and settlement of the area around the Great Lakes Region extending along a
3	portion of the St. Lawrence River, which we all call home. These are significant
3	representations of our colonial history and are still seen to this day as a reminder to all
3	of us of the responsibilities we hold as treaty people.
3	b) Bike Theft
3	Philip Petrunak, Resident of Wilmot spoke about his experience of having his bike
3	stolen from the Waterloo Public Library main branch, and how widespread bike theft is
3	in the Region. He offered some solutions to this issue, including a partnership with
3	Project 529. He then responded to questions of Council.
