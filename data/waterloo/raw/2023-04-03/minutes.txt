2	5. MOMENT OF REFLECTION
2	Councillor Vasic provided Council with moments of reflection.
2	At the beginning of this Council meeting, we pause to think about the needs of our
2	community. May we show wisdom and compassion in all our decisions.
2	a) I wanted to recognize that last Friday was Trans Day of Visibility and also
2	World Indigenous Languages Day. As a Council, I think we recognize the
2	devastating, systemic reasons behind why these days exist and how much
2	better we all are when we recognize the strengths and contributions of
2	these communities to our society.
2	b) Canada is home to one of the largest Sikh diasporas in the world, we
2	recognize this significance through Sikh Heritage Month in April each year.
2	This month is an important opportunity for us to celebrate the many
2	economic, political, social and cultural contributions of Sikhs in our
2	community and across the country.
2	Later this month, on April 14, our Sikh neighbours will celebrate Vaisakhi.
2	Communities and families will come together to celebrate faith, heritage
3	and culture with services at the Gurdwara, shared meals, music and
3	dancing.
3	This month is an opportunity to reaffirm our commitment to equality,
3	inclusivity and generosity, learning from our neighbours about how to live
3	out these important values. Our city is enriched by the presence and
3	contributions of Sikhs. We strive to be a community that fully embraces
3	and celebrates Sikh contributions.

4	9. STAFF REPORTS
4	a) Title: 2023-2026 Strategic Plan – Draft Scan Report
4	Report No.: CAO2023-015
4	Prepared By: Sandy Little
4	Sandy Little gave a presentation outlining the results of the scan and engagement
4	completed from November 2022-February 2023. Some of the themes that appeared in
4	the scan include climate change, transportation, recreation, housing, and reconciliation.
4	The key findings from the Engage Waterloo surveys included being walkable and
4	accessible, affordable housing, and public transit and pedestrian infrastructure. These
4	findings were reflected in the Community Engagement Workshops.
4	Sandy Little detailed the recent and next steps for the Strategic Planning process,
4	including the public skate that occurred on the weekend, and the draft Strategic Plan
4	coming out on May 8th. She then responded to questions of Council.
4	Moved by Councillor Bodaly, Seconded by Mayor McCabe:
4	1. That Council receives CAO2023-015 as information.
