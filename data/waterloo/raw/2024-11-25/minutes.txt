7	10. QUESTIONS
7	Councillor Freeman asked to follow-up on some direction Council gave last year for a
7	potential banner program associated with veterans along Regina Street, working with
7	the local Legions. She was also wondering about the possibility of a Veteran’s
7	Crosswalk at Regina Street leading to the Cenotaph, as it would create a space that
7	represents and recognizes the veterans that have served year-round, not just on
7	Remembrance Day. Jim Bowman responded to say the City would reinvigorate the
7	conversation with the Communications Team and the museum surrounding the
7	banners.
7	Mayor McCabe also raised concerns she had heard from the Legion, surrounding
7	designated parking for veterans on Remembrance Day, especially for those with
7	mobility issues, close to the Cenotaph. As well, the communications available around
7	Remembrance Day should include that there’s seating available at the Cenotaph.
7	Tim Anderson responded about the crosswalk, saying that the City would take that
7	under advisement with IPPW, and report back once they’ve worked out details and
7	determined if there are financial needs. The intent would be to consider both for 2025
7	implementation. Mayor McCabe asked for the BIA to be included in these
7	conversations, especially surrounding the banners.
7	Councillor Freeman also had a question for Bob Henderson, surrounding the painted
7	edge along Lee Avenue with the intent of reducing traffic volume speeds, but the centre
7	line is no longer visible and she was hoping that could be reestablished. Bob Henderson
7	responded saying they would reinstate the line, but it might not be possible this late in
7	the season. In that case it would be scheduled for Spring 2025.
