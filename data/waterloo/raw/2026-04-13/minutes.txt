2	4. MOMENT OF REFLECTION
2	Mayor McCabe provided Council with moments of reflection.
2	At the beginning of this Council meeting, we pause to think about the needs of our
2	community. May we show wisdom and compassion in all our decisions.
2	It is with great sadness that we share the heartbreaking news that City Staff member
2	Steve Bairos passed away on April 1, 2026, surrounded by the love of his family, after a
2	battle with cancer. Steve was a good friend and colleague and was a devoted and
2	loving father to his daughters Maizie and Zoë. He was an incredible partner to Jessica.
2	Jessica Bairos shared the following with us: "We want to sincerely thank everyone at
2	the City of Waterloo for the kindness, compassion, and unwavering support you showed
2	him throughout his journey. It meant the world to him. Stephen was so proud and
2	honoured to work for the City of Waterloo, and he spoke so highly of the people he
2	worked with—it truly meant so much to him to be part of that community."
2	Steve, as a person and on his treatment journey, was a positive force in City Utilities
2	after joining the City in 2019 as a Wastewater Operator. He split his time between the
2	Wastewater Operations Section, where he enjoyed "...dressing up in hazmat and
2	rodding clean outs, even on [his] spare time....", and the Water Operations Section, as
2	Water Operator, where he gained notoriety through his handling of old school paper
2	document processing.
3	Steve will be greatly missed by everyone who had the pleasure of experiencing his
3	positive outlook, humour, and incredible strength. The booster juice will be
3	remembered.
3	CAO Tim Anderson and I attended Steve’s visitation last Thursday, and staff colleagues
3	attended his funeral on Friday. Flags at all City facilities have been lowered since
3	learning of Steve’s death and will remain lowered until tomorrow.
3	We extend our sincerest condolences to Steve’s family, friends, and co-workers.
