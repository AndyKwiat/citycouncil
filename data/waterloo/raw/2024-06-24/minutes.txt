2	4. MOMENT OF REFLECTION
2	Mayor McCabe provided Council with a moment of reflection.
2	At the beginning of this Council meeting, we pause to think about the needs of our
2	community. May we show wisdom and compassion in all our decisions.
2	The summer solstice has long been significant for Indigenous peoples on these lands
2	as a time to honour the sun and celebrate the warmth, light, crops and plants it gives to
2	us. More recently, all Canadians have been invited to celebrate the heritage, cultures
2	and contributions of First Nations, Inuit and Métis peoples on National Indigenous
2	Peoples Day.
2	Residents of Waterloo are encouraged to explore learning resources at the Library,
2	attend celebratory events and support local Indigenous artists, business owners and
2	organizations. Through action, we can create a community where Indigenous
2	knowledge is valued and respected, and where all Indigenous people belong and thrive.
