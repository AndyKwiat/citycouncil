2	4. MOMENT OF REFLECTION
2	Councillor Roach provided Council with a moment of reflection.
2	This Wednesday, March 8, marks International Women’s Day. Each year, this is a day
2	for us to acknowledge and celebrate the economic, political and cultural achievements
2	of women, girls, Two Spirit, trans and non-binary people. It is also a time to reflect on
2	the work that still needs to be done.
2	International Women’s Day is rooted in the fight for women’s votes, as well as the work
2	of labour and peace movements. In order to attain an equal place in society, these
2	women knew they needed a seat at the table. They knew they had valuable things to
2	say about creating fair and inclusive communities.
2	As we reflect on these early struggles, contributions and achievements, we must
2	acknowledge that there are many who are still excluded; their capacity for contributions
2	not fully realized. We must not allow difference to divide us, with some advancing at the
2	expense of others. It is imperative that we include trans, non-binary, Two Spirit people
2	and all gender minorities in conversations about women’s rights, feminism and equality.
2	The unique experiences of women and gender minorities who are also Indigenous,
2	Black, racialized, disabled, lesbian and of other sexual minorities must also be
2	embraced.
2	This International Women’s Day, let’s commit to building a strong, inclusive movement
2	that celebrates and realizes the full potential of all women and gender minorities without
2	question or judgement.

6	12. NOTICE OF MOTION
6	a) Mayor Dorothy McCabe – Provincial Government Call to End
6	Homelessness for consideration March 6, 2023
6	Moved by Mayor McCabe, seconded by Councillor Vasic:
6	WHEREAS the homelessness crisis has had and continues to have a
6	significant impact on individuals, families and communities, undermining a
6	healthy and prosperous Ontario; and,
6	WHEREAS the homelessness crisis is the result of continued
6	underinvestment of the provincial governments in affordable and
6	supportive housing and mental health and addictions services; and,
6	WHEREAS the homelessness crisis in Waterloo Region is compounded
6	by the region being a fast growing designated “places to grow community”
6	which has in part led to displacement of low income residents; and,
6	WHEREAS there are approximately 1000 individuals experiencing
6	homelessness in Waterloo Region already; and,
6	WHEREAS homelessness requires a range of housing, social service and
6	health solutions from the provincial government; and
6	WHEREAS homelessness is felt most at the level of local government and
6	the residents and businesses that they serve; and
6	WHEREAS the City of Waterloo, since 2021, continues to contribute
6	significant and escalating staff time and resources including an additional
7	$130,000 in 2023 to support various homelessness initiatives including
7	encampment supports, street outreach, service provider operations and
7	patrols and are active participants on Region-wide Housing and
7	Homelessness working groups where the Regional Municipality of
7	Waterloo as the local government lead on homelessness in the Region;
7	and,
7	WHEREAS we appreciate the recent Province of Ontario contribution of
7	$8.5 million in capital funding to support the House of Friendship Shelter
7	Care site in Waterloo in addition to previously announced operational
7	funding, but recognize that many more additional resources, capacity or
7	tools are needed to address this complex challenge; and,
7	WHEREAS leadership and urgent action is needed from the provincial
7	government on an emergency basis to develop, resource, and implement
7	a comprehensive plan to prevent, reduce and ultimately end
7	homelessness in Ontario.
7	NOW THEREFORE BE IT RESOLVED THAT the City of Waterloo calls on
7	the Provincial Government to urgently:
7	a. Acknowledge that homelessness in Ontario is a social,
7	economic, and health crisis;
7	b. Commit to ending homelessness in Ontario;
7	c. Work with AMO, municipalities and a broad range of
7	community, health, Indigenous and economic partners to
7	develop, resource, and implement an action plan to achieve
7	this goal.
7	AND FURTHER THAT a copy of this motion be sent to the Minister of
7	Municipal Affairs and Housing; the Minister of Children, Community and
7	Social Services; the Minister of Health; local and area Members of
7	Provincial Parliament, municipalities within the Region of Waterloo and to
7	the Association of Municipalities of Ontario.
7	Carried Unanimously
