2	3. TERRITORIAL ACKNOWLEDGEMENT
2	Mayor McCabe opened the meeting with the following Territorial Acknowledgement:
2	We would like to begin by acknowledging that the land on which we gather (land on
2	which we are broadcasting from) today is the land traditionally cared for by the
2	Haudenosaunee, Anishinaabe and Chonnontan People. We also acknowledge the
2	enduring presence and deep traditional knowledge and philosophies of the Indigenous
2	People with whom we share this land today.
2	4. MOMENT OF REFLECTION
2	Mayor McCabe provided Council with a moment of reflection.
2	September 30 is a day to witness and honour the healing journey of residential school
2	survivors and their families. It is a day to pause and recognize the lasting impacts that
2	residential schools have had on thousands of Indigenous families and communities who
2	continue to work through various stages of grieving and healing.
2	We encourage all residents of Waterloo and the Region to stop, reflect, learn and act.
2	Attend a memorial event organized by local Indigenous leaders. Explore learning
2	resources curated by our local libraries. Donate to Indigenous-led organizations. These
2	small but important signs of solidarity are important today and all year round.
2	As a City, we re-commit ourselves to the long-term work of reconciliation so we have a
2	better community for the seven generations yet to come.
2	At the beginning of this Council meeting, we pause to think about the needs of our
2	community. May we show wisdom and compassion in all our decisions.

5	12. NEW BUSINESS
5	Councillor Bodaly wanted to highlight that the Clair Hills Community Association hosted
5	a fun fair yesterday, September 22, at St. Moritz Park. There were staff there talking
5	about the revitalization of the park. He noted that this could be the last event for the
5	Association as they’re struggling to replace members of the Board and volunteerism is
5	down. He added that Neighbourhood Associations are important to communities and
5	bring vibrancy to the areas, and it’s important to support them.
6	Councillor Wright had a weekend was full of activities, including connecting with the
6	Spinning Wheels team, a volunteer-run organization that raises money for Parkinson’s
6	supports in the community. She learned a lot about how addressing Parkinson’s can
6	“raise the tide” for all people experiencing neurological impacts. She also noted that
6	Canada has the highest rate of Parkinson’s per capita.
6	Councillor Wright also spoke about how Shalom Counselling held their mental health
6	walk, 10,000 Steps for Mental Health on Saturday, September 21. They surpassed their
6	fundraising goal and had fantastic volunteers. They offer a sliding scale of rates for their
6	counselling services. The Kidney Foundation also had their Kidney Walk on Sunday,
6	September 22, and they also surpassed their fundraising goal and it was great to
6	honour Dr. Tom Liu, who had been a pillar of renal care at Grand River Hospital.
6	Councillor Wright also mentioned that Emmanuel at Brighton is celebrating their 60th
6	anniversary. They provide childcare support for working families.
6	Councillor Roe mentioned the 3rd Annual Canadian Arab Women’s Association Festival
6	in Kitchener on Saturday, September 21. It was a fantastic event with music, food and a
6	market. The Association is being recognized by the United Nations for the work they do
6	empowering women and building community.
6	Councillor Roe also gave a shout-out to Lumen on Saturday, September 21, and the
6	staff and volunteers who helped put it on. The crowds were huge and the installations
6	were amazing.
6	Councillor Vasic wanted to highlight two events that she couldn’t go to, as the weekend
6	was so busy. There was a neighbourhood street party in her neighbourhood, and she
6	offered her thanks to the Neighbourhoods team for the funding, so that they’re able to
6	run these events each year. She also mentioned the counter-protest in Kitchener on
6	Friday, September 20, offering support for 2SLGBTQIA+ folks, and making sure
6	children and youth have access to resources, people, and the education they need to
6	feel like they can be their full selves.
6	Councillor Vasic also mentioned the Kitchener-Waterloo Art Gallery is holding their
6	flagship fundraising event, instead of a gala. They’re running a “whodunnit” event,
6	where you have to try to capture “The Paris Prowler.” It is happening on Saturday,
6	October 26 and tickets are limited.
6	Mayor McCabe mentioned that the weekend was very full and offered thanks to those in
6	the community, especially the volunteers and staff for all of their hard work in putting
6	these events together.
