4	8. WORKSHOP
4	a) Title: 2023-2026 Strategic Plan – Council
4	Workshop #2
4	Report No.: CAO2023-012
4	Prepared By: Sandy Little
5	Sandy Little gave a brief introduction to the Strategic Planning process, and noted that
5	the next public session was taking place at RIM Park on April 1, 2023 from 2:00 till 4:00
5	p.m. during a free skate.
5	Himay Antani, Manager, Deloitte facilitated the workshop and discussed the plan for the
5	session, which included developing the core concepts Council want to put forward to
5	create the City’s mission statement. He also responded to questions of Council.
5	Councillor Hanmer offered some comments on how the ideas put forward seem to be a
5	continuation of the current mission statement, including themes of inclusion, diversity,
5	equity, climate change, forward focused and community.
5	Councillor Roe offered some thoughts based on her experiences volunteering in the
5	community. She stated that themes of community connection and welcoming to
5	newcomers were incredibly important.
5	Councillor Freeman stated that some of the themes Councillors put forward on the
5	board were almost the same things they discussed during the budget, including climate
5	change initiatives and how to invest in them; fiscal responsibility and balancing a tax
5	increase with individual reality; and innovation in terms of thinking differently about how
5	the City does business.
5	Councillor Vasic shared some thoughts about inclusion, being less about making sure
5	people come to us, and more about making sure there are many different spaces
5	available. She shared that vibrancy was important, especially thinking about it through a
5	COVID recovery lens, meeting the needs of the community to help people reconnect.
5	Councillor Bodaly shared additional comments around innovation. He stated that people
5	look to Waterloo to be innovative as a technology and education city. He highlighted
5	that the decisions Council made shouldn’t be only for the needs of the current residents,
5	but also for residents that didn’t live here yet.
5	Councillor Wright offered commentary on effective engagement, and being more
5	descriptive about what effective engagement was, especially through the lens of
5	everything that has changed over the last term of Council. She suggested the City
5	should think deeply about what it meant for people to participate, engage, collaborate
5	and innovate within the City context.
5	Mayor McCabe shared some thoughts for the mission statement about the caring and
5	compassionate community of Waterloo, which can mean different things to different
5	people.
5	Himay Antani summarized the main themes from the board, which revolved around
5	sustainability, equity, affordability, being forward thinking, and fiscal responsibility. He
5	then led Council through drafting their own mission statements. Each Councillor
5	presented their mission statements.
6	Mayor McCabe: Globally recognized future focused municipal leaders serving residents
6	in a sustainable, innovative, and equitable manner.
6	Councillor Bodaly: Meeting the needs of current and future residents in a sustainable
6	and equity focused manner.
6	Councillor Hanmer: Driving innovative, fiscally responsible, and sustainable growth
6	supporting our inclusive and vibrant communities.
6	Councillor Wright: Waterloo is a future-ready community, serving its residents through
6	collaborative and inclusive opportunities while maintaining accountability and excellence
6	in service.
6	Councillor Roach: Waterloo will be recognized as Canada’s most livable city known for
6	its innovation, inclusion, economic stability and climate responsibility.
6	Councillor Freeman: Working together to create a sustainable, equity focused City. To
6	serve the public by creating an economically stable, diverse, climate resilient City.
6	Councillor Roe: Building a strong foundation of inclusivity through sustainability, and
6	innovation through compassion and fiscal responsibility.
6	Councillor Vasic: Muddling our way through to a new normal with bold, thoughtful, and
6	transparent leadership; by building connection across differences; and by having fun.
6	Council then listed off which mission statements, or mission statement themes, they
6	thought worked best.
6	Sandy Little closed out the workshop by outlining the next steps for the Strategic Plan
6	development.
