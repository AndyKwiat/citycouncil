2	4. MOMENT OF REFLECTION
2	Councillor Wright provided Council with a moment of reflection.
2	At the beginning of this Council meeting, we pause to think about the needs of our
2	community. May we show wisdom and compassion in all our decisions.
2	During this moment of reflection we reflect on the passing of the Right
2	Honourable Martin Brian Mulroney, Canada’s 18th Prime Minister.
2	Although no direct connection to Waterloo; he shared many of our same values
2	whether it was his stance against apartheid in South Africa or his actions towards
2	environmental sustainability and climate action. He was voted Canada’s greenest
2	Prime Minister in a 2006 survey, noting at the time that “climate change is the
2	most serious environmental challenge, and time is running out”. As Prime
2	Minister he negotiated the Acid Rain Accord of the 1990s with the US to improve
2	air quality, and passed the Environmental Protection Act which laid the
2	foundation for environmental protection and action.
2	Our flags remain at half-mast as a recognition of the commitment he made
2	towards public service and as a sign of respect for his accomplishments.

6	11. NOTICE OF MOTION
6	a) Ontario Heritage Act (Municipal Heritage Registers) Motion – Councillor
6	Roe
6	Ron Ormson responded to questions of Council.
6	Moved by Councillor Vasic, seconded by Councillor Bodaly:
6	WHEREAS subsection 27(16) of the Ontario Heritage Act stipulates that
6	any non-designated heritage property listed on the municipal register of
6	properties as of December 31, 2022 shall be removed from the municipal
6	register on or before January 1, 2025, if the council of the municipality
6	does not give a notice of intention to designate the property under
6	subsection 29(1) of the Ontario Heritage Act on or before January 1, 2025;
6	and,
6	WHEREAS Since January 1, 2023, municipal staff and members of the
6	municipal heritage committee at the City of Waterloo have been diligently
6	working to: review the municipal heritage register; research the heritage
6	value and interest of listed (non-designated) properties; determine which
6	properties should potentially be designated in accordance with the
6	provisions of Section 29 of the Ontario Heritage Act; contact owners of
6	such properties; and take all required steps to designate such properties;
6	and,
6	WHEREAS The above-noted work involving 152 listed properties in the
6	City of Waterloo will consume many valuable staff hours and has proved to
6	be a time-consuming project and cannot be completed by December 31,
6	2024 without reassigning staff away from projects that lead to housing unit
6	growth.
6	NOW THEREFORE BE IT RESOLVED that this Council request the
6	Mayor send a letter to Doug Ford, Premier of Ontario, with copies to
6	Michael Ford, Minister of Citizenship and Multiculturalism; Peter
6	Bethlenfalvy, Minister of Finance; and John Ecker, Chair, Ontario Heritage
6	Trust, requesting that Subsection 27(16) of the Ontario Heritage Act be
6	amended to extend the above-noted deadline for five years from January
6	1, 2025 to January 1, 2030 and that a copy of this resolution be circulated
6	to the Association of Municipalities of Ontario, local and area MPP’s and
6	heads of council.
6	Carried Unanimously
