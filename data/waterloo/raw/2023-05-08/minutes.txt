1	2. CLOSED MEETING
1	Moved by Councillor Hanmer, Seconded by Councillor Freeman:
1	That Council hold a closed meeting for the purposes of considering the following
1	subject matter:
1	a) a proposed or pending acquisition or disposition of land by the municipality
1	or local board (potential acquisition and disposition of land);
1	b) advice that is subject to solicitor-client privilege, including communications
1	necessary for that purpose (unsheltered housing update, LIT Appeal,
1	potential acquisition and disposition of land);
1	c) litigation or potential litigation, including matters before administrative
1	tribunals, affecting the municipality or local board (unsheltered housing
1	update, LIT Appeal, potential disposition of land);
2	d) a matter in respect of which a council, board, committee or other body may
2	hold a closed meeting under another Act (licence agreement);
2	e) the security of the property of the municipality or local board (unsheltered
2	housing update);
2	f) a trade secret or scientific, technical, commercial, financial or labour
2	relations information, supplied in confidence to the municipality or local
2	board, which, if disclosed, could reasonably be expected to prejudice
2	significantly the competitive position or interfere significantly with the
2	contractual or other negotiations of a person, group of persons, or
2	organization (licence agreement); and,
2	g) a position, plan, procedure, criteria or instruction to be applied to any
2	negotiations carried on or to be carried on by or on behalf of the municipality
2	or local board (licence agreement, potential acquisition and disposition of
2	land).
