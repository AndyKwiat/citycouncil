9	Councillor Wright – Canadian Red Cross Canadian Wildfire Fund Motion
9	Motion tabled September 14, 2026 for Council consideration on September 14,
9	2026.
9	Moved by Councillor Wright, Seconded by Councillor Roe:
9	WHEREAS communities across Canada have been severely impacted by
9	2026 wildfires causing significant damage, interrupted daily life for those
9	impacted and resulted in loss of life and property; and
9	WHEREAS wildfires are a significant public health issue impacting the
9	physical and mental health of populations with impacts inequitably
9	distributed and disproportionately impacting indigenous communities; and
9	WHEREAS during the summer of 2026, wildfire smoke blanketed the City
9	of Waterloo as southern Ontario experienced air quality conditions that
9	ranked among the worst in the world, demonstrating that communities far
9	from active wildfires are increasingly vulnerable to changing climate driven
9	environmental hazards; and
9	WHEREAS the Canadian Red Cross has an urgent appeal for donations
9	to the Canadian Wildfire Fund; and
9	WHEREAS the fund is intended to enhance Canada’s readiness and
9	response to the escalating wildfire risks and bolster resilience.
9	NOW THEREFORE BE IT RESOLVED that $15,000 be funded from
9	Council’s Community Priority and Contingency Reserve be donated to the
9	Canadian Red Cross Wildfire fund.
9	Carried Unanimously
