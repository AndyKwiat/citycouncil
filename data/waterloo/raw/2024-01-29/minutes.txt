2	4. MOMENT OF REFLECTION
2	Mayor McCabe provided Council with a moment of reflection.
2	January 29 is the National Day of Remembrance of the Quebec City Mosque Attack and
2	Action against Islamophobia. On January 29, 2017, 6 Muslims - Ibrahima Barry,
2	Mamadou Tanou Barry, Khaled Belkacemi, Abdelkrim Hassane, Azzedine Soufiane,
2	and Aboubaker Thabti were killed and 19 others injured in a hateful act of terror when a
2	person wielding a gun entered the Centre Culturel Islamique de Québec in Sainte-Foy
2	following evening prayers. This senseless killing was a result of Islamophobia, racism
2	and bigotry.
2	In memory of the victims, in solidarity with the survivors and those who were injured, the
2	families and friends who lost a loved one, and in recognition of those who put
2	themselves in harm’s way to protect others, we pause, reflect and take action.
2	This remembrance serves as a critical reminder of the work that must be done to
2	eliminate Islamophobia, hate, racism and all forms of discrimination and prejudice
2	locally and across Canada.
2	As a Council we will continue to respond and address all forms of hate and racism in
2	this community.

8	11. CONSIDERATION OF NOTICE OF MOTION GIVEN AT PREVIOUS MEETING
8	Social and Economic Prosperity Review Motion – Councillor Freeman
8	Moved by Councillor Freeman, Seconded by Councillor Vasic:
9	WHEREAS Current provincial-municipal fiscal arrangement are undermining
9	Ontario’s economic prosperity and quality of life; and,
9	WHEREAS nearly a third of municipal spending in Ontario is for services in areas
9	of provincial responsibility and expenditures are outpacing provincial
9	contributions by nearly $4 billion a year;
9	WHEREAS municipal revenues, such as property taxes, do not grow with
9	economy or inflation; and,
9	WHEREAS unprecedented population growth and the clear need for more
9	affordable, attainable and sustainable housing will require significant investments
9	in municipal infrastructure; and
9	WHEREAS municipalities are being asked to take on complex health and social
9	challenges such as homelessness, the mental health and addictions crisis and
9	support for asylum seekers and newcomers; and,
9	WHEREAS inflation, rising interest rates, and provincial policy decisions are
9	sharply constraining municipal fiscal capacity; and
9	WHEREAS property taxpayers, including those on fixed incomes, seniors, small
9	businesses and more cannot afford to subsidize income re-distribution programs;
9	and
9	WHEREAS the province can, and should, invest more in the prosperity of
9	communities across Ontario; and
9	WHEREAS municipalities and the provincial government have a strong history of
9	collaboration.
9	NOW THEREFORE BE IT RESOLVED that:
9	1) The Province of Ontario commit to undertaking with the Association of
9	Municipalities of Ontario a comprehensive social and economic prosperity
9	review to promote the stability and sustainability of municipal finances
9	across Ontario;
9	2) a copy of this resolution be sent to the Premier of Ontario, local area
9	MPPs and the Association of Municipalities of Ontario for further
9	consideration.
9	Carried Unanimously
