5	9. NOTICE OF MOTION
5	a) Solve the Crisis Campaign Motion – Mayor McCabe
5	Motion to be tabled September 9, 2024 for Council consideration
5	September 16, 2024.
5	Motion:
5	WHEREAS there is a humanitarian crisis in cities, large and small, urban
5	and rural, across Ontario. We need immediate action at all levels of
5	government, starting with the Province; and
5	WHEREAS the homelessness, mental health and addictions crisis
5	continues to grow with 3432 drug related deaths in Ontario in 2023 as well
5	as with an estimated 234,000 Ontarians experiencing homelessness and
5	over 1400 homeless encampments across Ontario communities in 2023;
5	and
5	WHEREAS the province has provided additional funding for mental health,
5	addictions and homelessness programs, it does not adequately address
5	the growing crisis and the financial and social impact on municipalities and
5	regions across the province; and
5	WHEREAS municipalities and regions are stepping up and working with
5	community partners to put in place community-specific solutions to
5	address this crisis, but municipalities and regions lack the expertise,
5	capacity, or resources to address these increasingly complex health care
5	and housing issues alone; and
6	WHEREAS this is primarily a health issue that falls under provincial
6	jurisdiction and municipalities and regions should not be using the property
6	tax base to fund these programs; and
6	WHEREAS there is no provincial Minister focused on this crisis leading to
6	unanswered questions that span over a dozen ministries, and a lack of
6	support to manage the increasing needs of those who are unhoused.
6	NOW THEREFORE BE IT RESOLVED THAT THE CITY OF WATERLOO
6	supports the Solve the Crisis Campaign;
6	AND calls on provincial and federal governments to commit to immediate
6	action to solve the Humanitarian Crisis that Ontario is facing as the
6	numbers of unhoused individuals and those suffering with mental health &
6	addictions grows exponentially;
6	AND that the province officially makes Homelessness a Health Priority;
6	AND appoints a responsible Minister and Ministry with the appropriate
6	funding and powers as a single point of contact to address the full spectrum
6	of housing needs as well as mental health, addictions and wrap around
6	supports;
6	AND request that the provincial government strike a task force with broad
6	sector representatives including municipalities, regions, healthcare, first
6	responders, community services, the business community and the tourism
6	industry to develop a Made in Ontario Action Plan;
6	AND that this provincial task force reviews current programs developed by
6	municipalities, regions and community partners that have proven
6	successful in our communities, to ensure that solutions can be
6	implemented quickly and effectively to tackle this crisis.
6	AND provides the adequate, sufficient and sustainable funding to ensure
6	that municipalities have the tools and resources to support individuals
6	suffering with mental health and addictions, including unhoused people
6	and those from vulnerable populations that may be disproportionately
6	impacted;
6	AND that this Council calls on the residents across the City and Region of
6	Waterloo to join us in appealing to the provincial and federal governments
6	for support by visiting SolveTheCrisis.ca and showing your support;
6	AND further that a copy of this motion be sent to:
