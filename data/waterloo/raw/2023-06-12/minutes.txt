6	10. NEW BUSINESS
6	Councillor Freeman talked about the Service Centre Open House and offered kudos to
6	all the team members and organizers that put on the event, including retired team
6	members who came back to help out. She also mentioned the Community Partners who
6	were there, including Reep Green Solutions and Supportive Housing of Waterloo
6	Region.
6	Councillor Wright talked about the Grand Porch Party that took place on Sunday, June
6	11.
6	Councillor Roe wanted to thank Mayor McCabe and Councillor Bodaly for attending the
6	World Refugee Day Community Launch on June 4, 2023, and bring attention to the
6	Walk with Refugees happening on June 24, 2023. More information can be found at
6	ccoric.ca.
6	Councillor Vasic talked about the Drag Storytime that occurred at the Kitchener Public
6	Library Central branch, and the counter-protest to communicate that hate has no place
6	here. She also attended Tri-Pride in Victoria Park. She also mentioned that Toronto
6	author Lindsay Zier-Vogel attended Roselea Park on June 10, 2023 and read her book,
6	“Dear Street”. Words Worth Books was there, and it was a community building event.
6	Councillor Bodaly talked about the Open Streets Festival, which also happened on June
6	10 on Willis Way. He also mentioned the trees that the Uptown BIA has put out along
6	King Street, which was great to see. He thanked everyone who is responsible for all the
6	events.
6	Mayor McCabe participated in the Belongathon organized by Christian Horizons, which
6	raised $90,000. She also briefly mentioned Greek Food Fest. Both events also
6	happened on June 10th.
6	Mayor McCabe also wanted to bring attention to CAO Tim Anderson’s milestone of
6	working 20 years in municipal management. She talked about his history in the
6	municipal field, and
6	Tim Anderson spoke about his career, and how great it is to work with everyone to build
6	the great City of Waterloo.
