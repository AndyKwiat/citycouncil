8	12. QUESTIONS
8	Councillor Freeman had a question about the work that’s happening in Hillside Park
8	around the creek expansion. She noted that there are signs up indicating that the trails
8	are going to be temporarily closed, and wanted to make sure the active users through
8	that corridor aren’t going to be diverted. Ron Ormson responded stating that there
8	would at least be a rolling closure at certain times for safety, but that staff will
8	investigate and make sure there’s a good detour and proper notification.
8	13. NEW BUSINESS

8	success, and to thank everyone who played an important role. There were 166
8	attendees for the full conference, with more people for the fireside chat Wednesday
8	evening. The responses from participants have been really positive, and many
8	attendees plan to attend the 2025 conference. She gave a shoutout to Chris Hodgson,
8	Louise Finlay, and Bob Henderson for attending weekly, evening planning meetings.

8	Waterloo, and John Vos ran a walking tour of Uptown Waterloo.
8	Councillor Freeman also gave kudos for Community Fun Day yesterday April 14th,
8	especially to Iliana and Linda for organizing it. The ice and the community room were
8	full, and it was an amazing day.

8	and Linda.

8	Councillor Bodaly for picking up the candy.
