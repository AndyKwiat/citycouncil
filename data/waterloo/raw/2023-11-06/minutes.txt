7	a) Mayor McCabe – Response to Minister of Municipal Affairs and Housing’s
7	Letter Dated November 2, 2023, Regarding Regional Municipality of
7	Waterloo – Regional Official Plan
7	Moved by Councillor Bodaly, seconded by Councillor Roe:
7	Request to waive Notice of Motion to consider the following at the November 6, 2023
7	Meeting of Council.
7	Carried Unanimously
7	Moved by Mayor McCabe, seconded by Councillor Wright:
7	WHEREAS the Regional Municipality of Waterloo (Region) commenced a
7	municipal comprehensive review of the Regional Official Plan (ROP) in
7	August 2018;
7	AND WHEREAS City of Waterloo staff participated in the ROP Review
7	process through the Area Municipality Working Group and provided input,
7	with Waterloo City Council’s direction, on technical assessments and other
7	components of the ROP;
7	AND WHEREAS Regional Council approved the ROP — which projects
7	growth to the year 2051 and ensures sufficient land for local municipalities
7	within the Region of Waterloo to reach and exceed provincial housing
7	targets — on August 18, 2022, with all heads of council voting in favour of
7	the ROP Amendment;
7	AND WHEREAS the Region is required to complete a municipal
7	comprehensive review of the ROP every five (5) years, and it is anticipated
7	that the next review will be completed by the Region in approximately 3.5
7	years, at which time further amendments can be approved following a
7	thorough, transparent, and participatory public consultation process;
7	AND WHEREAS the Ministry of Municipal Affairs and Housing approved
7	the ROP on April 11, 2023 with significant amendments to the urban
7	growth areas and the Countryside Line growth boundary that are
7	unnecessary to achieve growth targets, that were not requested nor
7	recommended by local and regional planning staff, that encroach on
7	important water re-charge areas and that promote expensive urban
7	sprawl;
7	AND WHEREAS the Minister of Municipal Affairs and Housing announced
7	on October 23, 2023 that the April 11, 2023 decision regarding the ROP
7	failed to meet the test of supporting the Province’s goal of building at least
7	1.5 million homes in a manner that maintains and reinforces public trust
8	and that the Minister will introduce legislation to reverse the Province’s
8	April 11, 2023 ROP decision, including the changes to the Countryside
8	Line;
8	AND WHEREAS the Minister of Municipal Affairs and Housing sent a letter
8	— dated November 2, 2023 and titled “Announcement Impacting
8	Provincial Decisions on Municipal Official Plans/Official Plan Amendments”
8	— to Mayor McCabe and other mayors of single and lower-tier
8	municipalities impacted by the April 11, 2023 decision;
8	AND WHEREAS the Minister’s November 2, 2023 letter provided mayors
8	with 35 days to identify — without public consultation, without consultation
8	with First Nations, and without the input of elected members of Council nor
8	professional staff — changes that each mayor would like to see made to
8	the ROP, based on the modifications that the Province had previously
8	made;
8	AND WHEREAS local and regional Councillors represent residents and
8	building a successful community is best done with the input of all elected
8	officials not solely the heads of council;
8	AND WHEREAS Mayor McCabe and other mayors have stated that they
8	will not use the Strong Mayor powers, and Mayor McCabe has stated that
8	she believes utilizing Strong Mayor powers will undermine the work of
8	Council and staff, reduce the input of residents and community partners,
8	and is a disservice to local democracy and effective community
8	representation;
8	NOW THEREFORE BE IT RESOLVED that Waterloo City Council directs
8	Mayor McCabe to submit the following feedback to the Ministry by
8	December 7, 2023:
8	(1) Waterloo City Council reaffirms its commitment to smart growth,
8	intensification, and to maintaining the Countryside Line;
8	(2) Waterloo City Council reaffirms its pledge to build 16,000 housing
8	units by 2031;
8	(3) Questions relating to the Regional Official Plan should be addressed
8	to the Region;
8	(4) Matters relating to official plans and other major decisions impacting
8	the City of Waterloo are to be made by all of Council with public input
8	as appropriate; and,
8	(5) The City of Waterloo asks that the Minister bring into effect the ROP
8	amendment as approved by Regional Council on August 18, 2022
8	without any changes or modifications.
8	Carried Unanimously
