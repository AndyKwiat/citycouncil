4	9. STAFF REPORTS
4	a) Title: 250 Lincoln Road Update and Next Steps
4	Report No.: CAO2026-003
4	Prepared By: Michelle Lee
4	Michelle Lee gave a presentation giving an outline of the current project timeline, and
4	the potential considerations for staff and Council going forward. She then responded to
4	questions of Council. Brad Witzel, Kevin Van Ooteghem and Ron Ormson also
4	responded to questions of Council.
4	Councillor Bodaly left the meeting. (Time: 7:10 p.m.)
4	Councillor Bodaly joined the meeting. (Time: 7:13 p.m.)
4	Victoria Richardson, Co-Chair, Lincoln Heights School Council gave a presentation
4	asking Council to consider the parking situation surrounding Lincoln Heights Public
4	School when deciding on development for the site, as well as the challenges of active
4	transportation for the school. Victoria Richardson responded to questions of Council.
4	Bob Henderson and Kevin Van Ooteghem also responded to questions of Council.
4	John Beresford, Resident of Waterloo asked Council to defer a decision on this site, due
4	to the slowing population growth in the Region and the need to reevaluate the housing
4	pledge. John Beresford also spoke about the fiscal risk in demolishing the site before a
4	proponent has been selected, as well as the zoning concerns with potential high density
4	for the site. John Beresford then responded to questions of Council. Michelle Lee also
4	responded to questions of Council.
5	Wendy Ridgway, Representative, Eyes on Waterloo gave a presentation asking Council
5	not to rezone the land to allow for more density or height, and to include that as a
5	condition of the REOI process. Wendy Ridgway also outlined the current housing trends
5	in the Region, and asked Council to reconsider approving the report. Wendy Ridgway
5	then responded to questions of Council. Michelle Lee also responded to questions of
5	Council.
5	Moved by Councillor Freeman, Seconded by Councillor Vasic:
5	1. That Council approve report CAO2026-003.
5	2. That Council direct staff to proceed with the planning and
5	procurement processes for the development of 250 Lincoln Road
5	for affordable housing, in accordance with the actions and timelines
5	set out in Table 1 in this report.
5	3. That Council direct staff to engage with the public, affordable
5	housing providers and community organizations to inform changes
5	to the planning framework and Request for Expressions of Interest.
5	4. That Council direct staff to proceed with demolition of the existing
5	structure at 250 Lincoln Road in spring 2026 to facilitate the
5	development.
5	5. That Council direct staff to consider possible interim use of the site
5	as a publicly accessible green space, following demolition of the
5	existing structure.
5	Carried Unanimously

5	11. NEW BUSINESS
5	Councillor Wright mentioned the major winter event that occurred over the weekend.
5	The City had the benefit of celebrating winter with Winterloo in Uptown on Saturday,
5	January 24. There were ice carvings and sled dogs, with many people enjoying the
5	bright, winter day. The Comfort Food Crawl also took place as a fundraiser for the Food
5	Bank. They raised $8,000 with nearly 400 people taking part, and it was a great
5	showcase of Uptown restaurants. She offered an additional shoutout to the Winter
5	Control Team for their perseverance this season.
6	Mayor McCabe also gave a shoutout to Bob Henderson, Director of Transportation, and
6	Robin Milne, Director of Parks, Forestry and Cemeteries, for their part in winter control.
6	There has been continual snow clearing since the beginning of November. She also
6	reminded residents to shovel their sidewalks.
6	Councillor Roe mentioned that the Province is funding a $17.8 million expansion of St.
6	David’s Catholic Secondary School in Ward 6, which is very exciting news, especially
6	with the projected growth in the area. She was happy to be there for the announcement
6	on Friday, January 23.
6	Councillor Vasic wanted to capture in New Business the community meeting around
6	traffic safety in the Cardinal Crescent area. She offered her thanks to the two residents
6	who organized it. Bob Henderson, Director of Transportation at the City, as well as
6	Darryl Spencer, Manager of Transportation Engineering at the Region and Mike
6	Henderson, Director of Transportation at the Region were able to attend with other
6	regional staff. Iliana, Executive Assistant to the Mayor, helped organize the event with
6	the residents. This was the second of two neighbourhood community events.
6	Councillor Vasic also wanted to thank staff for another meeting they helped run with the
6	community helping to educate them on the planning process, communications and
6	engagement with the City. Cari Van Niekerk, Director of Communications at the City
6	and Joel Cotter, Director of Planning both attended, with Linda, Constituency
6	Coordinator organizing the event. Both events were very well attended, with a lot of
6	good stuff happening in the neighbourhood.
6	Mayor McCabe thanked Councillor Bodaly for the many hours he spent door knocking
6	and engaging residents about the informal public meeting that came forward this
6	afternoon.
6	Councillor Roach has accepted an invitation from the Honourable Marc Miller, Canadian
6	Minister of Identity and Culture, to attend the Government of Canada’s Black History
6	Month celebration on behalf of Waterloo next week, marking the 30th anniversary of
6	Black History Month in Canada. It’s at the Canadian Museum of History in Gatineau.
