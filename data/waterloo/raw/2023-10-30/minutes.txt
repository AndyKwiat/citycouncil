5	10. CONSIDERATION OF NOTICE OF MOTION GIVEN AT PREVIOUS MEETING
5	a) Affordable Housing Commitment Motion – Councillor Bodaly
5	Councillor Bodaly introduced the motion and called on Adam Lauder to show examples
5	of triplexes and fourplexes in the area.
5	Shiva Subramanian, Resident of Waterloo spoke in support of the motion and about the
5	affordable housing crisis.
5	Nicholas Aboagye, Resident of Kitchener spoke in support of the motion and offered
5	suggestions on what else the City can do to address the housing crisis.
6	Joel Cotter responded to questions of Council.
6	Moved by Councillor Bodaly, Seconded by Councillor Hanmer:
6	WHEREAS the City of Waterloo population increased by 15.7% between
6	2016 and 2021, faster than the provincial average of 5.8% and the national
6	average of 5.2% during that same time period; and
6	WHEREAS the City of Waterloo’s population is forecasted to grow by 45%
6	to 185,000 people by 2051; and
6	WHEREAS the City of Waterloo has committed to supporting the creation
6	of 16,000 new housing units by 2031 as its Housing Pledge to the
6	Province; and
6	WHEREAS the City of Waterloo approved an Affordable Housing Strategy
6	in June 2023 with 81 actions, that, among other things, recommended
6	ways to increase housing supply and the diversity of housing options, with
6	an emphasis on areas near transit and transportation corridors.
6	WHEREAS the City of Waterloo currently permits up to three residential
6	units per property as-of-right on a parcel of urban residential land; and
6	WHEREAS the Government of Canada’s Housing Accelerator Fund (HAF)
6	grant program represents an opportunity for the City to review and
6	accelerate actions to achieve more housing supply faster; and
6	THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED:
6	1. That staff be directed to initiate an amendment to Zoning By-law
6	2018-050 to permit, as-of-right, up to four residential units and four
6	storeys on residential parcels within low density residential zones,
6	where appropriate (the “Amendment”) and report back to Council in
6	Q1 2024;
6	2. As part of the Amendment, that staff be directed to identify
6	appropriate zoning regulations and development criteria,
6	considering matters such as safe access and servicing capacity, for
6	Council’s consideration; and,
6	3. That Council confirm its support for the principle of intensification in
6	transit supportive areas and near post-secondary institutions, and
6	directs staff to identify opportunities to achieve the following
6	planning objectives through the Official Plan Review, as
6	recommended in the City’s Affordable Housing Strategy:
7	a. higher density housing options in areas supported by transit
7	and near post secondary institutions; and
7	b. increase the supply of missing middle housing, evaluating
7	opportunities for new missing middle corridors, modifications
7	to the existing nodes and corridors strategy using a missing
7	middle lens, and consideration of minimum height and density
7	targets in specified areas to facilitate missing middle housing;
7	4. That Council reaffirm its commitment to our Affordable Housing
7	Strategy, including the City of Waterloo’s goals to prioritize non-
7	market housing, optimize existing housing, increase the supply of
7	affordable housing, providing incentives to build new affordable
7	housing, and enable a greater supply and mix of housing within our
7	community.
7	5. That Council reaffirm its housing pledge of achieving the goal of
7	16,000 new homes by 2031, in partnership with the development
7	industry.
7	6. That this motion be forwarded to the Hon. Sean Fraser, Minister of
7	Housing, Infrastructure and Communities of Canada for
7	consideration as part of our Housing Accelerator Fund (HAF) grant
7	application.
7	Carried Unanimously
