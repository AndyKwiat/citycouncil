7	14. PUBLIC MEETINGS
7	Informal Public Meeting
7	a) Title: Zoning By-law Amendment Z-25-25
7	Anndale Holdings Inc.
7	445 Anndale Road
7	Prepared by: Madison Headrick and John Vos
7	Ward No.: Ward 4 (Northeast)
7	Madison Headrick gave a presentation outlining the proposed development, including
7	the site context, a breakdown of the units and parking, and the zoning by-law
7	amendments being requested. She then responded to questions of Council. John Vos
7	and Jenny Renaud also responded to questions of Council. Joel Cotter also responded
7	to questions of Council.
7	Christian Tsimenidis, UP Consulting gave a presentation further detailing the proposed
7	application, offering additional site context and breakdown of unit types. Christian
7	Tsimenidis also presented conceptual renderings of the site and additional planning
7	rationale for the zoning by-law amendments. Christian Tsimenidis responded to
7	questions of Council. Madison Headrick also responded to questions of Council.
7	Jim Whetstone, Resident of Waterloo gave a presentation discussing whether the
7	development conforms to Official Plan Amendment 58 and the Growth Management
7	Policy, as well as the impact on traffic in the area, the lack of amenity space for the new
7	residents, and the opportunities for collaboration with the community.
7	Carol Roy, Resident of Waterloo spoke about the increase in height being requested,
7	the lack of garages and basements for garbage storage, the lack of shared parking
7	between the Church and residents, the poor traffic flow on Anndale Road, and the lack
7	of a buffer between the house next door for privacy.
7	Annie Caroline Dixon, on behalf of Carol Dixon, Resident of Waterloo spoke about how
7	the shared parking is insufficient for the amount of visitors for the Church, safety for
7	students from school bus drop-offs, that the infrastructure has not been updated to
7	accommodate the units being built, and the concerns they have for the Church being
7	severed at a later date. Annie Caroline Dixon responded to questions of Council. Jenny
7	Renaud and John Vos also responded to questions of Council.
8	Amy Denstedt, Resident of Waterloo spoke about the overlapping projects that are
8	affecting the neighbourhood at the same time, including several road reconstructions
8	and developments. Amy Denstedt also spoke about the traffic and parking issues in the
8	area.
8	Lora Giangregorio, Resident of Waterloo spoke about the traffic and parking issues in
8	the area especially at peak times, as well as the lack of gradual increase of height
8	between the existing neighbourhood and the proposal. Lora Giangregorio then
8	responded to questions of Council. John Vos, Jenny Renaud and Ron Ormson also
8	responded to questions of Council.
8	Councillor Bodaly left the meeting. (Time: 8:35 p.m.)

8	Councillor Vasic joined the meeting. (Time: 8:42 p.m.)
