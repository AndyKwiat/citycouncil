  • Moved ByCouncillor Caron
    Seconded ByCouncillor Chew
    1. In accordance with Section 4.13 (b) of the City of Guelph Procedure By-Law City Council continue the meeting until 11:59 p.m.
    Voting in Favour: (13)Mayor Guthrie, Councillor Gibson, Councillor Goller, Councillor Allt, Councillor Downer, Councillor O'Rourke, Councillor Billings, Councillor Caron, Councillor Caton, Councillor Klassen, Councillor Richardson, Councillor Busuttil, and Councillor Chew
    Carried (13 to 0)
  • First Motion
    Moved ByCouncillor Richardson
    Seconded ByCouncillor Goller
    1. That City Council approve the draft Public Space Use By-law, as attached to staff memo 2024-359, and that staff be directed to place the by-law on the September 10, 2024 City Council agenda for final adoption after which the by-law will come into effect on October 1, 2024.
  • Amendment to First Motion
    Moved ByCouncillor Klassen
    Seconded ByCouncillor Goller
    1. That staff amend the draft Public Space Use By-Law to include the entirety of both St. George’s Square and Market Square as Sensitive Public Areas in Schedule “A”.
    Voting in Favour: (10)Mayor Guthrie, Councillor Gibson, Councillor Goller, Councillor Downer, Councillor O'Rourke, Councillor Billings, Councillor Caron, Councillor Klassen, Councillor Richardson, and Councillor Chew
    Voting Against: (3)Councillor Allt, Councillor Caton, and Councillor Busuttil
    Carried (10 to 3)
  • First Motion as Amended
    Moved ByCouncillor Richardson
    Seconded ByCouncillor Goller
    1. That City Council approve the draft Public Space Use By-law as amended, and as attached to staff memo 2024-359, and that staff be directed to place the by-law on the September 10, 2024 City Council agenda for final adoption after which the by-law will come into effect on October 1, 2024.
    Voting in Favour: (10)Mayor Guthrie, Councillor Gibson, Councillor Goller, Councillor Downer, Councillor O'Rourke, Councillor Billings, Councillor Caron, Councillor Klassen, Councillor Richardson, and Councillor Chew
    Voting Against: (3)Councillor Allt, Councillor Caton, and Councillor Busuttil
    Carried (10 to 3)
  • Second Motion
    Moved ByCouncillor O'Rourke
    Seconded ByCouncillor Goller
    1. That up to $450,000 be provided to the County of Wellington and funded from the Tax Rate Operating Contingency Reserve #180, to be dispersed through the Wellington-Guelph Health and Housing Community Planning Table for the provision of basic needs for people experiencing homelessness in Guelph.  The one-time funding is to be allocated for work commencing September 2024 and being completed no later than December 2025, and the funding is to be provided following receipt of interim and final reports from the Planning Table, subject to the satisfaction of the Deputy CAO of Public Services.  The funding commitment will be reduced if offsetting federal and/or provincial funds are identified.
    Voting in Favour: (13)Mayor Guthrie, Councillor Gibson, Councillor Goller, Councillor Allt, Councillor Downer, Councillor O'Rourke, Councillor Billings, Councillor Caron, Councillor Caton, Councillor Klassen, Councillor Richardson, Councillor Busuttil, and Councillor Chew
    Carried (13 to 0)
  • Third Motion
    Moved ByCouncillor Caton
    Seconded ByCouncillor Allt
    1. That staff be directed to engage with the Health and Housing Community Planning Table to identify needs and develop a plan, including a budget and funding source, to provide and maintain portable washrooms, waste bins and sharps disposal to encampments in the City of Guelph (subject to site feasibility), reporting back to City Council in Q1 2025.
    Voting in Favour: (13)Mayor Guthrie, Councillor Gibson, Councillor Goller, Councillor Allt, Councillor Downer, Councillor O'Rourke, Councillor Billings, Councillor Caron, Councillor Caton, Councillor Klassen, Councillor Richardson, Councillor Busuttil, and Councillor Chew
    Carried (13 to 0)
