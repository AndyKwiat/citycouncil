The following presented regarding the 2026 Budget Update Local Boards and Shared Services Agencies:
Shanna O'Dwyer, General Manager, Finance/City Treasurer/Chief Financial Officer
Gord Cobey, Chief of Police, Guelph Police Services
Ajay Sharma, Chair, Guelph Police Services Board
Dan Atkins, Chief Executive Officer, Guelph Public Library
Luisa Artuso, Social Services Administrator, Wellington County
Shauna Calder, Manager of Finance, Wellington County

  • Moved ByCouncillor K. Hauser
    Seconded ByCouncillor Downer
    1. That an amended 2026 operating budget for the City’s local boards and shared services (LBSS) agencies be approved, inclusive of the following:
      1. The LBSS agencies budget requirement, net of the proportionate share of assessment growth, totalling an increased net levy requirement over 2025 of $15,458,560 or 4.40 per cent inclusive of the following:
        1. Guelph Police Services: an increased tax levy requirement over 2025 of $6,631,188
        2. Guelph Public Library: an increased tax levy requirement over 2025 of $1,979,286
        3. County of Wellington: an increased tax levy requirement over 2025 of $8,191,200
        4. Guelph–Wellington-Dufferin Public Health: an increase tax levy requirement over 2025 of $132,524
        5. The Elliott Community, an increase tax levy requirement over 2025 of $61,317
        6. The LBSS proportionate share of assessment growth revenue in the amount of $1,536,955
      2. Grand River Conservation Authority budget with an increase in gross operating expenses over 2025 in the amount of $50,000.
      3. Total transfers to/from Reserve and Reserve Funds in 2026 in accordance with the LBSS reserve and reserve fund update.
    2. That Council approves the application of assessment growth revenue above the amount budgeted to reduce the 2026 tax levy impact, contrary to the prescribed treatment under the Revenue Budgeting Policy.
    3. That an amended 2026 net levy requirement from taxation and payment in lieu of property taxes inclusive of City and LBSS agencies up to $383,403,808 be approved.
    4. The Downtown Guelph Business Association budget with gross expenditures of $807,754 and a total levy of $742,836.
    5. That 2027 and 2028 operating budget forecasts, where one has been provided by the organization, be received for information.
    6. An amended 2026 LBSS capital budget with a net decrease of $514,300 from the 2026 adopted budget resulting in the gross 2026 expenditure amount of $4,734,300 inclusive of funding transfer from capital reserve funds and other sources including partnerships, grants, and debt financing.
    Voting in Favour: (11)Mayor Guthrie, Councillor Gibson, Councillor Goller, Councillor Allt, Councillor Downer, Councillor Billings, Councillor Caton, Councillor Klassen, Councillor Busuttil, Councillor Chew, and Councillor K. Hauser
    Carried (11 to 0)
