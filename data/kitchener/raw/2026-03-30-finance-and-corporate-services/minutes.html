Rental Renovation Licensing By-law, COR-2026-045

60 m
 
1.Rental Renovation Licensing By-law - COR-2026-045.pdf
2.Attachment A - Rental Renovation Licensing By-law - FV.pdf
3.Attachment B - Proposed Rental Renovation Licensing Fees.pdf
4.Attachment C - Optimus SBR - Recommendations Report.pdf
5.STAFF - Item 9.4 - Rental Renovation Licensing Presentation.pdf
6.CONSULTANT - Item 9.4 - Evictions Due to Renovation (FCS - Part 2).pdf
  • Councillor S. Stretch declared a conflict on this item. (Councillor S. Stretch declared a pecuniary interest with respect to the Rental Renoviction Licensing By-law, COR-2026-045, listed as item 8.4 on the agenda this date, due to owning property and renting out units and did not participate in any voting or discussion on this matter.)
  • Councillor P. Singh declared a conflict on this item. (Councillor P. Singh declared a pecuniary interest with respect to the Rental Renoviction Licensing By-law, COR-2026-045, listed as item 8.4 on the agenda this date, due to owning property and renting out units and did not participate in any voting or discussion on this matter.)

The Chair noted that by consensus of the Committee, the order of items on the agenda this date was amended to consider this matter first.

The Committee considered Corporate Services Department report COR-2026-045, dated March 12, 2026 recommending approval of the proposed Rental Renovation Licensing By-law, its' associated fees and the request for two (2) Full Time Equivalents (FTE’s) to support the administration, inspection, and enforcement of the By-law.

K. VanDerGeld provided opening remarks and introduced J. Burns and B. Tharakan, Optimus SBR, the City’s Consultant who presented their recommendations related to a Rental Renovation Licensing By-law. K. VanDerGeld then presented the staff report including recommendations and responded to questions from the Committee. A. Fusco and N. Goss were also in attendance and responded to questions from the Committee.

In response to questions from the Committee, K. VanDerGeld noted, the proposed By-Law enhances transparency, ensures tenants receive timely and accurate information about their rights and provides the City with oversight related to renovation based evictions while avoiding duplication of provincial processes. 

K. Vogelpohl and J. Wells, ACORN, M. Walker, Social Development Centre, E. Wand, B. Smith, L. Vos, S. Azevedo, M. Milosz, J. Domingo, R. Murdoch, L. Intini, E. Melo, C. Degroot-Magetti, K. Kurt and P. Margani provided comments in support of the proposed By-Law.  

K. Andrade, Ontario Landlords Watch, H. Shwey and K. Breitner provided comments in opposition to the Staff recommendation noting the proposed By-Law is a duplication of existing provincial policies related to renovation based evictions. 

Councillors M. Johnston and J. Deneault left the meeting at this time.

Councillor A. Owodunni left the meeting at this time.

Councillor J. Deneault re-entered the meeting at this time.

Counciilor M. Johnston re-entered the meeting at this time.

Councillor P. Singh re-entered the meeting at this time.

Councillor D. Chapman brought forward a motion to approve the staff recommendation outlined in the report including an amendment that the By-law include the following provisions:

Where, upon being served with an N13 Notice by the Landlord, a Tenant has exercised their rights in compliance with section 53 of the Residential Tenancies Act to have a right of first refusal to occupy the Rental Unit after repairs and renovations are completed, the Landlord shall:

a)    Compensate the Tenant an amount equal to the difference between the current rent of the affected Rental Unit being repaired or renovated and the most recently published Average Market Rent of a Comparable Rental Unit for the entire duration of the repair or renovation; or,
b)    Make arrangements agreeable to the Tenant, in the Tenant’s discretion and as indicated by a signed agreement between the Landlord and the Tenant, for the Tenant’s temporary alternate accommodation in a Comparable Rental Unit for the entire duration of the repair or renovation;
In addition to the compensation or alternate accommodation, every Landlord who serves an N13 Notice on a Tenant shall pay to the Tenant a one-time amount of $1,000.00 towards moving expenses. Where there are multiple Tenants in the affected Rental Unit, the payment shall be divided equally among each tenant;
The compensation shall be paid by the Landlord to the Tenant within seven (7) calendar days before the first (1st) day of each month for the entire duration that the Tenant is displaced from the Rental Unit because of the repair or renovation;
The compensation noted above shall be paid to the Tenant no later than the termination date specified on the N13 Notice, or no later than the date that the Tenant vacates the Rental Unit, whichever is earlier; and further,
The above the compensation and/or alternate accommodation requirements shall be in addition to the Tenant’s rights of compensation and any other rights, as applicable, under the Residential Tenancies Act.

Councillor D. Schnider brought an amendment to defer consideration of the staff recommendation to the 2027 budget cycle.

It was requested that the motion to approve the Staff recommendation be voted on separately from the amendment brought forward by Councillor D. Chapman. 

Mayor B. Vrbanovic brought forward a motion to defer the amendment brought forward by Councillor D. Chapman to the April 13, 2026 Council meeting. 

A recorded vote was requested.

Councillor D. Schnider's motion to defer consideration of the staff recommendation to the 2027 budget cycle was then voted on and was LOST on a tie, on a recorded vote with Councillors S. Davey, B. Ioannidis, C. Michaud and D. Schinder voting in favour, and Mayor B. Vrbanovic and Councillors D. Chapman, J. Deneault and M. Johnston voting in opposition.

Mayor B. Vrbanovic's motion to defer consideration of Councillor D. Chapman's amendment to the April 13, 2026 Council meeting was then voted on and was Carried, on a recorded vote, with Mayor B. Vrbanovic and Councillors D. Chapman, J. Deneault, C. Michaud and D. Schnider voting in favour; and, Councillors S. Davey, B. Ioannidis and M. Johnston voting in opposition.

The following motion was then voted on, on a recorded vote.

  • On motion byCouncillor D. Chapman

    it was resolved:

    "That the Rental Renovation Licensing By-law be approved in the form shown attached to Corporate Services Department report COR-2026-045 as Appendix “A”; and,

    That the fees set out in Appendix “B” of Corporate Services Department report COR-2026-045 be approved; and further,

    That the request for two (2) Full Time Equivalents (FTE’s) to support the administration, inspection, and enforcement of the Rental Renovation Licensing By-law be approved."

    In Favour (5)Mayor B. Vrbanovic, Councillor C. Michaud, Councillor M. Johnston, Councillor D. Chapman, and Councillor J. Deneault
    Contra (3)Councillor S. Davey, Councillor D. Schnider, and Councillor B. Ioannidis
    Conflict (2)Councillor P. Singh, and Councillor S. Stretch
    Carried (5 to 3)
