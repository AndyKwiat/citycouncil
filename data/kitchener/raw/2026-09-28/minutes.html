PRESENTATIONS

 

5.1
2026 Volunteer Long Service Awards

 

Mayor B. Vrbanovic and Avalon Sands, Volunteer Engagement Associate, presented the Affiliated Group Long Service Volunteer Awards.

Mayor B. Vrbanovic presented the Long Service Volunteer Awards for neighbourhood associations and minor sports and on behalf of City Council, thanked them for their valued programs, events and services for Kitchener residents enjoy. Mayor B. Vrbanovic noted this year, we are recognizing four affiliated neighbourhood associations and seven affiliated minor sports groups. Together, these organizations represent an extraordinary 435 years of service to our community. 

Mayor Vrbanovic recognized the following neighbourhood associations: Eastwood Neighbourhood Association in Ward 10 for 5 years of community programming; Auditorium Neighbourhood Association in Ward 10 for 35 years of neighbourhood programming; Mill-Courtland Neighbourhood Association in Ward 9 for 40 years of neighbourhood programming; and, Doon Pioneer Park Community Association in Ward 4 for 45 years of community programming.

Mayor Vrbanovic then recognized the long service Minor Sport Groups, including: Waterloo Region Minor Football Association celebrating 30 years of service; Kitchener-Waterloo Water Polo Club celebrating 50 years of service; and, Kitchener Minor Girls Softball Association celebrating 60 years of service. Mayor Vrbanovic then noted there were several groups not able to be in attendance this evening that were also celebrating milestones, including: Top Spin Table Tennis Club of Kitchener-Waterloo for 25 years of service; Tsuruoka Kai Karate Do for 30 years of community sports; Region of Waterloo Swim Club (ROW) for a milestone 50 years of programming; and, Conestoga Sailing Club who is celebrating 65 years of service.

Councillor J. Deneault left and returned to the meeting at this time.

6.2
Consideration of Objection to Notice of Intention to Designate 265 Weber Street East, DSD-2026-348, listed as item 11.1.b

 

Council considered, Consideration of Objection to Notice of Intention to Designate 265 Weber Street East, DSD-2026-348, listed as item 11.1.b on the agenda this date. Garett Stevenson, Director, Development and Housing Approvals, was in attendance to respond to questions from Council.

S. Secrett and J. Olinski, Eastview Co-operative Inc. were in attendance to represent their objection to the property municipally addressed as 265 Weber Street East being designated under Part IV of the Ontario Heritage Act. Concerns were expressed regarding the attributes analysis and lack of historical integrity over a number of years and construction projects. S. Secrett then responded to questions from Council regarding the small number of volunteers in the co-operative and their majority of the co-operative board is in opposition to the designation.

Mayor B. Vrbanovic left the meeting at this time and Councillor S. Davey assumed the role of the Chair. 

Mayor B. Vrbanovic returned to the meeting at this time and re-assumed the Chair. 

Garett Stevenson, Director, Development and Housing Approvals then responded to questions of Council. 

Councillor S. Stretch brought forward a motion that was seconded by Councillor J. Deneault, as follows:

"That with respect to the Notice of Objection dated July 30, 2026, concerning the Notice of Intention to Designate that was approved at the June 29, 2026, Council meeting, for the property municipally addressed as 265 Weber Street East under Part IV of the Ontario Heritage Act, staff be directed to take no further action on the proposed designation and to allow the applicable 120-day statutory period to expire; and further,

That pursuant to Section 29 of the Ontario Heritage Act, the Clerk be directed to publish a Notice of Withdrawal of Intention to Designate the property municipally addressed as 265 Weber Street East."

The following motion was then voted on, by a recorded vote.

  • Moved byCouncillor S. Stretch
    Seconded byCouncillor J. Deneault

    "That with respect to the Notice of Objection dated July 30, 2026, concerning the Notice of Intention to Designate that was approved at the June 29, 2026, Council meeting, for the property municipally addressed as 265 Weber Street East under Part IV of the Ontario Heritage Act, staff be directed to take no further action on the proposed designation and to allow the applicable 120-day statutory period to expire; and further,

    That pursuant to Section 29 of the Ontario Heritage Act, the Clerk be directed to publish a Notice of Withdrawal of Intention to Designate the property municipally addressed as 265 Weber Street East."

    In Favour (11)Mayor B. Vrbanovic, Councillor S. Davey, Councillor D. Schnider, Councillor J. Deneault, Councillor C. Michaud, Councillor A. Owodunni, Councillor P. Singh, Councillor B. Ioannidis, Councillor M. Johnston, Councillor D. Chapman, and Councillor S. Stretch
    Carried, unanimously on a recorded vote (11 to 0)

6.3
Heritage Permit Application HPA-2026-IV-021 - 16-20 Queen Street North, Removal of a Portion of the Side Return and Altering Existing Window Opening into a Door on Floor 2 of Front Façade, DSD-2026-316, listed as item 7.1.a

 
1.Presentation - Hal Jaeger.pdf

Councillor P. Singh left and returned to the meeting at this time.

Mayor B. Vrbanovic left the meeting at this time and Councillor S. Davey assumed the role of the Chair. 

Council considered Heritage Permit Application (HPA) HPA-2026-IV-021 - 16-20 Queen Street North, Removal of a Portion of the Side Return and Altering Existing Window Opening into a Door on Floor 2 of Front Façade, DSD-2026-316, listed as item 7.1.a. Council was also in receipt of a written submission from MHBC Planning, Sue Savor and Leslie Stallard.

The Mayor noted that Heritage Kitchener considered this matter on September 1, 2026 and the staff recommendation was voted on and was LOST. The staff recommendation as outlined in the addendum report has been included on the Council agenda in the affirmative, it if it is Council’s will to refuse the Heritage Permit Application, members of Council should vote in opposition of the recommendation.

Dan Currie, MHBC Planning, addressed Council on behalf of the property owner in support of the Heritage Permit application and the staff recommendation as outlined in DSD-2026-348 and requested a deferral on the item until the fall as they undertake a review of the community benefit charges for uses on the ground floor. 

Marinus de Groot and Hal Jaeger addressed Council in support of the proposed deferral recommended by the Applicant.

Yvonne Ip delegated in support of the proposed deferral and recommended the matter be deferred until consideration can occur as part of the materially changed project.

A motion was brought forward by Councillor D. Chapman, which was seconded by Councillor S. Stretch to defer the recommendation to January 2027, to allow the property owner further opportunity to discuss the alteration plans with staff and the neighbourhood.

  • Moved byCouncillor D. Chapman
    Seconded byCouncillor S. Stretch

    "That the following matter be deferred to a Council meeting in January 2027, to allow further discussions related to the alterations between the applicant, City Staff and the neighbourhood:

    "That pursuant to Section 33 of the Ontario Heritage Act, Heritage Permit Application HPA-2026-IV-021 be approved, as outlined in Development Services Department report DSD-2026-316, to permit the removal of a portion of the side (south) return and the alteration of an existing window opening into a door on the 2nd floor of the front (east) façade on the property municipally addressed as 16-20 Queen Street North, subject to the following conditions:
    1. That the Heritage Impact Assessment Addendum, dated July 23, 2026 and prepared by MHBC, be approved by the Director of Development and Housing Approvals prior to the issuance of a heritage permit;
    2. That an updated Conservation Plan be submitted and approved by the Director of Development and Housing Approvals prior to the issuance of a Heritage Permit; and
    3. That the final Building Permit drawings be reviewed and heritage clearance provided by Heritage Planning staff prior to the issuance of a Building Permit."

    Carried
