6.1
Zoning By-law Amendment Application ZBA21/019/S/BB, Draft Plan of Vacant Land Condominium 30CDM-24215, 26 Stanley Avenue and 31 Schweitzer Street, DSD-2026-283, listed as item 7.3.d

 

Council considered Zoning By-law Amendment Application ZBA21/019/S/BB, Draft Plan of Vacant Land Condominium 30CDM-24215, 26 Stanley Avenue and 31 Schweitzer Street, DSD-2026-283, listed as item 7.3.d. It was noted since the Planning and Strategic Initiatives Committee meeting, staff have provided addendum staff report on this matter, Development Services Department report DSD-2026-332, which was included in the agenda this date. In addition, Council was in receipt of a written submission from Stuart McKee related to the subject application.

Kristen Barisdale, GSP Group, on behalf of the applicant, addressed Council in support of the subject application and the revised staff recommendation.

Mary Lou Schaefer, delegated in opposition to the revised recommendation citing gaps in the report to address concerns from the August 10, 2026 PSIC meeting, regarding mitigating additional strain on aging watermain and sewer, traffic congestion on neighboring streets, traffic flow counts in the Region of Waterloo's mobility plan and traffic impact study (TIS), emergency response access and pathway through the cemetery being retained in the draft plan. M. Schaefer requested Council defer the recommendation to proactively address the concerns raised. 

James Orsan, delegated in opposition to the revised recommendation noting traffic concerns and emergency response abilities to access the site and requested that Council defer the recommendation.

E. Wittmann, Senior Planner, noted that the Applicant has revised the plan to remove the walkway through the cemetery, addressed questions regarding fencing around the subject property, components of the Region of Waterloo Traffic Impact Study (TIS) and mobility plan and abilities of the Applicant to appeal the city's non-decision to the Ontario Land Tribunal (OLT). 

In response to questions from Council, Garett Stevenson, Director, Development and Housing Approvals explained the use of a Holding provision for zoning by-law amendments, and noted the TIS' address the additional traffic expected from the existing application and modest projected growth counts.

Councillor S. Davey then brought forward a motion, which was seconded by Councillor S Stretch to approve the recommendation as outlined in addendum staff report DSD-2026-283, including an amendment to "direct staff to amend the site specific zoning to include a site specific provision, to be applied to the a new AREA 3, shown roughly as the area of lots 9-32 as shown on the draft plan of condominium, show in the form shown on the revised "Draft Plan of Condominium" attached to report DSD-2026-332 as Attachment B1l which states that notwithstanding Section 4.14.2 and 4.12.4, a balcony and/or a covered or uncovered porch that exceeds 0.6 metres in height above the ground shall not be permitted to project within a rear yard, a notwithstanding Section 7, Table 7.3, the maximum building height for semi-detached dwellings shall be 9 metres and 2 storeys", in addition to staff direction to provide "formal correspondence from Council to the Region of Waterloo sharing concerns about traffic congestion and access to the Bridgeport Neighbourhood, requesting a report back on future traffic considerations."

  • Moved byCouncillor S. Davey
    Seconded byCouncillor S. Stretch

    "That Zoning By-law Amendment Application ZBA/21/019/S/BB for 26 Stanley Avenue and 31 Schweitzer Street for Newo Holdings Ltd BE APPROVED, and as amended this date, to amend Zoning By-law 85-1 and Zoning By-law 2019-051 in form shown in the ‘Proposed By-law’, and ‘Map No.1’ attached to Report DSD-2026-332 as Attachments ‘A1’ and ‘A2’; and,

    That Vacant Land Plan of Condominium Application 30CDM-24215 for a portion of 26 Stanley Avenue and 31 Schweitzer Street for Newo Holdings Ltd BE APPROVED shown in the form shown on the revised ‘Draft Plan of Condominium’ and revised ‘Draft Plan of Condominium Conditions’ attached to Report DSD-2026-332 as Attachments ‘B1’ and ‘B2’; and,

    That pursuant to Section 34(17) of the Planning Act, R.S.O. 1990, c. P. 13, as amended, further notice is not required to be given in respect to Zoning By-law Amendment Application ZBA21/019/S/BB; and further,

    That staff be directed to send formal correspondence to behalf of Kitchener City Council, to the Region of Waterloo, sharing concerns about traffic congestion and access to the Bridgeport Neighbourhood Community."

    Carried, as amended
