9.2
Notice of Motion - P. Singh - Pothole Technology Exploration

 

Councillor P. Singh has given notice to introduce the following motion for consideration this date:

"WHEREAS the past two winter seasons presented unusually challenging conditions for roadway maintenance, resulting in increased pothole formation due to prolonged deep freezes, significant temperature fluctuations and significant spring rainfall; and,

WHEREAS Council has expressed ongoing interest in improving roadway condition monitoring and ensuring timely, efficient repair of potholes; and,

WHEREAS staff have been actively working to strengthen data collection methodologies to better track road conditions and inform operational decision-making;

THEREFORE BE IT RESOLVED THAT staff be directed to explore opportunities to enhance the City’s pothole monitoring and repair program, including:

a. Improvements to data collection, tracking, and reporting tools using AI technology to provide more accurate and timely information on roadway conditions;

b. Options to optimize resource allocation and response times for pothole repair, taking into consideration service demand and operational constraints; and,

c. Using data to inform long-term approach to roadway maintenance and asset management, including service level expectations and funding strategies; and,

THEREFORE BE IT FURTHER RESOLVED that staff report back with findings and recommendations through a future budget discussion."
